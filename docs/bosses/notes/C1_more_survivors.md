# C1 — More survivors-likes: Spirit Hunters, Army of Ruin, Megabonk, Magicraft, Nova Drift, Ravenswatch

Research strand C1 for the Survivor Unchained boss-fight design document. Every factual claim carries an inline URL. "(secondary)" marks claims seen only in a search-result summary or a third-party guide that could not be checked against a primary source. Wikis that blocked automated access were read through the MediaWiki API; developer patch notes come from the Steam news API (`steamstore-a.akamaihd.net` links are the canonical Steam announcement URLs). Already covered elsewhere and not repeated: Gungeon, Nuclear Throne, Isaac, Hyper Light Drifter, Cuphead, Touhou, Returnal, Furi.

## Summary of lessons

1. **Night should change behaviour, not only numbers (Ravenswatch).** By day, undead hogs explode and ghosts are invisible; by night, gnolls sleep, treants release spiders and tentacle nightmares become mobile laser-casters. Heroes change too: Scarlet becomes a werewolf, and the Pied Piper's notes switch from single-target to piercing. The wiki even publishes a "best by day / best by night" list. For our ember arenas, each enemy family should get one night verb.
2. **Flip the boss to night at 50% HP (Ravenswatch).** All three Master Nightmares cycle the arena to night at half health and enrage (faster attacks, same patterns, red-glowing bar). The phase change is felt through the player's own kit.
3. **Do not let night take away a hero's core damage (Ravenswatch).** The Snow Queen's night form stripped her damage and was reworked as "not satisfying enough, especially at night". Night forms should feel like a gift with a trade-off.
4. **A fixed clock creates tension, but tune the power curve, not the clock (Ravenswatch, Spirit Hunters).** Ravenswatch refused to remove its 18-minute timer ("the tension, the pressure of making choices"), but when a shorter clock dropped the win rate from 23% to 8%, the fix was +10–25% XP. Pair the clock with an **early-summon reward** (Hourglass of Dreams) and an **overtime penalty** (up to +100% boss HP and damage over 3 minutes) instead of extending it.
5. **If the horde ramps during the boss, anything that soaks projectiles kills players (Army of Ruin).** Telemetry showed the Forge boss's saw attack blocked shots, dragged the fight out, and let the boss-wave minion ramp peak deaths at minute 15. The fix: lower boss HP and a slower minion ramp. Equal HP does not mean equal fights.
6. **Late, a bigger horde helps an evolved build (Army of Ruin).** On a long stage the boss became "incredibly easy … adding new enemies may even work in favor of the player". Late threat must come from mechanics, not head-count.
7. **Gate boss damage through adds, with visible armour and a stun payoff (Ravenswatch, Nova Drift).** Ravenswatch bosses take −50% to −75% damage while their adds live, then are stunned when the adds die. Nova Drift's Dweller *loses HP whenever a minion dies* — the best way to make AoE horde-clearing count during a boss.
8. **For absurd builds, prefer a fight-length floor to a damage cap (Nova Drift).** Star Eater takes increasing damage from 35 s into the fight (halved in phase 2), so weak builds are carried to the end. The opposite tools — Megabonk's weapon strip, Magicraft's 25-second invulnerability shields — work, but need a release valve: Megabonk now returns a weapon each phase, and Magicraft's shield drops on its own.
9. **A final boss is the build-viability bar; keep endless separate (Nova Drift, Megabonk).** Nova Drift's developer added a final boss so builds would be judged by "can it beat the boss" rather than by wave height. Megabonk's endless Final Swarm produced "5 hours … at 1.5fps" world records, fixed with ghosts that speed up after 20 minutes and a crowd-control immunity cap that shrinks to zero.
10. **Arena walls should hurt, not kill, and knockback near walls needs care (Spirit Hunters).** The shrinking one-hit spike cage became a fixed cage of "Boss Hurty Spikes" (with boss HP raised to match). The bugs that drew complaints were enemies and abilities that knocked the player into the spikes.
11. **Make bosses findable and their state legible (all).** Off-screen arrows to bosses and minibosses (Spirit Hunters, Army of Ruin), boss icons on a timeline, names and enrage state on the HP bar (Ravenswatch), shadows under invisible bosses (Magicraft), auras under elites, and a projectile-opacity slider (Megabonk) — each added after release in response to complaints.

## Ravenswatch (Passtech Games / Nacon, EA April 2023, 1.0 September 2024)

Not a survivors-like strictly speaking — it is a co-op top-down ARPG roguelite with hand-played abilities — but it is the closest commercial analogue to Survivor Unchained's day/night split, and its chapter structure (a timed open map, then a forced boss) is the same shape as a 30-minute ember arena.

**Why the timer exists, in the developers' words.** When players asked for it to go, Passtech refused: "The game is built around this timer, and although a modifier allows you to remove it entirely, this takes away the essence of the game: the tension, the pressure of making choices, and the storytelling" ([Hourglass highlights](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1809869180066240)). In Dev Diary #04 they added: "Having a pocket-open world gives the impression that everything should be explored, whereas in a roguelike you still must make choices. Exploring everything won't make you stronger, choosing to focus your time on Activities and participate in combat will" ([Dev Diary #04](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5211283418278104506)).

**What went wrong when they shortened it.** Cutting chapters from 4 days to 3 produced a backlash: "in normal mode, the success rate for completing Dark Hills before the update was 23%, and the success rate for completing both Chapters is now 8%"; "Almost all the criticism we received was about the shorter timer" ([Dev Diary #04](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5211283418278104506)). The fix was not more time but more XP: +25% XP on Normal/Nightmare 1, +20% on N2–3, +10% on N4–5, unchanged from N6 up, because "XP acquired by heroes is a pillar of the game's development … to be sufficiently prepared for the final confrontation" ([0.14.02 hotfix](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5480373588782873521)). Lesson: the boss-timer is perceived as the difficulty knob even when the boss itself has not changed; tune the pre-boss power curve rather than the clock.

**Early-summon data.** After 1.3, players who triggered the Dark Hills boss after only ~5 minutes "met your doom with 77% of fails", whereas "triggering the Boss just before the original timer ends (up until 3 minutes before) results in an 83% success rate" ([Community Spotlight Oct 2025](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1813674389068877)). Speedrunners immediately exploited it (4 min 12 s solo record) ([same](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1813674389068877)). The early-summon + overtime pair is a clean, symmetric risk dial: fight early for loot, fight late for power but feed the boss.

Night does not simply "make everything harder"; it swaps behaviours per enemy family and per hero, so the *same map* asks different questions at different times.

### The Master Nightmares (chapter bosses)

All three share a skeleton ([wiki: Tentacle](https://ravenswatch.fandom.com/wiki/The_Tentacle_Master_Nightmare), [Faceless](https://ravenswatch.fandom.com/wiki/The_Faceless_Master_Nightmare), [Claws](https://ravenswatch.fandom.com/wiki/The_Claws_Master_Nightmare)):

- **Armour gate via adds.** The boss is armoured while its adds live; kill all adds and it is "unarmored and stunned for a few seconds". Faceless: after summoning eyes it takes "Double Armor (-75% damage taken) instead of its regular Basic Armor (-50% damage taken)" while any eye lives, and "will not re-summon Eyes while some are still alive" — added because re-summoning before players could clear them "could be frustrating" ([0.15.01 patch notes](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/6560117310264581723)).
- **"At 50% health, the time of day cycles to night."** The boss arena flips to night mid-fight, so night-form heroes (Scarlet) always get their night kit for the second half (wiki pages above).
- **Enrage at half health**: "all attacks will be faster, but the patterns are the same", shown by the health bar glowing red (wiki pages above). The red bar was itself a later readability fix: "The enraged effect on Master Nightmare is now displayed on their Health Bar" ([0.17.00 patch notes](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5762994032405566311)). Enraged variants also widen radii and speed up projectiles ([0.17.03](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5842939267309830904)).
- **Invulnerability rules are named keywords**: most attacks can be passed with INTANGIBLE (dash), but a few big ones — Tentacle's Divide (154+ damage), Claws' Smash and Dance 2 — need INVINCIBLE or positioning (wiki pages).
- **Healing orbs drop from killed adds**, but "Masters Nightmares now stop dropping healing orbs after some time" — a soft enrage ([0.14.01](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5159491482909201220)).
- **Random pattern sets** since 1.1: Tentacle +3 patterns, Faceless +4, Claws +5, "selected at random", to "create a surprise effect: each time you meet them, you will get a different combination of attacks" ([Nightmares Unleashed highlights](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1786573930652327)).

**Scaling to the player's build.** Difficulty tiers make Master Nightmares "stronger" (Twilight) and "even stronger" (Darkness) ([wiki: Difficulty Scaling](https://ravenswatch.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulty_Scaling)); per extra co-op player, elites get +60% HP and +60–90% stagger resistance ([same](https://ravenswatch.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulty_Scaling)). Elite and boss stagger resistance also scales by chapter (+20% Ch.2, +50% Ch.3) because "Players' ability to inflict stagger on enemies can increase significantly from one chapter to the next" ([0.17.03](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5842939267309830904)). The devs admitted the Ch.2/3 bosses were "oversized in terms of resistance. This is harder to notice, as there are currently too many ways to 'break' the game. But for players who don't make builds optimally, these bosses are too powerful" ([Dev Diary #09](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5742731639334152485)) — the classic broken-build vs average-build tension. Optional pre-boss work also weakens the boss: clearing the one Nightmare Tumor per chapter gives XP and "-30% Master Nightmare HP" ([wiki: Points of Interest](https://ravenswatch.fandom.com/wiki/Points_of_Interest)).

**Mini-bosses as side quests with a stake.** Arthur attacks the Golden Tree with a laser that removes half its HP bar while summoning skeletons that walk to the tree; save it for a legendary item ([wiki: Arthur](https://ravenswatch.fandom.com/wiki/Arthur,_the_Fallen_King)). The Cyclops eats captured NPCs and then Sinbad on a ~3-minute clock, and heals 25% if he grabs a player ([wiki: Steropes](https://ravenswatch.fandom.com/wiki/Steropes,_the_Cyclop)). If the Master Nightmare clock runs out mid-fight, the heroes are teleported to the boss ([wiki: Arthur](https://ravenswatch.fandom.com/wiki/Arthur,_the_Fallen_King)). Most-lethal mini-boss after 1.3: Simorgh, 119,000 hero deaths ([Spotlight Oct 2025](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1813674389068877)).

**Player opinion.** Aggregated Steam-review sentiment rates the timer the largest complaint: "The timer mechanic creates unnecessary pressure and limits player freedom" (weight 0.49), and multiplayer scaling: "Enemies become excessively tanky and deal high damage" (0.75); bosses themselves are a minor positive: "The boss fights are challenging and rewarding" ([Vaporlens summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/2071280/ravenswatch.md), secondary aggregation). On Steam one player calls it "a very difficult, frustrating, and cheap game", while another says "I probably win about 80% of nightmare 9 games…it's far from impossible" ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2071280/discussions/0/5971271658110154464/)). A review noted the Master Nightmare "can deal half of your HP in a single hit" (secondary, [GameGrin review](https://www.gamegrin.com/reviews/ravenswatch-review/)).

**Takeaways for Survivor Unchained.** (1) Make night change *what enemies do*, not just their numbers — exploding by day, invisible by day, sleeping at night — so players plan routes around time. (2) Flip the boss arena to night at 50% HP: a phase change players feel through their own kit. (3) Gate damage behind adds with a visible armour icon and a stun payoff for clearing them. (4) Pair an early-summon reward with an overtime penalty rather than extending the clock. (5) Never let a night form remove a hero's core damage.

## Megabonk (vedinad, solo developer; 1.0 September 2025)

3D survivors-like with jumping and sliding over procedurally generated hills. Wiki coverage is thin (the [fandom wiki](https://megabonk.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulty) is a stub; megabonk.wiki.gg refused automated access), so concrete boss numbers below come mostly from guide sites and are flagged.

### Run structure: find the gate before the clock runs out

- Each stage gives "ten minutes to find that gate and spawn the boss before waves of angry ghosts come to finish you off" ([PC Gamer review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/roguelike/megabonk-review/)). The boss is **not** spawned by the clock — the player must find a portal (white gusts of wind lead to it) and press to spawn it (secondary, [Gamerblurb](https://gamerblurb.com/articles/megabonk-how-to-find-the-boss-portal)). The portal "will not only spawn the stage boss, but will also allow you to go to next stage directly" ([Netto's Game Room review](https://www.nettosgameroom.com/2025/10/megabonk-review.html?m=1)).
- **Tiers** set the number of stages: "Tier 1 = 1 stage. Tier 2 = 2 stages. Tier 3 = 3 stages" ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3405340/discussions/0/600791249529633914/)). Players were soft-locked by the next-stage portal not opening on Tier 1 and called it "pretty bad game design … softlocked by the game, then it mocks you for dying, THEN it shows that you actually did win" (same thread). Lesson: tell players clearly when the win has happened.
- **Final Swarm**: when the timer reaches zero, or if you stay after the final boss, "an endless wave of enemies that overtime gain more power and speed" ([Netto's](https://www.nettosgameroom.com/2025/10/megabonk-review.html?m=1)). Surviving it gates unlocks: Ghost 60 s, Bob (Dead) 2 min, Soul Harvester 6 min (secondary, [RogueRanker](https://rogueranker.com/soul-harvester-megabonk/)).

The HP figures conflict between guides and could not be checked against a primary source. Treat them as orders of magnitude only — roughly ×40 per tier.

**The final boss's weapon strip, and how the developer softened it.** The final boss takes away the player's weapons. Patch 1.0.4 changed it: "For every phase, the boss gives back one of your weapons. You only need to do the first phase with your starter weapon now", "Pylon zones are bigger, and heal the boss less", "Orbs deal less damage, and blue orbs freeze you for only 1s, instead of the previous 3s", "Orbs have slightly longer cooldown", "Weapon range has been increased during the fight", and "You can't knock bosses out of the map anymore lol" ([Patch #2 notes](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1811772772356053)). Guides written against the launch version say "once they are removed, they are gone for the rest of the battle" ([Gamerblurb](https://gamerblurb.com/articles/megabonk-final-boss-guide-how-to-beat-the-final-boss)). Guides say the pylon phases make him heal quickly, back to full if the beacons are not destroyed in time (secondary, same and [dtgre](https://www.dtgre.com/2025/10/megabonk-bark-vader-final-boss-strategy-guide-.html)). The design aim is a **build-normalising cap**: by removing everything except the starter weapon, a survivors game whose builds explode in power gets a final fight it can tune. Players were angry because it invalidates the build they spent the run on; the developer kept the idea but handed weapons back phase by phase.

**Boss Curse shrines (opt-in difficulty).** Each one activated spawns "one additional boss during the boss fight", with slightly more HP, and "you receive an extra chest for each curse you've activated"; they stack (secondary, [Gamerblurb](https://gamerblurb.com/articles/megabonk-boss-curse-guide-boss-curse-shrines)). Challenge shrines spawn a small horde for loot ([Deltia's](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=355916), secondary). Difficulty is a stat raised by Skullegs, difficulty shrines and the Cursed Tome, increasing enemy count, HP and damage, and players raise it on purpose "to gain more kills" ([fandom: Difficulty](https://megabonk.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulty)).

### Endless play and the 5-hour problem

Megabonk is the clearest example of what endless post-boss play does to a leaderboard. Patch 1.0.12: "The current meta required you to wear sunglasses and watch a slideshow / lightshow at 1.5fps for 5 hours to get a WR. This was never intended, and I don't think anyone actually enjoyed that … The final swarm is harder to survive, and your executes and black holes won't really save you anymore… probably. New ghosts appear in the Final Swarm" ([v1.0.12 notes](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1813041031176676)). The same patch capped Joe's Dagger growth at "200 per minute" because "you could get 20 000% damage in 10 seconds". The Spooky Update added a creeping cap on crowd control: "Final swarm ghosts will start increasing their speed after 20 minutes have passed", and while an enemy normally becomes immune after being stunned or frozen 15 times, "starting at 20 minutes, the CC cap will slowly decrease until they can no longer be stunned or frozen" ([Spooky Update notes](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1818752592131516)). Silver multiplier in the Final Swarm was halved from 16× to 8× (same).

**Readability under the player's own effects.** v1.0.17 added an opacity slider for "Game Particles and effects" because "When you do have a fuckton of projectiles with ridiculous size covering the entire screen, you might wanna make them fully transparent"; headline: "No more sunglasses needed for late-game" ([v1.0.17 notes](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1813674389045096)). Money pickups move faster during the Final Swarm "so that you can still pick them up while running" (same).

**Player and critic opinion.** PC Gamer's reviewer liked it overall but singled out "a late-game boss with a surprise insta-kill. What skill was that testing, clairvoyance?" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/roguelike/megabonk-review/)). One player in a GamingOnLinux comment thread suggested replacing the "bullet sponge" final boss with a huge final wave (secondary, [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/12/the-popular-megabonk-gets-a-mega-update-with-new-content/page=1/)). The early-kill bonus is liked: "If you do take on the final boss far too early and win, the game will reward you" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/roguelike/megabonk-review/)).

**Takeaways.** (1) A boss you must *find and start* gives the clock meaning without forcing the fight. (2) Weapon-stripping normalises a fight against absurd builds, but players need something back — return power phase by phase. (3) Endless mode needs a hard ceiling (ramping speed, eroding CC immunity) or the leaderboard turns into 5-hour slideshows. (4) Ship a projectile-opacity slider.

## Nova Drift (Chimeric / Pixeljam; Early Access 2019, 1.0 August 2024)

A wave-based twin-stick in an asteroids-style arena that wraps at the screen edges, with roughly 200 mutually compatible upgrades. It is the genre's clearest case study of a **fixed boss cadence** and of a game that only got a final boss after five years of endless-only play.

### Boss cadence

| Wave | Standard boss | Nemesis alternate | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | Spitfire | Ceratotitan | [wiki: Bosses](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Bosses) |
| 40 | Station Omega | Dreadnought | same |
| 60 | Scion | Dweller | same |
| 80 | Warbringer | Crucible | same |
| 100 | Seraph | Glaucus | same |
| 120 | **Star Eater** (final boss); reappears at 240 and every 240 waves in Endless | — | [wiki: Star Eater](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Eater) |
| 200+, every 100th | "Cursed Waves" pit the player "against two powerful enemies at once" (Scion, Seraph eligible) | — | [wiki: Seraph](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Seraph) |

Bosses are **recycled as elites**: after first defeat, Spitfire "will appear at later waves together with other enemies, effectively serving as an elite enemy in terms of difficulty" ([wiki: Spitfire](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Spitfire)); Scion's and Seraph's "attacks will become much faster after their first encounter, scaling with the wave at which [they spawn]" ([wiki: Scion](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Scion)). Alternates (Nemesis challenge mode) are "more difficult (but also more rewarding)" and were added because "alternate boss encounters will go a long way to ensuring that every run feels unique" ([2022 Upcoming Content](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4474904295745172674); [Enemies 2.0 Part 1 notes](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4224939931846888948)). Boss Rush faces "every boss in the game in succession, gaining several levels between each" ([1.0 release post](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5963414363404952954)).

### Mechanics worth stealing

- **Weak points that change the fight as they break.** Dreadnought's body resists damage but "loses some of its damage resistance for each gun destroyed", and attacks that need a destroyed gun "fail" ([wiki: Dreadnought](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Dreadnought)). Warbringer's core takes double damage from direct hits, but each destroyed frontal section adds more bullets to a radial spread — breaking armour makes it angrier ([wiki: Warbringer](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Warbringer)).
- **Boss HP tied to the horde.** Dweller "loses health when a minion is destroyed … They take more damage the stronger the minion was", and alternates between a fighting phase and a Galaga-style formation phase ([wiki: Dweller](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Dweller)). For a survivors game whose damage is mostly AoE, this is the cleanest way to make horde-clearing *count* during a boss.
- **Healer adds as an enrage.** With the Annihilation modifier, Station Omega's escort heals it, and "it can become difficult or impossible to deal damage faster than the healing: the rate of healing is proportional to Station Omega's large amount of maximum hull" ([wiki: Station Omega](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Station_Omega)).
- **Arena rule change.** Crucible "disables screen wrapping" — the player bounces off the edges — and draws burning lines that start dark (light damage) before brightening (heavy damage), a two-stage telegraph ([wiki: Crucible](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Crucible)).
- **Telegraph tuning in patch notes.** The Crucible's hoop got "a longer wind-up", targeting that "ramps up … over a second instead of immediately having good targeting", and "a more clear indicator of the trajectory" ([0.33.33 notes](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/V0.33.33)).

### Star Eater, the final boss (wave 120)

- **Foreshadowing**: "The boss appears in the background two waves before it spawns. A large red star will appear alongside three long segments of its body in the background" ([wiki: Star Eater](https://nova-drift.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Eater)).
- **Fixed six-attack cycle**: Burst (3 volleys of 20+ fireballs, 4 below 50%) with a ring of asteroids closing in; Flamethrower or Ultra Energy Beam (50/50); Lunge ×3 (×4 below 50%); Vortex Barrage (2 streams above 50%, 4 below, and a longer-lived vortex); Splitter Orbs; Coil Lightning — five passes across the screen whose body forms shifting diagonal walls you must wrap around (same).
- **Anti-stall rule (a hidden enrage that favours the player)**: "To keep the fight from lasting too long, Star Eater has a hidden vulnerability mechanic. 35 seconds after the fight starts, Star Eater will take increasing amounts of damage from the player until the fight's conclusion. Upon reaching phase 2, this amount is reduced by half" (same). This is the inverse of a classic enrage. Weak builds are not walled; they are carried to the end.
- **Victory**: minions die instantly, "an elaborate death animation, culminating in a white flash that envelops the whole screen for a few seconds"; in Endless, "a large amount of XP Orbs and various power-ups are dropped" (same). The ending sequence plays only if Endless is off, and Endless itself unlocks on the first Star Eater kill ([1.0 release post](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5963414363404952954)).

### Why add an ending at all — the developer's reasoning

"Games with an endless mode are massively harder to balance than games with endings. For instance, in Hades, builds are considered viable if they can defeat the final boss. In Nova Drift, a build tends to be judged by how far it can push in waves. The higher the wave, the fewer builds are considered viable for it. This isn't great for build variety. Thankfully, this changed with 1.0" ([1.0 release post](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5963414363404952954)). Earlier, the developer had committed to "removing the need for a 'spammy' late game. Enemies will trend toward tactical, dangerous, and diverse instead of numerous … encounters will be deadly for reasons which are better than 'there were too many mines and bullets on the screen'", and to replacing edge-spawns "without warning" with fades, warp portals or "a HUD warning" ([State of the Game, April 2020](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/2978509755363675037)). Survivor Unchained's "boss at 30:00 then endless" maps exactly onto this: the 30:00 boss should be the viability bar, and endless should be a separate scoreboard.

**Player opinion.** Some feel the final boss narrowed builds — "fun builds are gone because of the new final boss" — while others reply "Warpbreaker with void slice is still fun and can deal with him easily enough" and "A really strong Sword build will eat the final boss" ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/858210/discussions/0/4518885302421106196)). Version 1.2 later made "Difficulty adjustments for Scion, Dweller, Seraph, Spitfire, Station Omega, and Star Eater, making them more in line with the power curve of other bosses, especially for Boss Rush mode" ([1.2 devlog](https://pixeljam.itch.io/nova-drift/devlog/960454/version-12-daily-challenge-mode-added)).

## Spirit Hunters: Infinite Horde (Ant Workshop; EA May 2022, 1.0 June 2023)

A 2D survivors-like with nine zones, a meta tree (the "Divinity Web") and, at 1.0, "200+ Unique monsters including 70 minibosses and 27 end bosses", ending with the Cavern boss Endlord ([1.0 release notes](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5896948309857553879)). It matters to us because it uses a **survivors-style clock that ends in a caged boss arena** — almost exactly Survivor Unchained's 30:00 boss — and it iterated publicly on that cage.

### Run structure

- "All Games have a 15 minute timer … At 15 minutes a Spike Cage Arena will pop up and you will face the Final Spirit of your hunt"; "Some Bosses will play dirty and push you into the spikes" (secondary guide, [GamePretty](https://gamepretty.com/spirit-hunters-infinite-horde-basic-guide-to-new-hunter/)). Speed modes from patch 1.2: "2x means standard 15 min runs become 7.5 minutes (clock ticks twice as fast)" ([Patch 1.2](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5288974951222015545)).
- **Two tiers of boss** from the start: "Sub bosses are larger normal monsters … drop lots of cool stuff", and "Main bosses are the big old beefy boi's you have to defeat to complete some zones … drop HUGE rewards" ([Development Update #2](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4259848535841854080)). End bosses are drawn at random per zone: "You wont know which ones you will be facing in each run" ([Development Update #4](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4348799082198647186)). Minibosses drop a purple spirit chest ([GamePretty](https://gamepretty.com/spirit-hunters-infinite-horde-basic-guide-to-new-hunter/), secondary).
- Boss icons sit on a **battle timeline** in the HUD ([Buffs patch](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4437747060845087108)), so the player can see a boss coming.

### The spike cage: from DPS check to plain arena

This is the most instructive change history in this strand.

1. **Launch design (pre-EA)**: "When its time for the final show down you will be fighting inside an ever shrinking arena of spikes! You are going to want to kill the boss as fast as you can because if you leave it too long you will fall to the spikes!" ([Development Update #3](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4353300763399246497)). A shrinking ring is an enrage: a hard DPS check.
2. **July 2022**: "Boss cage no longer shrinks so players will have much more time and space to play with. It still kills you, though so don't touch it!" ([Progress Report](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4474904295778579040)). Content Update 01: "Now it no longer shrinks. Now you will have as long as you want to fight the end bosses … we gave all end bosses a huge health boost to avoid them going on strike" ([Content Update 01](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4598757090450561753)).
3. **April 2023**: "BOSS DEATH SPIKES — Will no longer kill you in one hit. Now they're the Boss Hurty Spikes and just do a large amount of damage" ([Content Patch 8](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5133583642646969460)).

So the studio moved from a timed, lethal arena to an untimed one with damaging walls, and paid for it with much bigger boss HP. The cage kept its main job: it stops the player kiting away from the boss across an infinite field. Knockback into spikes was the main source of unfair deaths; Content Update 02 fixed exploding enemies that could "shoot the player across the screen … launched into the death spikes" ([Content Update 02](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4584121659780934658)), and Content Patch 07 blocked hero abilities after the boss dies because players dashed into the spikes during the victory moment ([Content Patch 07](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5059267908810220435)).

### Readability and fairness fixes

- **Off-screen miniboss arrows**: "As soon as a miniboss spawns, an arrow with an icon will point out the direction it is (if offscreen) and it will stay onscreen until you defeat it or the end boss spawns … If the boss dies offscreen, the indicator will be used to point at the chest it drops" ([Content Update 02](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4584121659780934658)).
- **Loot does not vanish**: souls, gold and hearts now "stay on the ground until picked up or the end boss spawns" ([Content Patch 07](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5059267908810220435)) — the boss spawn is the cut-off.
- **Immunity limited to bosses**: "Normal enemies are no longer immune to any skill … Brutal Scourge, Some minibosses and End bosses are still immune to Freeze, Shock/Stun and Poison" (same). Crowd-control immunity is the standard cap that stops a build trivialising a boss.
- **No spawns after death**: minibosses and bosses kept spawning after the player died, "creating a bit of a weird experience" ([Content Patch 8](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5133583642646969460)).
- **Photosensitivity**: an option "to turn off the Boss Flash that happens when you defeat a boss" ([Patch #2](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4469272893506977924)) — evidence the victory moment was a full-screen flash.

### Scaling to the build

Boss HP was retuned repeatedly against the player's rising power: "Hard mode bosses that you guys were chewing through in seconds got a lil beefier" ([Buffs patch](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4437747060845087108)); after a global damage buff, "a TON more HP to minibosses and end bosses" ([Content Update 01](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4598757090450561753)). Difficulty is layered per zone (normal, hard, brutal); brutal adds "the Scourge, a group of very dangerous enemies that can drop Shadow Souls", and is "faster and the enemies have much more health and do more damage" (Content patch 06, [news](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5623338145768613558)). Shuriken was nerfed from 4 s volleys to 1.6 s because it was "a super strong boss killer, but making it difficult to use through the majority of a run" ([Content Update 4](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4732747437198500377)) — a reminder that weapons tuned for bosses distort the drafting choice.

Meta-progression is gated on bosses: "kill gates" require killing specific monsters. The studio removed random minibosses from these gates — "now there is no more luck required to complete the Divinity Web" ([Content Update 03](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4606641560263073477)).

**Opinion.** Seasoned Gaming liked that "an end-of-level boss traps you in a cage of spears, presenting itself as an actual challenging yet beatable obstacle", unlike Vampire Survivors ([Seasoned Gaming](https://seasonedgaming.com/2023/07/21/review-spirit-hunters-infinite-horde-unending-spirit/)). Much of the early feedback was about grind rather than bosses (the [Content Update 01](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4598757090450561753) notes cut meta-tree costs by "30-90%").

## Army of Ruin (Milkstone Studios; EA October 2022, 1.0 June 2023)

A compact, mission-based survivors-like ("a quick and thrilling game experience" — [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1918040/Army_of_Ruin/)). Each mission ends in a boss — an "Army General" — fought *inside* the horde. Its developer blog is unusually open about telemetry, which makes it the most useful source here on **how the boss wave actually kills players**.

### Bosses and the boss wave

| Area | Boss | Pattern (developer description) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defiled Graveyard | The Gravekeeper | "a slow but relentless enemy that will follow you until you submit! Surrounded by hundreds of creatures, you better take him out quickly or face your demise!" | [The Defiled Graveyard](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/6402455182504661682) |
| Ancient Forest | Forest Spirit | "will keep her distance while summoning dangerous carnivore plants near you" | [The Ancient Forest](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4714731755412573407) |
| Forge City | Mechanical Scorpion | "will try to slice you to pieces with its circular saws" | [The Forge City](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4714731755430287013) |

**The enrage is the horde, not the boss.** Once the boss spawns, its minions get stronger over time; the game shows "A popup … when minions get stronger in the boss battle" ([EA Update #8](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4980448574127227096)). The telemetry post explains how this goes wrong. On Forge 1, "once people get to the boss, the number of loses increases considerably, and fights drag on for longer than in the other two stages, with loses getting to their highest value at minute 15, when minions become so strong that players can't survive against them". The cause was the saw attack "blocking projectiles and forcing players to dodge it, so they can't spend as much time attacking the boss" — even though "Forge 1 and Forest 1 bosses have the same health". The fix: "lowering the boss health a bit and lowering the rate at which minions get stronger once the boss spawns" ([EA Update #4](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4732747437217040527)). Players also said "enemies feel a bit too spongy later on", so the studio reduced the "spongyness" of boss minions — "they will still become stronger, just not as fast" (same).

This is a direct warning for a 30:00 boss in a horde. If the horde ramps while the boss lives, **any boss attack that blocks or soaks the player's projectiles silently lengthens the fight and turns the ramp into a death sentence**. Equal HP does not mean equal fights.

**The opposite failure: the boss too weak for late builds.** On Graveyard 3, "minutes 17 and higher, including the boss, become incredibly easy and almost everyone wins! This is the result of increased mission length paired with evolutions and skill upgrades becoming too powerful for the enemies to overcome, to the point that adding new enemies may even work in favor of the player" (same). Lesson: in a run long enough for evolutions, more enemies feed the player more XP and procs. Late boss threat has to come from mechanics (zones, telegraphed bursts, damage caps), not from count.

**Telemetry by minute.** The day-1 report plotted mission end times: "on Graveyard 1 most people finishes the mission at Minute 11 … most people is reaching the boss stage", with a spike of deaths "at minute 2" when ghosts first appear; "nobody being able to get past the 8 minute mark on Forest 2 yet" ([Day 1 report](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4706851091176395276)). The intended curve: early stages "rather easy to complete as long as you don't mess up too hard", difficulty "slowly increasing" in later stages ([EA Update #4](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4732747437217040527)). We recommend plotting deaths per minute for every ember arena in the same way.

### Optional and endless bosses

- **Special Bosses** spawn "in a far away point (2-3 minutes walk)"; each is "a fast and tough enemy that will drop a chest when killed … If you do it quick enough, a second one will spawn elsewhere, and so on … Once all bosses are defeated, a Big Chest will be dropped, with a guaranteed 3 item rewards" ([Nov/Dec status](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4979322040013540730)). Clearing all of them on Ruin 2 for a given stage and character unlocks hero variants (same); the last special boss's chest was raised from 3 to 5 upgrades ([EA Update #9](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4991708208074774618)). A chain of optional bosses you must *travel to* is a good use of the open map before the main boss.
- **Endless Stages**: "New stages with no final boss and no set limit, with enemies that grow stronger over time" ([EA Update #16](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5070528176576977494)).
- **Ruin Levels** replace a "Curse" skill as the difficulty dial ([EA Update #5](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4789043687474667154)).

### Readability and build caps

- An off-screen arrow points to the stage boss ([EA Update #3](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4732747437202499525)); minibosses got health bars ([EA Update #8](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4980448574127227096)); the boss's name appears on its health bar ([EA Update #19](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5133583642669587771)); elites got "an aura below them" ([EA Update #4](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4732747437217040527)); "an explosion effect when the boss is killed" was added late ([EA Update #12](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5035622743880314222)).
- A revision of "all weapon visual effects to improve visibility when combined with high fire rate + size effects"; and Cooldown was replaced with Rate of Fire "to avoid exponential improvement of stacked Cooldown upgrades that quickly got to the capped value, leading to performance and visual issues on top of being extremely powerful" ([EA Update #5](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4789043687474667154)).
- A hero skill was "just too strong, melting bosses in a matter of seconds" and was nerfed ([EA Update #23](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/6557848386959785711)).

## Magicraft (Wave Games; EA October 2023, 1.0 November 2024)

A room-based spell-crafting action roguelike rather than a pure survivors-like: five chapters, a mid-chapter boss and an end-chapter boss, a Demon Lord at the end ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2103140/Magicraft/)). It "sold over 600,000 copies" by mid-2025 ([Developer Log 11](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1803527891470148)). Its "wand-building" makes player power very swingy, so its boss rules are relevant to our drafted weapons. Individual boss HP and pattern numbers were not found in a primary source.

### Mechanics

- **Timed invulnerability shields, with a hint for breaking them.** "Chapter 4 bosses now use invincibility shield up to 2 times, and the invincibility shield will be lifted after 25 seconds if it is not broken" ([V0.80.31](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5686430301717676717)); a "Hint effect added to rune that disarms shields on chapter 4 bosses" (V0.80.27, [notes](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/7309977427452909250)). The 25-second limit keeps a weak build from being walled forever; the rune gives a strong build a way to skip the wait. Later, "Flight now grants immunity to certain boss abilities" ([V1.2.36](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1840310314353873)).
- **Hitless reward room.** Beat the area boss without taking a hit and a hidden room opens offering epic or legendary items paid for with max HP (about 35 / 55 / 99) (secondary, [KosGames](https://kosgames.com/magicraft-tips-and-tricks-for-new-players-47478/)). Players found the trigger baffling; the developer's answer: "there is always a need for some hidden elements in the game … But if most players feedback this problem, it means it's really a problem, so we plan to use the plot dialogue to briefly hint at it, and then no more hints after that" ([Developer Log 01](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5159491482911434873)). A skill reward on top of the boss kill, not a gate.
- **Two-bar bosses**: the patch notes mention "double blood bar bosses" ([V1.0.48f2](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1788311419011185)), and "Twin Bosses" ([V1.0.47](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1785774543653819)).
- **Stealth bosses get a ground shadow.** "Added a light shadow on the ground after stealth bosses become invisible" ([Further adjustments to Chapter 3](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5288974951217248699)) — a minimal readability cue for an invisible threat. Compare Ravenswatch's day-invisible White Ladies.
- **Big numbers break the UI.** "Fixed the issue where the boss blood bar would display decimal points or scientific notation", and Pandora's Box effects no longer "override the boss display screen" ([V1.1.11](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1789580505331492)). Build damage in the millions needs a planned HP-bar format.

### Boss rush as the answer to mastery

When players found the game too easy by Chapter 4, the studio made the last chapter a boss rush: "Considering that many Magicians have mastered the game at the fourth chapter, making the difficulty levels less challenging … the fifth chapter will be a boss rush chapter, featuring full of elite monsters and bosses" ([Developer Log 06](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5746109972552535025)). Bosses also got distinct HP-bar frames "so don't bosses look more powerful now?" ([Developer Log 08](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/6250522009516422527)). Polish came last: death animations and sound effects for "some elites and bosses" were added only at 1.0 ([V1.0.44](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/6148070194801757887)), and the final boss's second-stage attack interval was "slightly shortened" ([V1.0.46f5](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1785774543557188)).

**Player complaint**: "monster values rising off a cliff in Chapter Three" — the developer planned to "nerf this cliff-like increase" and then add difficulty tiers so first-timers "won't be overly frustrated" ([Developer Log 01](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/5159491482911434873)).
