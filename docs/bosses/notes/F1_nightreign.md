# F1 — Elden Ring Nightreign: boss fights at the end of a day/night run

Strand: Elden Ring Nightreign (FromSoftware / Bandai Namco, released 30 May 2025 on Steam, app 2622380). Research compiled 3 October 2026 for the Survivor Unchained boss-fight design document.

Source conventions: every factual claim carries a URL. "(secondary)" marks a claim seen only in a search-engine summary and not confirmed on the page itself. Numbers that could not be found are marked "not found". Where two wikis disagree, both figures are given. Wiki figures are fan-measured (datamined or tested), not official.

Abbreviations: "Fandom" = eldenring.fandom.com; "wiki.gg" = eldenring.wiki.gg (Nightreign namespace); "Fextralife" = eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com.

---

## 1. Why Nightreign is the closest analogue

Nightreign is a co-operative roguelite for three players. Each run, called an **expedition**, names its final boss before it starts. The run is a timed build-up through two in-game days, each closed by a shrinking ring of rain that herds the party into a **night boss** arena. Day 3 is a single fight against the **Nightlord**. Survivor Unchained runs a 30-minute ember arena with a fixed boss at 30:00. Nightreign has the same shape: a known boss at the end of a timer, and a build you assemble in the time before it. The main difference is that the Nightreign player picks the target in advance, and much of the game's boss design rests on that choice.

## 2. The three-day expedition structure

### 2.1 Overall shape
- An expedition targets one specific Nightlord and lasts three in-game days. Days 1 and 2 last 15 minutes each and end in a boss fight. Day 3 is given over entirely to the Nightlord fight. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Expedition
- Fandom's flowchart breaks it into six beats: Day One Noontide (drop in by Spectral Hawk, then the Night's Tide limits safe space) → Day One Night (confined to a small area, fight a wave of tough enemies, then a night boss) → Day Two Noontide (the tide fully recedes and farming resumes; random events) → Day Two Night (second night enemies and boss, then enter the Spirit Shelter) → Final Day Noontide (last preparations, enter the tree) → Final Day Night (Nightlord). Source: https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Expeditions
- Each player starts at Level 1 and can reach Level 15 by spending runes at Sites of Grace. The total cost to reach Level 15 is 513,116 runes. Gear rarity is gated by level: Uncommon is optimal from Level 3, Rare from 7, Legendary from 10. Dying costs one level, which is dropped as runes at the death spot. Source: https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Expeditions
- Rewards (Relics and the currency Murk) are paid out win or lose, and scale with how far the team got. Defeating a Nightlord for the first time also gives a unique Relic. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Expedition
- After each night boss falls, the team's HP, FP and flask charges are refilled before the next day begins. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Expedition
- Runs take roughly 40–45 minutes in practice. One Steam reviewer writes that "every run lasts 45 minutes at most" (https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198093512126/recommended/2622380/). Each day being "around 15 minutes" is from https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=247780 and https://beebom.com/elden-ring-nightreign-three-day-cycle-explained/amp/ (secondary).

### 2.2 The closing circle ("Night's Tide") timetable
Per wiki.gg, each day runs as follows (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Night%27s_Tide and https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Expedition):

| Day clock | Event |
|---|---|
| 0:00–4:30 | Free exploration of the whole map |
| 4:30 | The Night's Tide appears as a large blue ring and shrinks to a marked white circle over 3 minutes |
| 7:30–11:00 | Explore inside the reduced circle (3 min 30 s) |
| 11:00 | Second shrink, "portending the end of night". It moves faster and funnels the team to the night-boss arena (marked by a glowing white tree) over about 3 minutes |
| ~14:00+ | A wave of lesser enemies, then the night boss |

Damage rules for the rain (wiki.gg, https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Night%27s_Tide):
- Inside the rain, the HP bar turns violet and the player takes "2% +15/30 damage once per second (0–110 seconds) then 10% + 30 damage twice per second (110–120 seconds)". After two consecutive minutes it becomes lethal within seconds. The timer resets when the player leaves the rain. Fandom puts the escalation at "exactly 100 seconds" (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Night%27s_Tide), so the exact threshold is disputed between the wikis.
- The rain does not damage enemies, destroy items or disable Sites of Grace (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Night%27s_Tide).
- Content inside the tide shuts down: field bosses deep in the tide despawn, evergaols stay locked, and non-fixed NPCs vanish until the next day (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Night%27s_Tide).
- After the Night 2 boss falls, tide damage rises so that players survive only seconds in it, and Sites of Grace under the tide become inaccessible. This pushes the team on to the Spirit Shelter instead of staying to farm (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Night%27s_Tide). Patch 1.02.2 further "Increased the Night's Tide damage after defeating the second night boss" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1810503566383135).

How the circle shapes the arena. The ring sets the pace and also builds the boss arena: the night fight takes place inside the last circle, around the white tree. Players and reviewers read it as a battle-royale storm. PCGamesN describes it as "a challenging boss rush where you're being herded by a Fortnite-style circle of flame" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/PCGamesN/1800991756343673). One Steam reviewer from battle-royale games says "the circle closing system/day-night cycle felt really at home for me" (https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561199123057821/recommended/2622380/). Another complains: "you have to keep running from the shrinking zone like in Fortnite" (https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198093512126/recommended/2622380/).

### 2.3 Night bosses (end of Day 1 and Day 2)
- A boss appears at the meeting point of the Night's Tide at the end of each of the first two nights. "Before the bosses, a short gauntlet of enemies spawns. These enemies are thematically related to the boss and therefore indicate its identity to allow for some preparation." Examples: fingercreepers before the Centipede Demon, a Demi-Human battle group before the Demi-Human Queen and Swordmaster, cleaver-bearing Omens before the Fell Omen, and a Page, Perfumer and Wandering Nobles before Tree Sentinel and two Royal Cavalrymen. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Night_Boss
- Rune rewards listed there: Centipede Demon 40,000; Demi-Human Queen 20,000 and Swordmaster 20,000; Draconic Tree Sentinel 77,000; Fell Omen 70,000; Royal Cavalryman 2,400 each (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Night_Boss).
- The night-boss pool is selected by expedition target. For example, the Nameless King never appears in the Tricephalos expedition (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Expeditions). In the Tricephalos expedition, Night 1 is a 10/20 split between Demi-Human Queen & Swordmaster and Bell Bearing Hunter, and Night 2 a 10/20 split between Tree Sentinel + 2 Royal Cavalrymen and Fell Omen (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:List_of_Night_Bosses_by_Expedition_Target). The Gaping Jaw expedition typically draws the Gaping Dragon or the Crucible Knight & Golden Hippopotamus duo for Night 2, while its Everdark version draws an Ancient Dragon (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Adel,_Baron_of_Night). Everdark expeditions "use a different encounter pool for end-of-night bosses" (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Everdark_Sovereigns).
- The night-boss roster is a "greatest hits" of FromSoftware bosses: Centipede Demon, Gaping Dragon (Dark Souls), Smelter Demon, The Duke's Dear Freja (Dark Souls II), Nameless King, Dancer of the Boreal Valley (Dark Souls III), and Elden Ring bosses such as Tree Sentinel, Fell Omen, Night's Cavalry duo, Ancient Dragon and Godskin duo. DLC additions include Knight Artorias, Demon Prince and Lord of Blood. Source: https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Nightreign_Bosses
- **Double night bosses.** A special event ("The Night threatens us anew") spawns a second boss after the first falls, for extra runes and Dormant Powers (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Nightreign_Bosses). HP, FP and flasks are refilled before this second fight. A missing Site of Grace after the first kill is the tell that a second boss is coming (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Special_Events).

### 2.4 Day 3
After the Night 2 boss, the party enters the Spirit Shelter. They have "one last opportunity to make preparations" (merchants, levelling) before entering the Nightlord's arena through a petrified tree (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Expedition; https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Nightlords). "Nightlord Slain" flashes on screen on victory (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Nightlords).

## 3. Preparing for a known boss: the core loop

Nightreign gives the player several ways to prepare for a boss they know is coming:

1. **The target is chosen and its weakness is published up front.** Director Junya Ishizaki told the PlayStation Blog: "If poison is effective against an upcoming boss, you may proactively search for and loot items that inflict poison more often in that run" (https://blog.playstation.com/2025/05/07/elden-ring-nightreign-interview-reimagined-game-design-anchored-by-in-depth-combat/). The expedition menu shows each Nightlord's weakness, and the flavour text itself gives a hint. The Gaping Jaw text ends "Only poison will stop it in its tracks" (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Adel,_Baron_of_Night). The Tricephalos text: "When assailed by multiple shadows, only sacred light can hinder the hunt" (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Gladius,_Beast_of_Night).
2. **Weaknesses are scripted, not only numerical.** "Each Nightlord has a scripted weakness to a particular source of damage. For example, dealing even a small amount of Lightning damage to Maris … as it channels its global Sleep spell is enough to break its stance and cancel the attack" (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Nightlords). Poison staggers Adel in Phase 1 and he takes the whole poison tick at once. Fire makes Gnoster's moths burn over 15 seconds. Fire staggers Caligo and applies a 30-second defence debuff. Holy staggers Heolstor and applies an attack and defence debuff. Sources: https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Adel,_Baron_of_Night, https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Gnoster,_Wisdom_of_Night, https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Caligo,_Miasma_of_Night, https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Heolstor_the_Nightlord
3. **The Nightlord can raid the run beforehand.** Several Nightlords appear mid-run as **raids**: a weaker version or a hazard that gives the party a preview and a buff (detailed in 5.1). Raids are weighted by target, so the event pool tells you something about the end boss. Morgott invasion appears in Adel, Gnoster and Heolstor runs; Maris bubbles in Gnoster, Caligo and Heolstor runs; the Gnoster plague in Maris, Libra and Heolstor runs; Libra's curse in Fulghor, Caligo and Heolstor runs. Only one event happens per expedition. Day 1 events trigger 6–8 minutes in, Day 2 events 2–8 minutes in. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Special_Events
4. **Relics are pre-run loadouts.** Relics are equipped before the expedition. Deep of Night guidance is explicit: "It is advisable to prepare well-rounded Relic loadouts that would be helpful during any Nightlord fight, as the Nightlord you're hunting won't be revealed until the expedition begins" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1810503566416852). That implies a normal expedition invites a Relic loadout built for one specific boss.
5. **Shifting Earth.** Map-wide variants (Crater, Mountaintop, Rotted Woods, Noklateo and later others) each give a completion blessing that suits some bosses. The Mountaintop gives anti-dragon damage and frostbite benefits that suit Caligo (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Caligo,_Miasma_of_Night). Noklateo's auto-revive suits Everdark Maris (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Augur_(Everdark_Sovereign)).
6. **Community knowledge.** Within two weeks of launch, players had built a "cheat sheet for the weaknesses and resistances of every Nightlord" (VG247 via Steam: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/VG247/1802354289571673). A Steam reviewer recommends a "boss arena mod" for practising the bosses outside runs. The complaint behind it: "it can feel like ♥♥♥♥ to have to do a whole run only to loose to the boss in 2 seconds" (https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198041133135/recommended/2622380/).

## 4. The Nightlords

Unlocks: Tricephalos is open from the start. Beating Gladius unlocks the other six base expeditions. Any four distinct Nightlords unlock Night Aspect (Heolstor) (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Expedition). The Forsaken Hollows DLC (4 December 2025) added Balancers (Harmonia) and Dreglord (Straghess) (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1818118366170456; https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Expedition).

Weakness summary. Negative negation means more damage taken. Values are from the Fandom table at https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Nightlords, where "+35%" denotes bonus damage.

| Expedition | Nightlord | Signposted weakness | Notable values |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tricephalos | Gladius, Beast of Night | Holy | Holy +35%, Fire −50%, Madness immune |
| Gaping Jaw | Adel, Baron of Night | Poison | Poison/Rot/Frost/Sleep 154 (low), Lightning −50% |
| Sentient Pest | Gnoster (moth) + Faurtis (scorpion) | Fire | Moth Fire +40%, scorpion Fire +35% |
| Augur | Maris, Fathom of Night | Lightning | Lightning +40%, immune to Poison, Bleed, Sleep and Madness |
| Equilibrious Beast | Libra, Creature of Night | Madness | Holy +35%, Fire +20%, Madness 154 |
| Darkdrift Knight | Fulghor, Champion of Nightglow | Lightning | Lightning +20%, Holy −30% |
| Fissure in the Fog | Caligo, Miasma of Night | Fire | Fire +35%, Strike +15% |
| Night Aspect | Heolstor the Nightlord | Holy | Ph1 Holy +35%, Fire +20%; Ph2 Holy +20%, Lightning +20% |
| Balancers (DLC) | Weapon-Bequeathed Harmonia | Sleep | Sleep 84 (very low), Holy −30% |
| Dreglord (DLC) | Traitorous Straghess | "no obvious weakness" (Holy) | Holy +25%, Fire +20% |

HP scaling. Boss HP scales linearly with party size. Adel has 13,140 HP solo and 39,420 trio (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Adel,_Baron_of_Night). Fextralife gives Gladius 11,328 / 22,656 / 33,984 for solo, duo and trio (https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Gladius+Beast+of+Night). This matters for the solo controversy in section 6.

### 4.1 Gladius, Beast of Night (Tricephalos): the tutorial boss
- A three-headed wolf with an extra set of forelimbs. It swings a greatsword on a long chain and launches fireballs. It can split into three one-headed wolves, and only the original keeps the flail-sword. A howling roar signals the form change (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Gladius,_Beast_of_Night).
- Phase 1 has basic swipes, slams, fire breath and bites. At about 75% HP Gladius becomes wrapped in flames, flies forward as a fireball and splits into three wolves. After "roughly 45 seconds, the wolves reform". The three wolves "alternate between passive and aggressive states, so players can focus their attention on whichever wolf is currently being aggressive". Telegraphs include "fire particles will be seen inside its mouth" before the breath. Stance 120. Solo players are advised to "retreat from the wolves until they merge back". Source: https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Gladius+Beast+of+Night
- Gladius was the only Nightlord in the network test (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Gladius,_Beast_of_Night). Ishizaki said it was "due to receive some 'updates'" after test feedback (https://gamingbolt.com/elden-ring-nightreign-will-adjust-the-difficulty-following-network-test-feedback). A search summary characterises these as buffs (secondary, same URL).
- Everdark (the last Sovereign to debut): starts split. The wolves merge into a new phase at about 75%, which opens with a giant explosion "similar to the one Heolstor does". Gladius can split and merge again at about 25% and gains Fulghor-like long-distance charges. The wiki notes it can two-shot even high-Vigor characters (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Tricephalos_(Everdark_Sovereign)). Fextralife's Everdark HP is 17,558 / 35,116 / 52,674 for solo, duo and trio. A stacking enrage applies: when Gladius is damaged while walking slowly with the sword in its mouth, it gains +7.5% attack for 18 seconds, stacking three times (https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Gladius+Beast+of+Night).

### 4.2 Adel, Baron of Night (Gaping Jaw): a weakness that switches off by phase
- A black dragon with a sideways jaw. Phase 1 deals only Standard damage. Phases 2 and 3 add violet lightning. The biting grab inflicts heavy Blood Loss, heals Adel, and grants him 112% damage for 30 seconds, shown by a red aura. Button-mashing does not end the grab early. Allies can free a grabbed player by breaking his stance. Source: https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Adel,_Baron_of_Night
- Poison is the scripted weakness. When poison procs, Adel takes the full damage-over-time at once and can be re-poisoned sooner. It also staggers him, but in Phase 1 only (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Adel,_Baron_of_Night). The design lesson: the advertised weakness is strongest early, and the boss partly outgrows it.
- Everdark: starts in Phase 2 (so poison no longer staggers) with +25% HP. At 60% (Fandom) or 50% (wiki.gg) he teleports erratically, then makes a lightning slam that creates a tornado. The tornado deals Lightning damage to players outside its ring, so the eye of the storm is the only safe zone. Blue Spiritstream jumps appear so players can enter from above. The tornado lasts 60 seconds with a 60-second cooldown. Sources: https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Adel,_Baron_of_Night; https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Gaping_Jaw_(Everdark_Sovereign). This is a boss-made shrinking circle inside the fight, echoing the Night's Tide.

### 4.3 Gnoster, Wisdom of Night (Sentient Pest): a duo with a shared bar
- A moth (Gnoster) and a whip scorpion (Faurtis Stoneshield) share one life bar. The moth showers magic projectiles and sprays poison mist. A poisoned player grows a neck parasite that hatches when the scorpion roars, dealing 15 damage per second for 120 seconds (1,800 total). Teammates can remove it by attacking the infected ally, or the player can eat a Neutralizing Bolus before it hatches. When Faurtis hardens it takes 90% less damage but stops moving, which leaves a window to focus the moth. At 50% they merge: the moth mounts the scorpion. A roar marks a target, then a 10-second magic beam fires while the scorpion charges three times and finishes with a conical shockwave. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Gnoster,_Wisdom_of_Night
- Fandom gives HP of 13,027 solo and 39,081 trio (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Gnoster,_Wisdom_of_Night).
- Everdark: the fight opens with the merged pair (their combined HP is lower than the regular version). On depletion, a third entity, **Animus, Ascendant Light** (a red moth), revives both at 25%. Animus cannot be targeted while both hosts live. It possesses a corpse and revives it, and is vulnerable during that moment. Damage to the possessed host also damages Animus. If a possession lasts 55 seconds, or Animus stays free for 55 seconds, it unleashes an ultimate attack (red laser beams). Killing Animus ends the fight. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Sentient_Pest_(Everdark_Sovereign). This version shipped with a soft-lock bug (Animus stuck out of bounds), fixed on 4 July 2025 (same source; hotfix https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1803527891762827). It was tuned again in 1.02.2 (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1810503566383135).

### 4.4 Maris, Fathom of Night (Augur): summoned adds that fight back
- A floating, jellyfish-like stellar being. It is passive and drifts out of melee reach. Moves: Spinning Charge, Cyclone, Splash (a bouncing water wave, best dodged by rolling into it), Summon Jellyfish (which chase and explode in Sleep mist), Summon Plants (stationary lashing stalks), and in Phase 2 only, Atomic Teardrop (slow-falling Sleep bombs). Source: https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Maris,_Fathom_of_Night
- **Hypnosis** is the defining telegraph. Maris "makes a siren sound, repositions, and enters a cradle position" to channel an "unavoidable" arena-wide Sleep spell. It must be interrupted, and even a small amount of Lightning breaks the channel (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Maris,_Fathom_of_Night; https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Nightlords).
- The adds Maris summons work as ammunition against it: "Plants and Jellyfish defeated by the Nightfarers release bubbles that chase and damage Maris. Destroying the slow-falling bombs releases larger bubbles that deal considerably more damage." Phase 2 begins at about half health (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Maris,_Fathom_of_Night).
- Everdark: Phase 1 is a weak "Augur" form. Killing it drops a **legendary +3 copy of each player's starting weapon carrying the Storm Ruler skill**. Then the true Maris, now huge and out of reach of most attacks, descends. Storm Ruler charges over 5 seconds, restores FP while charging, and fires a vertical wave that "can deal thousands of damage". At half health Maris spawns five orbiting laser orbs, and destroying all of them staggers it. Tidal waves come in quick succession at low HP. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Augur_(Everdark_Sovereign). Patch 1.02.1 specifically buffed Storm Ruler for this fight (VG247 via Steam: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/VG247/1807966710803986). The design lesson: the fight hands out the tool that solves it, so the build gathered during the run matters less. The wiki says "Getting the right equipment is of lower importance" for this run.

### 4.5 Libra, Creature of Night (Equilibrious Beast): the boss that bargains
- Libra appears first as the Scale-Bearing Merchant and offers three deals drawn at random from a pool. Each is a "Monkey's Paw wish": a strong buff with a real cost. Players may refuse or attack him outright (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Libra,_Creature_of_Night). Examples: "I want a high level" grants 3 levels, but each flask drink subtracts a level. "I desire a powerful weapon" grants a random purple weapon for 2 levels. "I wish for eventual greatness" triples HP, FP and Stamina but halves all other attributes. "I wish to fight at full strength" buffs both the player and Libra (same source). A 60-second countdown runs, after which the fight starts automatically (https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Libra+Creature+of+Night).
- In the fight he meditates cross-legged inside a golden sphere to inflict Madness. Shattering the barrier, by force or by destroying nearby sigils, makes him rampage like a beast. If he finishes channelling, he gains a powered-up state. His attacks scatter false-gold pieces that reduce Madness buildup when collected. A goat-eye icon over the players warns of an incoming Frenzy proc (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Libra,_Creature_of_Night; https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Libra+Creature+of+Night).
- His weakness is his own element. Libra inflicts Madness but is "extremely vulnerable" to it, and a maddened Libra stops casting and relies on his staff (https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Libra+Creature+of+Night). Patch 1.01.3 "Increased the chance of obtaining weapons with the madness status ailment" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1802893906699676). VG247 described this as FromSoftware making "the Libra fight easier" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/VG247/1802893906705833). The fix went into the loot table during the run, not the boss.
- Everdark (widely regarded as the hardest): the phase change comes after about 15% HP. Libra then casts announced **hexes**, shown as on-screen text, and takes 70% less damage while casting. The hexes are:
  - Summon the Condemned: hostile copies of each player's Nightfarer.
  - Incite a riot: up to two Condemned per player, with friendly fire among them.
  - Preach madness: every attack inflicts Madness.
  - Shroud allies: players cannot see each other.
  - Offer a challenge: Max HP halved for about 30 seconds.
  - Ever-watching eye.
  - False blessing: unlimited flasks, but each drink costs a level.

  Condemned left alive when he re-summons are healed 50% and gain +20% damage. Madness stops him casting hexes. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Equilibrious_Beast_(Everdark_Sovereign). Patch 1.02.2 later toned the Condemned down (lower Guardian attack, removed a damage-negation gain, lower death resistance) (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1810503566383135). Player verdict: "Everdark libra is genuinely the worst boss design of all time … he keeps summoning his friends until he has an army" (https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561199685856566/recommended/2622380/). That reviewer still recommends the game.

### 4.6 Fulghor, Champion of Nightglow (Darkdrift Knight): the pure duellist
- A masked, one-armed centaur with a lance and holy attacks, fast and mobile. Moves include a Charging Stomp that leaves fissures, a channelled Golden Lance that "can be interrupted by attacking him directly during the cast", and a Golden Charge with explosive trails. At 50% "his left arm bursts from his body": an amorphous Night arm that adds wide claw slams and sweeps. Weak to Lightning, resistant to Holy. Stance 155. Source: https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Fulghor+Champion+of+Nightglow. HP is 14,273 solo and 42,820 trio per Fandom (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Fulghor,_Champion_of_Nightglow); Fextralife gives 11,894 / 23,788 / 35,682, a disagreement between the wikis.
- Everdark: starts with the Night arm. At half health he cuts it off and regrows a real arm, and Lightning no longer staggers him. He gains a bow (an arrow rain spreading outward from his position) and a twinblade (5-hit, 4-hit and 1-hit combos with a blind spot at his rear). His signature nuke: he gallops into the air, marks one player with a tracking golden beam, then fires an explosive arrow. The marked player is told to hold position so allies can scatter, then sprint once the beam locks. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Darkdrift_Knight_(Everdark_Sovereign)

### 4.7 Caligo, Miasma of Night (Fissure in the Fog): weather as a telegraph
- A four-winged ice dragon. Her breath deals Magic damage, builds Frostbite and leaves ground crystals that explode when stepped on. Each Frostbite proc permanently lowers the victim's Frostbite resistance by 15 for the fight. When she hides in dense mist, one of two attacks is coming. Either a giant ice disc above the arena shatters into shards (safe spots are far away or right next to her), or she draws in winds, raises ice pillars and releases a frigid blast that players must shelter behind the pillars from. At half health she flies strafing runs that shower ice shards, and crystals begin forming on their own. Fire staggers her and applies a 30-second defence debuff. She counts as an Ancient Dragon. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Caligo,_Miasma_of_Night
- HP is 12,007 / 24,014 / 36,021 (Everdark 16,810 / 33,620 / 50,430) per https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Caligo+Miasma+of+Night.
- Everdark: starts in Phase 2. At half health she encases herself in ice armour (−50% damage taken) that repeated Fire breaks. She can also cast a 30-second blizzard with constant Frostbite buildup (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Fissure_in_the_Fog_(Everdark_Sovereign)). Patch 1.02 fixed Caligo wandering out of the battle area and desynced ice-block positions in co-op (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1806698490676875).

### 4.8 Heolstor the Nightlord (Night Aspect): the final boss
- Phase 1, **The Shape of Night**: a hunched figure in rags that is already wandering the arena, dragging a broken sword. There is no introduction cutscene, which is unusual (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Heolstor_the_Nightlord). It "usually only does one attack at a time, having very little combo potential". At about 60% it draws a second greatsword and gains "roars and dash combos". HP 4,984 / 9,968 / 14,952 (https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Heolstor+the+Nightlord).
- Phase 2, **Heolstor**: silver armour, a Moonlight-style greatsword, teleports (including a backstab teleport against players who keep their distance), magic waves. HP 11,214 / 22,428 / 33,642 (https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Heolstor+the+Nightlord). After enough damage he flies up, "slices the skybox open, imbuing its broken sword with the power of a previous Nightlord" (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Heolstor_the_Nightlord). Fextralife describes the "Night of the Lord" slam whose fissures carry a randomly selected element (fire, lightning, holy, poison, sleep, madness, frostbite) (https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Heolstor+the+Nightlord).
- Holy is the scripted weakness: enough Holy staggers him and debuffs his attack and defence. Phase 1 is also weak to Fire; Phase 2 swaps Fire for Lightning, though only Holy staggers. He is the only Nightlord that can be parried, and a critical needs 3 parries (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Heolstor_the_Nightlord).
- Patch 1.02 added the option to skip part of the cutscene after defeating the Night Aspect (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1806698490676875).
- Design note: the final boss works as a recap. Phase 1 is a slow, readable duel. Phase 2 borrows the powers of the earlier Nightlords, and the Everdark Gladius explosion reuses a Heolstor attack (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Gladius,_Beast_of_Night).

### 4.9 DLC Nightlords (The Forsaken Hollows, December 2025)
- **Weapon-Bequeathed Harmonia (Balancers):** seven Valkyries (spear, halberd and hammer variants) appear at once. When all seven fall, the last one is "crowned" with a new moveset and the others resurrect. After that all damage goes to a shared bar, and killing the crowned one passes the crown on and repeats the transition. Their very low Sleep resistance (84) lets players thin the pack, but hitting a sleeper wakes her. They can be parried. Source: https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Weapon-Bequeathed_Harmonia. The Everdark version ("Salvation's Standard-Bearers"): Phase 2 coats the arena in blood. Blood loss removes 50% max HP. Fallen Bearers resurrect on a timer with a stacking +7% attack. At 80%, 50% and 20% HP they merge into a giant worm with a telegraphed bloom explosion that players interrupt by destroying clots or stance-breaking. Only two Bearers (one solo) lock onto each player at a time (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Balancers_(Everdark_Sovereign)).
- **Traitorous Straghess (Dreglord):** a corpse fiend with 11,554 HP across both phases (solo, wiki.gg). Its expedition tip says it has "no obvious weakness", though it takes extra Slash, Pierce, Fire and Holy damage (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Traitorous_Straghess). This is the one deliberate break from the "known weakness" contract.

## 5. Raids, events and the run's own boss rhythm

### 5.1 Nightlord raids during the day
Per https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Special_Events and the boss pages:
- **Sentient Pest raid:** locust swarms steal levels in explosions, then fly towards Gnoster's hidden location. Killing it returns the levels and grants a rune-on-hit boon. The stolen levels are permanently lost if the raid is not completed (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Expeditions).
- **Augur raid:** giant bubbles teleport whoever touches one into a fight with a lesser Augur. The reward is 50,000 runes plus a boon that makes flasks restore FP (https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Maris,_Fathom_of_Night).
- **Libra raid:** Libra's face flashes on screen and curses everyone with −30% max HP. He is marked on the map with a red circle and lifts the curse for 15,000 runes on Day 1 or 35,000 on Day 2, or if killed. Paying grants an attack buff that scales with runes held and unlocks cost-free deals.
- **Fell Omen raid:** the Fell Omen hunts one marked player across the whole map. If the target dies, the team takes 10% more damage for the rest of the run. If the Omen dies, every grace touched raises damage.
- **Balancers raid (DLC):** the team is split across points of interest and flasks are cut by 3. Each Balancer killed restores one flask. Finishing grants a random auto-revive blessing (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Weapon-Bequeathed_Harmonia).
- **Caligo raid (DLC):** a fogged ice storm. A single hit on Caligo drives her off and grants a dodge-on-lethal-damage buff (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Caligo,_Miasma_of_Night).

### 5.2 Other events
Meteor strike (a Fallingstar Beast), Wandering Mausoleum (duplicate a weapon), Frenzied Flame Tower (a Madness hazard that rewards a frenzy weapon and buff, useful against Libra), and night hordes (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Special_Events). Cataclysm events add "Night Invaders" (patch 1.03.2 notes, https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1821922921815369).

## 6. The launch controversy: solo scaling and the answer

### 6.1 What went wrong
- The game was "built with three-player co-op in mind, and that often leaves lone wanderers coughing in the dust" (PCGamesN via Steam: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/PCGamesN/1800991756350558). PCGamesN's solo guide called solo difficulty "excruciating" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/PCGamesN/1800991756337462).
- There was no two-player option at launch. Before launch, Ishizaki told IGN the team "kind of overlooked and neglected" duos (VG247 headline via Steam: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/VG247/1800357164436020). Coverage at https://wccftech.com/elden-ring-nightreign-may-get-proper-duo-support-post-launch/amp/ (secondary). Duos could only queue as a trio, or use a modded two-player workaround that appeared within days (RPS via Steam: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/Rock, Paper, Shotgun/1800991756380689).
- Steam reviews opened at "Mixed", making it "From's worst-rated game", with missing features blamed more than the format (PCGamesN via Steam: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/PCGamesN/1800991756343673). Metacritic: 77 from 58 critics, the lowest for a modern FromSoftware game, against 96 for Elden Ring (https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/elden-ring-nightreigns-low-scores-make-it-fromsoftwares-lowest-rated-modern-game, secondary). One search summary reported "Mixed" from more than 31,000 reviews (https://www.laptopmag.com/gaming/console-pc-games/why-elden-ring-nightreign-is-both-a-hit-and-a-headache-for-fans, secondary).
- The structural reason: Nightlord HP is divided by three for solo play (Adel 13,140 vs 39,420), but a lone player also loses revives, aggro splitting, healing support and two other players' loot pickups. One negative reviewer says "the healing and damage scaling for the game is not very well done, as it starts high and only gains a small amount for each person in the expedition". The same reviewer criticises the snowball effect: "small initial mistakes causing disproportionately large repercussions as you fall behind the progression curve" (https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198107968668/recommended/2622380/).

### 6.2 The response, in order
1. **30 May 2025 (launch day):** an announcement said patch 1.01.1 would add "Automatic Revival Upon Defeat", allowing revival once per night boss battle, to solo expeditions, and increase rune gain (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1800991756346576).
2. **2 June 2025, patch 1.01.1:** shipped those two solo-only changes. It also raised the number of high-rarity Relics awarded for reaching Day 3, and raised high-rarity odds from the Small Jar Bazaar (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1800991756438483). VG247 called the changes "not massive" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/VG247/1800991756439447). PCGamesN said the patch "finally makes solo runs much fairer" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/PCGamesN/1800991756441449).
3. **3 June 2025:** FromSoftware confirmed duos and "enhanced" Nightlord fights (RPS via Steam: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/Rock, Paper, Shotgun/1800991756474030).
4. **16 June 2025:** Steam rating reached "Very Positive", "less than a month after it debuted to a 'mixed' reception" (PCGamesN via Steam: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/PCGamesN/1802354289691394).
5. **18 June 2025, patch 1.01.3:** more Madness weapons (helping against Libra). It also fixed "a bug where players would lose a battle after being revived from near death against a Nightlord" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1802893906699676). A player survey followed (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1802893906716031).
6. **31 July 2025, patch 1.02:** "Added Two Player Expeditions", with "The game balance will be adjusted to the current number of players." It also removed the idle-disconnect timer for solo and fixed a bug where allies revived with a Wending Grace "would be counted as defeated during a battle against Night bosses or Nightlords" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1806698490676875). The patch was delayed one day by a tsunami warning in Japan (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1806698490640979).
7. **Later:** 1.02.2 (10 September 2025) rebalanced every Nightfarer and the Everdark fights (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1810503566383135). 1.03 (3 December 2025) broadly buffed melee and low-HP triggers (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1818118366169784). 1.03.2 (16 January 2026) raised Nightfarer damage negation and scaled Revenant's spirits and Executor's transformation "in accordance to the depth level" in Deep of Night (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1821922921815369).

Takeaways. The first fix was a one-off per-fight safety net (one auto-revive per boss) plus faster rune income. It did not lower boss HP. The structural fix, an explicit duo setting with per-headcount balance, took two months. Specific solo HP multipliers for duo mode: not found.

- **What they are:** "challenging versions of the standard Expeditions, available for a limited time on a weekly rotation". They require beating the base Nightlord first. They are "significantly more difficult, featuring upgraded strength and new movesets. They start at Phase 2 and advance to an empowered Phase 3". Online only. Source: https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Everdark_Sovereigns
- **Rollout:** announced 18 June 2025, three weeks after launch, with Everdark Adel available 19–26 June. Fulghor (26 June) and Gnoster (3 July) followed. The first three then returned in pairs, and Maris (31 July), Fissure in the Fog (7 August), Libra (14 August) and Tricephalos (21 August) came later. After that all seven rotated two at a time. Everdark Harmonia arrived one week after the DLC, and from 1 January 2026 the rotation shows three at once (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Everdark_Sovereign).
- **Rewards:** 25 Sovereign Sigils on a first kill, then 5–7 per repeat, spent at the Collector Signboard. Each Sovereign also unlocks a "Dark Night of the X" relic (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Everdark_Sovereign; https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Everdark_Sovereigns). HP rises by about +25% (Adel and Fulghor; https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Adel,_Baron_of_Night) to around +50% on Fextralife's solo figures (Gladius 11,328 → 17,558).
- **The remix pattern:** skip the boss's teaching phase (Phase 1), start at the escalated phase, then add a third phase that changes the fight's rules:
  - Adel: a tornado safe zone.
  - Gnoster: a third, possessing boss.
  - Maris: a handed-out weapon and an out-of-reach boss.
  - Libra: rule-changing hexes and player clones.
  - Fulghor: a marked-target nuke.
  - Caligo: Fire-breakable armour.
  - Gladius: repeated split and merge.

  Sources: the wiki.gg Everdark pages cited in section 4.
- **Reception:** PCGamesN reported that players "were still dancing with the devils under the pale moonlight", which pushed FromSoftware towards Deep of Night (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/PCGamesN/1809235871593508). CGMagazine called them "Brutal" (https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/CGMagazine/1802893906733092). A Steam reviewer counts the 7 enhanced Nightlords as "basically completely new bosses" (https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197963859335/recommended/2622380/).

## 8. Deep of Night: the boss as an unknown

- Added on 11 September 2025 and unlocked after beating Night Aspect. "The target Nightlord will not be revealed until the expedition begins". Enemies are stronger, and "variants" with a red sheen appear. Weapons can carry several effects, including detrimental ones. Depths Relics go in 3 extra slots and work only in this mode. Rating moves the player through Depths. "Sometimes, an Everdark Sovereign will appear on the third day." Source: https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1810503566416852
- Depth thresholds: 0–999 = Depth 1, 1,000 = 2, 2,000 = 3, 4,000 = 4, 6,000+ = 5. A win is worth +200, with +100 bonuses for concealed points of interest or a concealed Nightlord. Losses are steeper at higher Depth and earlier in the run: −200 extra for a Day 1 loss, −100 for Day 2, 0 against the Nightlord. From Depth 2 the Nightlord may be its Everdark form, revealed only at the arena. From Depth 3 the target may be hidden until the final day (https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightreign:Deep_of_Night). FromSoftware's own wording invited players "to see if you can surpass Depth 3. Depths 4 to 5 will feature an endless battle" (quoted at https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Expeditions).
- Players call it "basically a Boss rushing simulator" and the most addictive mode (https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198175326815/recommended/2622380/).

## 9. Lessons for Survivor Unchained

1. **Name the boss early and reward preparation.** Nightreign's best-received fights tell the player the boss and its weakness up front. The run then becomes a shopping list (Holy for Gladius, Poison for Adel, Lightning for Maris). For a 30-minute ember arena, show the 30:00 boss (or a choice of two) at the start or on the 15:00 blessing screen. Make at least one drafted weapon tag a scripted counter, not just a damage multiplier.
2. **Script weaknesses as interrupts.** A small amount of Lightning cancels Maris's arena-wide Sleep, poison staggers Adel, and fire breaks Caligo's armour. In an auto-firing game this maps onto a "channel" telegraph that one elemental hit, or N hits, breaks. It turns weapon choice into a decision mid-fight, not just a DPS check.
3. **Let the weakness fade as phases go on.** Adel stops being staggered by poison in Phase 2, and Everdark Fulghor ignores Lightning stagger after his arm regrows. This stops a perfect counter-draft from trivialising the whole fight while still paying off the preparation.
4. **The shrinking circle can build the arena.** The Night's Tide is both the clock and the arena wall, and it shuts off content outside it so the funnel feels natural. For the 25:00–30:00 approach, a closing ring of dawn-dark or ash that herds the horde and the player into the boss ground would mark the climax spatially without a loading screen. Keep the edge lethal but survivable for a short time, as the 2-minute escalation does.
5. **Preview the boss mid-run.** Nightlord raids (the Augur bubble, the Libra curse, the Gnoster locusts) show the end boss in miniature and pay out a related buff. A 15:00 mini-encounter with an echo of the 30:00 boss teaches its telegraphs and could offer the great blessing as the reward.
6. **Use thematic trash as a warning.** Nightreign spawns enemies related to the night boss just before it appears. Bias the horde composition in the last minutes before 30:00 towards the boss's faction so attentive players can predict and adapt.
7. **Bosses that change the rules beat bosses that only add HP.** The Everdark remixes kept a fixed roster fresh by skipping the teaching phase and adding one rule-changing phase: a tornado safe zone, a possessing third entity, a boss-given weapon, announced hexes. For endless mode after the win and for repeat nights, plan "ember-dark" remixes of each boss instead of stat inflation alone.
8. **Bargaining bosses work, but cap the cruelty.** Libra's Monkey's-Paw deals fit dark fantasy well. Everdark Libra's stacking adds, clone summons and vision-blocking hexes became the most hated fight. Telegraph every hex in text, as Libra does, and stop adds from snowballing through heals on re-summon.
9. **Fail softly at the boss.** Losing a 30–45 minute run in seconds at the final boss is a recurring complaint, and boss-practice mods exist to address it. Consider one auto-revive per boss (FromSoftware's day-two fix), a practice mode for bosses already met, or partial rewards on a boss loss (Nightreign pays Relics win or lose, scaled by progress).
10. **Plan solo balance from the start.** Nightreign's HP scaled linearly with players while survivability did not, and the duo mode was "overlooked". Survivor Unchained is single-player, so the lesson applies to build variance: check the 30:00 boss against weak and off-meta drafts, not just strong ones, and add a catch-up mechanic so early mistakes do not snowball.
11. **Do not let travel crowd out fighting.** Players praise the bosses and complain about running. The arena structure already avoids long travel, but the 0–15 and 15–30 stretches should build towards the boss with clear signals (blessing choices, boss-themed elites) so they do not feel like filler.
12. **Keep shipping bosses.** Reception recovered as bosses were added (Everdark, DLC Nightlords). By 2026 the main complaint was "0 boss updates". Plan the roster and its remixes as ongoing content.
