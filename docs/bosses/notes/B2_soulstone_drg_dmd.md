# B2 — Boss fights in Soulstone Survivors, Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor and Death Must Die

Research strand for Survivor Unchained (night half: 30-minute ember arenas, boss at 30:00, endless after the win). Researched 3 October 2026. Every factual claim carries a URL. "(secondary)" marks claims seen only in a search-result summary or a third-party summary, not on the primary page. "Not found" means I looked and could not verify.

A note on terms in the brief. Two of the terms do not match these games. Soulstone Survivors has no separate "Void/Corruption" difficulty system. "Corruption" there is the *Overwhelming Corruption* curse plus the *Corrupted Void Hunter* bosses (see §1). Death Must Die has no "Torment". Its difficulty ladder is **Darkness** (0–30 for loot, extended to 100), plus selectable difficulty modifiers (see §3). DRG: Survivor has **no Hiveguard**. The DRG:S wiki's creature list and every patch note from Early Access to the Knockout announcement (September 2026) name only the Dreadnought, the Dreadnought Twins, the Ommoran Heartstone (Escort Duty) and the Brood Nexus (Egg Hunt) as end bosses ([DRG:S Creatures](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Creatures); [Steam news feed, app 2321470](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470)). The Hiveguard belongs only to the original Deep Rock Galactic ([DRG wiki](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Glyphid_Dreadnought_Hiveguard)).

## Summary: lessons for Survivor Unchained

1. **Boss HP should not scale with the clock or the player's speed.** In March 2024 Soulstone changed Lord HP so that it is "no longer related to the time of a match, in general making it harder for very fast builds but easier for slower builds" ([Soulstone, A New Beginning](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5749481331437537172)). Use a fixed HP per difficulty tier and let heat or curse tiers scale it openly. Do not tie it to elapsed time.
2. **Give a spawn grace beat.** Soulstone Lords now "appear encased in statues, and only when infused start to fight, giving you a few seconds to prepare" (same source). DRG:S hides its bosses in cocoons that the player can choose to pop early. Our 30:00 boss should get a short, readable arrival: a statue, a cocoon or an ember gate. It should not just appear inside the horde.
3. **Pause or thin the horde while the boss is alive, and fence the fight.** In Death Must Die, normal timed minion spawns stop while a boss is alive and resume when it dies. Boss waves raise a barrier that traps the player, and the barrier dissolves after a set time even if the boss lives ([DMD wiki: Monster](https://dmd.fandom.com/wiki/Monster); [DMD wiki: Waves](https://dmd.fandom.com/wiki/Waves)). That one rule fixes readability and stops a boss from being cheesed by kiting forever.
4. **Enemy telegraphs must draw above the player's effects, and the game must ship good defaults.** Soulstone's longest-running complaint is boss telegraphs hidden under the player's own VFX. The developer answered with a visibility slider. It also exempted boss, Void Presence and enemy-hero attacks from that slider, and hid the player's own target circles in endless ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2066020/discussions/0/3591086730691827584); [v0.9.028e](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/4789043687478223868); [Devlog #5](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/6240387642459054660)). Players still said a slider is a band-aid. Build the layering rule into the renderer from day one.
5. **Punish stalling with a ramping clock, not a hard enrage.** DRG:S raises the Alien Threat Level by one every 60 s during a boss fight ([DRG:S Hazard](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Hazard)). It also pays out a loot crate graded by time to kill: Legendary, Epic, Rare, or nothing ([DRG:S Objectives](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Objectives)). This fixed a real problem: "the optimal play was often to ignore the end-of-stage elite to farm" ([1.0 OUT NOW](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/1811138915355931)). For an arena that runs on after the win, a kill-speed reward ladder works better than an all-or-nothing enrage.
6. **One wind-up plus a lateral dodge is the core of a crowd-boss.** The DRG:S Dreadnought's jump is learned in one sentence: "When he stops he is about to jump. When he stops do a 90 degree turn and he will miss" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/6292161380820333006)). Players who miss the tell call it an instakill. Some ask for a ground circle as well as the pose ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/6292161380814310056)). Pair every big hit with **both** a body wind-up and a ground decal, and react to the wind-up, not the decal. Death Must Die's Frog King is criticised because its slam must be read from the sprite before the red circle appears ([Steam, Act 3 feedback](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/596273605315164244/)).
7. **Use the boss to reshape the horde and the terrain, not just to add HP.** The best-liked mechanics here act on the horde or the terrain. The Dreadnought spits cocoons that hatch Glyphids and stomps up rock spikes the player must dig out of ([Dreadnought Rework](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5766372999943095109)). The Brood Nexus sprouts that must be cleared ([Heavy Duty](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/1831432155567663)). The Ommoran Beamers turn the fight into an errand run ([Objectives](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Objectives)).
8. **Don't make the boss deny a build's core verb.** Death Must Die's Frog King cannot be dashed through, which cancelled builds based on dashing through foes ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/596273145166509317/)). The Lady's charm on summons caused a hotfix that cut it from 20% to 7% ([Act III Hotfix](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1797820624532108)). Counter-mechanics should be answerable, for example an add that channels control and can be killed, which is what DMD's developer proposed (Act 3 feedback thread above).
9. **Fights that let you "dance" are the loved ones.** The Lady, "the epitome of a great boss … you can dance with her," rotates between ranged and melee patterns (Act 3 feedback thread). DRG:S's Twins heal-balance is disliked in the parent game because it punishes focus fire. DRG:S kept it as a health *transfer* with a net gain ([Hotfix 0.2.280d](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5842939267327369373)). Avoid mechanics that undo the player's damage.
10. **Endless needs caps on boss speed, or the late game becomes "immortal or one-shot".** Players reported endless bosses whose "attack speed almost becomes instant and most attacks one shot you" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2066020/discussions/0/5829413348552209543)). Soulstone later capped enemy attack speed at +70% and movement speed at +70%. HP keeps scaling ×10 per cycle ([Endless Mode wiki](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Endless_Mode)). It also added minimum cooldowns so Lords keep using their full kit at high levels ([Winterfall](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5000716037440647402)). Scale HP and damage, but cap tempo so telegraphs stay readable.

## 1. Soulstone Survivors (Game Smithing, 1.0 on 17 June 2025)

### 1.1 Structure: where bosses sit

Soulstone is **not** clock-driven like our arenas. Bosses arrive when the player meets kill objectives.

| Mode | Boss trigger | Bosses | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Void Fields (core mode, 5 maps) | Kill quota met → 1–4 **Lords of the Void** spawn; kill them all → next quota; **five sequences** end the match | 7 Lords (Alexi, Colummeth, Aelfraed, Tiaggro, Keythaiz, Gammuth, Allaxiz); with curses up to 20 Lord kills per match | [Void Fields](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Void_Fields); [Bosses](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Bosses) |
| Endless (yellow portal) | Unlocked by finishing Void Fields in **under 15 min**; same loop, random map per cycle | Lords, scaling per cycle | [Endless Mode](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Endless_Mode); [Portals](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Portals) |
| Overlord (red portal), the de facto **boss rush** | Unlocked by finishing in **under 13 min**; circular, obstacle-free Overlord Arena; "tasks are quicker… eliminating of just a few elite enemies… focus squarely on the boss encounters" | Rising number of Lords per cycle | [Overlord](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Overlord); [v0.9.028c](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/6368680725440765344) |
| Titan Hunt | Collect 15 Void Fragments from map tasks (white/green/blue/purple; harder tasks = 2–4 Lords or 4 "Shadows of the Void") → arena door opens → Titan | 7 Titans; after 4 Titan Blood → Netherworld → Corrupted Void Hunter → (Sigil of the Void) Void King | [Titan Hunt](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Titan_Hunt) |
| Unholy Cathedral (daily) | Four cycles; final cycle ends in a boss | Mh'thaeus, the Heretic | [Unholy Cathedral](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Unholy_Cathedral) |
| Secret events | Map events open gold or Christmas portals | Ruanok the Broodlord; Bumble the Abominable | [Portals](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Portals) |

The developer's own summary of Overlord: "Face an increasing number of powerful Lords of the Void, which require less and less enemies per cycle until you are facing Lords all day long!" ([v0.9.028c](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/6368680725440765344)).

**Speed-gated endless.** Endless and Overlord open only if the main match was fast (15 or 13 minutes). Only builds that are already strong get the post-win mode ([Portals](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Portals)). This differs from Survivor Unchained's plan of endless after a fixed 30:00 boss. It is a cheap way to keep endless for strong builds. Since early 2024 portals need a one-second interaction, after players ended great runs by walking into the home portal by accident ([1 Year Anniversary update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5288974951222245503); [Dev Talk #1](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5229301621580813946)).

### 1.2 Lords of the Void: mechanics and telegraphs

The wiki gives no per-Lord HP; I found none. Lord HP scales by curse points (+20% per point), by cycle (below) and by mode. Ability details from patch notes:

- **Aelfraed (Fallen King):** Bladestorm (a spinning approach). The developers made him use it from further away and move slightly faster ([v0.9.027h](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/4732747437224834340)), then made him slow down during it ([A New Beginning LIVE](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5756237364326943757)). They also enlarged the visual indicator of his Shield Bash ([Scorching Sands](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5143713753733103900)).
- **Gammuth (Incarnation of Fire):** the Flamethrower damage area was realigned to match its visual ([Omen of Spring](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5036749277244302242)). The Fireball was wrongly faded by the player's visibility slider "again" ([Arms of the Ascended](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5409450219988160528)).
- **Keythaiz (Prophetess):** Lightning Bomb dealt damage "in an area larger than the visual would indicate", which was fixed ([Cursed Fleet](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/6148070194801615587)). Colummeth has an Ice Bomb counterpart ([Scorching Sands](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5143713753733103900)).
- **Alexi (Plaguebearer):** Poison Volley is a *tracking* attack. Its reticule was changed "to be more representative of the potential area of attack" ([v0.5.018i](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/4587497456699970846)).
- **Allaxiz (Chaos Bringer):** a self-buff, limited to his own attack and movement speed ([Arms of the Ascended](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5409450219988160528)).
- **General:** "Added minimum cooldown to most Lord of the Void skills, so at very high levels they will still use all of their skills instead of always prioritising the same skill" ([Winterfall](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5000716037440647402)). Bosses are "virtually immune to pushback effects" (same). Several updates enlarged boss indicators that were "slightly undersized" ([v0.9.028e](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/4789043687478223868); [v0.9.028c](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/6368680725440765344)).
- **Corrupted Void Hunters** (enemy versions of playable heroes) got longer charge times. Templar's Verdict and Holy Fire went from 1.2 s to 2.0 s. Obliterate and Shadow Spikes went from 1.2 s to 1.6 s ([Winterfall](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5000716037440647402)). These are the only concrete wind-up timings I found for the game. They suggest about 1.6–2.0 s is where the developer settled for a big boss hit inside a horde.

**The pattern: heavy ground telegraphs, tuned afterwards.** Most Soulstone boss changes are about matching the ground decal to the real hit area: indicators enlarged, hitboxes shrunk to the visual, tracking reticules redrawn. The lesson is that decal and hitbox must be the same asset, or at least checked against each other automatically.

### 1.3 Curses: the opt-in boss-density dial

Curses come in seven tiers, chosen before a Void Fields run, and each tier stacks on the last ([Void Fields § Curses](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Void_Fields)). Each curse point adds **+5% enemy health, +1% enemy damage, +0.4% enemy attack speed, +1% enemy move speed and +20% Lord health** (same page). Boss-relevant curses:

| Curse | Effect | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Warlords of the Void | Lords +10% health and +10 armour power | +1 |
| House of Lords | +1 Lord per match, Lords +5% health | +2 |
| Council of Lords | +1 Lord **active at the same time** (auto-enables House of Lords) | not found |
| Ethereal Lords (tier 7) | Dead Lords are "reborn in an ethereal invulnerable form and continue assisting the other lords until the whole group is eliminated" | +3 |
| Pillars of Despair / Protection | Destructible pillars that cast spells at you, or give enemies −20% damage taken per pillar | not found |
| Void Presence (tier 6) | "An invincible void presence slowly follows you, causing fatal damage if it's able to attack you" | +3 |
| Overwhelming Corruption (tier 6) | Corrupted Void Hunters "seek to collect your soulstones" | +2 |

Source for all rows: [Void Fields § Curses](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Void_Fields). Tier 7 needs 6 curse intensity, "making Ethereal Lords officially optional" ([A New Beginning LIVE](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5756237364326943757)). Elimination objectives "only start counting when all Lords of the Void are eliminated, to avoid multiple groups of Lords accumulating at the same time" ([A New Beginning](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5749481331437537172)). That is a hard rule against boss stacking, and only an opted-in curse breaks it.

**Void Presence: an invincible pursuer is a readability hazard.** The developers listed it as a top complaint: it "can sneak up on you under the chaos of the game and ruin a run in a way that feel unfair" ([Dev Talk #1](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5229301621580813946)). Their fixes were special visuals to set its attack apart from normal enemy attacks ([1 Year Anniversary](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5288974951222245503)), exemption from the opacity slider ([v0.9.028e](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/4789043687478223868)), and a damage area shrunk to the red circle ([Hotfix 0.10.036a](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5218041989047654996)). This is directly relevant if our arena has a "death" pursuer, a Reaper-style enforcer or a post-win hunter.

### 1.4 Final bosses

**The Void King** (1.0 headline boss, in The Netherworld). The wiki lists three phases with **62.5 bn / 75 bn / 100 bn HP** ([The Void King](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/The_Void_King)). Those totals are for the scaled Titan Hunt sigil. The page does not say which sigil they apply to.

Phase 1 (only phase documented): the King "remains seated on his throne" and casts:
- void pillars at the player's current position after a short delay;
- two laser beams from floating chained crystals that slowly follow the player;
- void vortexes that appear on the player and then move in a random direction;
- void rocks that rise from the ground across a large area (same page).

Phases 2 and 3 are undocumented on the wiki. The Titan Hunt page says he "grows increasingly aggressive across his three phases, introducing new abilities, overlapping AoEs, and environmental threats" ([Titan Hunt](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Titan_Hunt)).

A hotfix eight days after launch "Reduced effective health of the Void King, especially in the last stage of the fight". It also reduced arena coverage by the Corrupted Void Hunters ("smaller size, longer cooldown or in general would use less of the arena") and "Considerably reduced the effective health and area of effect of Shadow Lords of the Void" ([Hotfix 1.0g2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/1803527891486105)). The same hotfix fixed "ascensions and other objects could spawn inside the Void King in phase 1". A seated, stationary boss phase means pickups can spawn inside it. Launch notes also fixed summons and beam skills failing to damage him in stage 1 ([1.0 Release](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/1802354289729724)). A static throne phase breaks build types that need pathing or line of sight.

**Reward and victory.** The Void King drops the *Crown of the Void*: pick one of eight Void Powers, such as Presence of the Dark Lord (+50% damage, +40% cast frequency, +40% multicast chance) ([The Void King](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/The_Void_King)). Titans drop Titan Blood, which buys a Titanic Power. Corrupted Void Hunters drop the Flame of Corruption, which buys a Corrupted Power ([Titan Hunt](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Titan_Hunt)). In Soulstone **the boss reward is an in-run power spike, not only loot**. That matches our "great blessing" beats. Void Fields Lords give soulstones of a tier fixed by sequence: Corrupted, Vile, Wicked, Hateful, Rogue ([Void Fields](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Void_Fields)).

**Mh'thaeus, the Heretic** closes Unholy Cathedral. At Ritual of Heresy, Lords get **+700% extra health** (+800% at +2) ([Unholy Cathedral](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Unholy_Cathedral)). Mechanics: not found.

### 1.5 Scaling across weak and absurd builds

- **Endless and Overlord cycle scaling.** Enemy health goes +100%, +200%, +600%, +2,400%, +12,000%, then roughly ×6–×10 per cycle to +362,880,000% at cycle 10, and ×10 per cycle beyond. Damage reaches +1,100% at cycle 10 and then +400% per cycle. Armour is +700 at cycle 10, then +100 per cycle. **Capped:** attack speed +70%, movement speed +70%, body mass +4,000, armour penetration +50%, healing received −90% and absorption shields −90% ([Endless Mode](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Endless_Mode)). Overlord's cap on attack speed is +100% ([Overlord](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Overlord)). After a failed scaling rework in March 2024 the developer reverted curse and endless scaling but kept "limited scaling on Attack Speed and Movement Speed" ([A New Beginning LIVE](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5756237364326943757)).
- **Titan Hunt sigils** give bosses **+900% / +1,800% / +2,700% / +3,600%** extra health against +100% to +400% for regular enemies ([Titan Hunt](https://soulstone-survivors.fandom.com/wiki/Titan_Hunt)). Bosses scale about nine times faster than trash, which keeps the boss a real check when trash melts.
- **No time-linked HP** (lesson 1). Supply-pod-style burst tools exist in DRG:S. Soulstone has none.
- **Player-side complaint: fights that are too long or too short.** One Steam thread title reads "Void Lords are ruining the game for me…": "They have way to much heath, hit to hard, and cover the entire fighting area with to many AOE attacks." Replies give a 10–15 minute fight with a tanky build and a **90-minute Titan Hunt V fight** ([Steam, June 2025](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2066020/discussions/0/603034456245154349)). At the other end, a pre-1.0 endless player quit at wave 11 because "the HP scaling of the enemies was more or less exponential while my damage boosts were linear", and one wave took 12–15 minutes ([Steam, January 2024](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2066020/discussions/0/5829413348552209543)). Uncapped boss HP plus capped player growth gives fights measured in tens of minutes. **We need a ceiling on fight length:** a timed barrier like DMD's, phase gates, or a percentage-HP mechanic.

### 1.6 Readability: the clutter problem in detail

- The developer's own diagnosis lists four related complaints: "Endless Balance: How runs can become stale, where you are either immortal or instantly killed without being able to react"; "Game Visibility: How the game can be very hard to navigate with the spam of skills"; "Sneaky Void Presence"; and accidental portal entry ([Dev Talk #1](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5229301621580813946)).
- Fix 1, the **Special Effects Visibility slider**, with hotkeys ([1 Year Anniversary](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5288974951222245503)) and a first-time info card in Endless and Overlord ([Hammer Time update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/1808061939379909)).
- Fix 2, **exempt threats from the slider**. Enemy Void Hunter attacks are "mostly unaffected by visual effect opacity setting". Void Presence is unaffected. The elite Troll shockwave is "only partially" affected ([v0.9.028e](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/4789043687478223868)). The patch notes keep finding boss attacks that the slider faded by mistake: Mhyzahet's spikes, Aruanak's attacks, Gammuth's Fireball and Corrupted Void Hunter skills ([Hotfix 1.0g2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/1803527891486105); [Arms of the Ascended](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5409450219988160528)). **Lesson: tag every VFX as "player" or "threat" at authoring time. Never let a global fade touch "threat".**
- Fix 3, **a shader change so player VFX don't cover damage areas**. "Some of our skills VFX, more specifically Ice Vortex, Blizzard, Frozen Shard, Poison Puddle, Weaking Shot and Napalm Blast were overlapping with the damage areas… We've implemented a few changes to the shader code." Programmer Lucas: "Damage areas being overlapped by VFX has always been an issue of the game and it can make the game way more difficult than it should be" ([Devlog #2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/5969041959969620250)).
- Fix 4, **hide the player's own skill target circles in endless and Overlord**, because "they get redundant later on when you are pretty much blowing the whole map at once" ([Devlog #5](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2066020/view/6240387642459054660)).
- **Players still object to the slider approach.** In "Runs ending because I can't see" the original poster writes of "runs ending not because I made a mistake, but because I literally could not see the incoming attacks through my own attack fx". He adds that even at 25% visibility enemy indicators stay hidden, and that indicators should be overlaid, not underlaid. Another player warns that overlays clash with red-coloured chaos builds. Another likens the slider to a badly calibrated gamma default. Another says many players simply disable effects ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2066020/discussions/0/3591086730691827584)). A 1.0 review commenter: "you'll definitely need some of the options to tweak visibility and transparency in order to actually see all the enemy AOE attack areas on the ground" ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/06/soulstone-survivors-hits-1-0-bringing-crazy-blinding-action-with-a-huge-skill-tree/comment_id=280141)).
- **Colour design caveat.** Red is both the boss decal colour and a damage-type colour on the player side, per the player in the thread above. Reserve one hue, and ideally one shape language such as a hard rim plus a fill sweep, for enemy telegraphs only.

### 1.7 What players love and hate (Soulstone)

- Love: the boss reward is a big build spike (Titanic, Corrupted and Void Powers). Overlord is a pure boss mode for strong builds. Curses let players opt into multiple simultaneous Lords. Evidence of love is indirect: sales of over a million in Early Access and "Very Positive" from 20,000+ reviews ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/06/soulstone-survivors-hits-1-0-bringing-crazy-blinding-action-with-a-huge-skill-tree/comment_id=280141)). I found no thread praising a single named Lord.
- Hate: telegraphs hidden by the player's own VFX. Arena-filling AoE at endgame. Very long fights with damage-sponge HP. Endless bosses that attack "almost instant" (pre-cap). The invisible Void Presence. All cited above.

## 2. Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor (Funday Games / Ghost Ship Publishing; 1.0 on 17 September 2025)

### 2.1 Structure and timing within a dive

**Elimination** (the main mission) has five stages ([DRG:S Elimination](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Elimination)):

| Stage | Content | End condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Background trickle + 1 swarm wave | Elite(s) spawn → kill → drop pod |
| 2 | Trickle + 2 waves | Elite(s) → drop pod |
| 3 | Trickle + 3 waves | Elite(s) → drop pod |
| 4 | Trickle + 3 waves | Elite(s) → drop pod |
| 5 | Find and break **cocoons**: four Elites, then the Dreadnought cocoon | Dreadnought (50%) or Dreadnought Twins (50%) → gear drop → extraction |

Partway through each stage the player calls a Supply Pod to a landing zone that must first be cleared of rock. It carries a choice of three artifacts ([Elimination](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Elimination); [Supply Pod](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Supply_Pod)). The shop sits between stages. **Stage durations in minutes: not found.**

**The player controls the cocoon timing.** "If the player only fends off the background trickle of enemies, the Elites and the Dreadnought will eventually emerge from their cocoons on their own. However, players can accelerate this process by locating and manually tearing open the cocoons" ([Elimination](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Elimination)). Cocoons also burst automatically after "killing enough bugs" ([Objectives](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Objectives)). This is a neat hybrid between a fixed boss timer and a boss the player summons. A weak build can stall for XP; a strong build pulls the boss early. Steam advice for the Dreadnought follows this: "rush objectives and pop dreadnought early to have less enemies during the fight" (secondary: search summary of [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/6292161380820333006)).

**Other mission bosses:**
- **Escort Duty** (1.0): three stages. The last ends at the **Ommoran Heartstone**. The player must stay inside the Drilldozer's radius while it drills the shell. "After a certain amount of damage… it will begin to attack the Drilldozer using Ommoran Beamers. While the beamers are active, the Drilldozer will not be able to damage the Ommoran Shell. As such, the player must go out and mine the beamers with their pickaxe." When the shell breaks it "causes an explosion that instantly kills all active enemies" and drops gear and the Heartstone, which starts extraction ([Objectives](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Objectives); [Escort Duty](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Escort_Duty)). The design note: this mission "lends more weight to crowd control and less weight to making a build that can blast a boss down quickly" ([New Mission in 1.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/1809869180027645)). That is a deliberate boss for builds that cannot burst.
- **Egg Hunt** (Heavy Duty DLC, 30 April 2026): "Deliver the final egg, and everything changes. A Brood Nexus erupts on top of the pod, locking down your exit and triggering a multi-phase boss fight. It spawns relentless waves of enemies, and at key moments, creates Nexus Sprouts that must be destroyed before you can damage the core again" ([OUT NOW: Heavy Duty](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/1831432155567663)). HP and phase thresholds: not found.
- **Biome Masteries** use 10 stages with Dreadnoughts on stages 5 and 10 ([Masteries](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Masteries)). **Endless Mode** (April 2026): stages 1–10 are "shorter, with increased enemy density"; from stage 11 "Difficulty increases significantly"; artifacts are capped at 15 ([Coming Soon: Endless Mode](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/1830797770235497)). How bosses recur in endless: not found.

"Biome bosses": there are none in the boss sense. Biomes vary the **elites** instead. The Q'ronar Shellback appears only on Hazard 2+ in Crystalline Caverns, Magma Core and Salt Pits. The Warden is exclusive to Azure Weald ([Update 02](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5768625435410017639); [Creatures](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Creatures)).

### 2.2 The Dreadnought

**Stats** (wiki data page): base HP **16,500**; Hazard 5 scalar **×1.40**; contact damage 66; move speed 2.40; freeze and slow resist 0.90; knockback resist 1 (immune); mining damage 20; XP value 100; flagged "massive" ([Glyphid Dreadnought/Data](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Glyphid_Dreadnought/Data)). How hazard levels 2–4 interpolate: not found.

**Kit** (reworked in Update 02, May 2024; before that it had "one trick in its book: A jump/charge attack") ([Dreadnought Rework](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5766372999943095109)):
1. **Charge/leap.** A long straight-line leap for heavy damage. Tell: "it'll pause in place for a brief moment" before jumping ([GINX guide](https://www.ginx.tv/en/survivor-how-kill-dreadnought)). Players say "It stops and rears back for a good long second before the jump. Just start moving laterally when it does that" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/6292161380814310056)). The pause length in seconds: not found.
2. **Trembling Stomp.** "The Dreadnought roars, stomps, and creates volcanic spikes that shoot up from the ground… these spikes shoot out in a tight cone that traps you until you dig through or outrun them. This is probably a good idea if you don't want to be vulnerable to a charge attack follow-up" ([Dreadnought Rework](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5766372999943095109)). **This is the strongest use of terrain in the strand.** The boss writes temporary terrain that turns the game's mining verb into the dodge, and sets up its own combo. The roar is the audio tell.
3. **Swarm Bladder.** "A ranged projectile attack where the Dreadnought spits out a cocoon that bursts open and spawns a pack of Glyphids" (same source). The boss feeds the horde. Cocoon hatchlings include grunts, slashers and praetorians ([Creatures](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Creatures)).
4. Both new abilities "will be more deadly in higher hazard levels, just as the Dreadnought's health and damage scales accordingly" ([Dreadnought Rework](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5766372999943095109)). A press article described the rework as "an epic three-stage battle, where abilities change as the fight progresses" (secondary: search summary of [GodisaGeek](https://www.godisageek.com/2024/05/deep-rock-galactic-survivor-hoxxes-fights-back-update-adds-new-boss-and-enemies); the page returned 403 to me). Phase HP thresholds: not found.

**Arena edge cases.** The leap could carry it off the map and soft-lock the run. A fail-safe now kills the boss if it charges off-map ([Update 01](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5686430301714226692); [Hotfix 0.2.280d](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5842939267327369373)). The drop pod could also land on the supply pod and block the exit ([Update 01](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5686430301714226692)). Any charge in an open-ended arena needs a bounds clamp.

### 2.3 The Dreadnought Twins

Two smaller Dreadnoughts: the purple melee **Lacerator**, which charges and shares the leap, and the orange ranged **Arbalest**, which fires fireball spreads "while your attention is on its sibling". They have separate health bars and "heal each other if the difference in their remaining health becomes too large". "In higher hazard levels these abilities become more and more intense" ([The Dreadnought Twins](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5768625435392018331); [Creatures](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Creatures)).

Exact numbers from a hotfix: "The twin with most life should transfer **10% of its max HP** – the one with the lowest life should be healed for **20% of its max HP**." Each twin's base HP rose **from 9,000 to 9,500** to compensate for a bug fix ([Hotfix 0.2.280d](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5842939267327369373)). The HP-gap threshold that triggers the heal: not found for DRG:S. In the original DRG it is a 20% difference, with the twins immune during the share (secondary: search summary of [DRG wiki Twins](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Glyphid_Dreadnought_Twins)).

**Colour as role:** purple = melee, orange = ranged. That is a cheap, crowd-proof way to read two bosses at once. Both twins together total 19,000 base HP against 16,500 for the single Dreadnought (computed from the two sources above).

Opinion: in the parent game the Twins are "widely thought of as the worst type of dreadnoughts to face", because the heal punishes rushing and "you are wasting a LOT of ammo" (secondary: search summary of [DRG Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/548430/discussions/1/3124928124056940438)). I found no DRG:S-specific thread on the Twins' heal. Auto-firing weapons cannot split damage on purpose, so a heal-balance mechanic is harder to play around in a survivors game. **Treat this as a warning for our design** (inference).

### 2.5 Timers, enrage and rewards

- **Alien Threat Level** "increases by one every 60 seconds during boss battles" in Elimination ([Hazard](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Hazard)). The timer starts when the elites or the Dreadnought spawn ([Objectives](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Objectives)). It is a soft enrage that strengthens the swarm rather than the boss alone. In Escort Duty "the threat counter starts ticking from the beginning of each stage" ([New Mission in 1.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/1809869180027645)).
- **Kill-speed loot.** "End-of-level Elites now drop loot crates, the faster you kill them, the better the reward" ([Patch 4.2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/1795917897322495)). The tiers are Legendary, Epic, Rare, or none if too slow ([Objectives](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Objectives)). The 1.0 retrospective explains why: "In early EA, the optimal play was often to ignore the end-of-stage elite to farm mineral and XP, which felt backwards. Now, the faster you kill the boss, the better chest reward you earn, making for a tradeoff between chest reward and farming" ([1.0 OUT NOW](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/1811138915355931)). The developer bumped enemy HP about 5% on stages 2–5 to offset the power this added (Patch 4.2).
- **The Dreadnought drops a piece of Gear**, the 1.0 meta-loot system, then the drop pod comes ([Objectives](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Objectives)). The Ommoran's death clears the screen, a strong victory beat ([Objectives](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Objectives)). Camera and VFX of the kill: not found.
- Achievement: "Close Call – Kill the Dreadnought with less than 30 HP left" ([Update 01](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5686430301714226692)).

### 2.6 Extraction under pressure

After the elite dies, the drop pod lands with a **30-second** timer. Missing it ends the run (secondary: search summary of [Steam thread "PLEASE increase drop pod time"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/4518884696781910840); the thread itself says "the 30 seconds that you are given"). Opinion is split. For: "The timer serves as a limiter to how much farming you can do on a level. Without it players could get to levels of power that pretty much invalidate the Hazard difficulty." Against: "Holding an elitist attitude of git gud does not make an unfun mechanic any funner." The original poster blamed terrain between him and the pod ("punishingly stupid"). Others blamed move-speed penalties from items (same thread). Patch notes show the developer smoothing the edges: the pod pulling the player off the ramp at 0, black screens on auto-entry, and seeing through the pod when close ([Patch 4.2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/1795917897322495)). An artifact, the Tactical Cookie, heals 50% max HP on entering the pod ([Update 04](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/6212245217911949902)).

**Relevance:** if a night arena ends with "reach the ember gate", the post-boss dash is part of the boss fight. Terrain between the player and the exit must be clearable within the timer even for the slowest build.

### 2.7 How mining and terrain change boss fights

- Players pre-clear the arena: "Pre-clear terrain for movement space" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/6292161380820333006)); "Clear the boss fight area beforehand of enemies, terrain obstacles, and hazards" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/6292161380814310056)). The setup phase is part of the fight.
- Players stash healing: "Mine Red Sugar before the fight and leave it on the ground for emergency healing" ([GINX](https://www.ginx.tv/en/survivor-how-kill-dreadnought)).
- The **Supply Pod as a boss weapon.** Its impact "deals heavy damage". Players reported it taking about 50% of the Dreadnought's HP ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/6292161380820333006)). Patch 4.2 then capped pod damage to bosses at **33/30/27/24/20%** by hazard (elites 100/90/80/65/50%) ([Patch 4.2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/1795917897322495)). The esports promo jokes about "baiting a Dreadnought into a supply pod" ([esports post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/6212245217911854403)). **Players love an environmental trick that deals a percentage of boss HP**, and the developer kept it while scaling it down by difficulty. That suits our arenas: a shrine, falling bell or ember geyser that hurts the boss by percentage.
- The boss writes terrain (stomp spikes). Elites break terrain (Praetorian, Slasher, Shellback). Rock blocks your shots at the Elite Mactera ([Creatures](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivor:Creatures)).

### 2.8 Player opinion (DRG:S)

- On the jump: "That jump is some ♥♥♥♥, killing everything pretty easy runs going well only to get insta killed from a jumping dreadnaut." Players suggested "visual damage indicators (circular radius markers)" ([Steam, "Dreadnaut Boss Jump"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/6292161380814310056)).
- On difficulty: once trash is handled, "the Dreadnaught is a piece of cake, even if you have low single target damage, as it is very easy to dodge when you don't have to worry about nearby enemies" (secondary: search summary of the same threads). The boss is hard mainly through the crowd around it.

Advice: keep at least +10% movement speed to dodge the jump, prioritise weapon upgrades, "It's only about damn RNG" ([Steam, "Tips for killing the Dreadnought?"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/6292161380820333006)).
- I found no rigorous review quote calling the boss anticlimactic. The developer's own reasoning for the rework ("to not make the Dreadnought encounter feel like 'the simple one'") suggests the one-move version was seen as thin ([Dreadnought Rework](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2321470/view/5766372999943095109)).

The closest genre match to our 15:00 blessing and 30:00 boss: **three tent-poles in 20 minutes, plus mini-hordes in between**.

### 3.2 Arena and horde rules: the most transferable systems

- **Spawn suppression:** "While a Boss is alive, normal time-based Minion spawns do not occur. They immediately resume upon the boss' death" ([Monster](https://dmd.fandom.com/wiki/Monster)). The boss's own summons are the only adds, so every enemy on screen during the fight belongs to the boss's pattern.
- **Barrier arenas:** waves "sometimes also have a Barrier that traps the player within until they defeat a certain enemy, or which will release players after a certain duration even if the enemy is still alive" ([Waves](https://dmd.fandom.com/wiki/Waves)). A player confirms the box "disappears after a while if you don't kill the boss" ([Steam, "extreme rate of bosses"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/4036978698783342049/)). This gives a weak build a way out: the boss is not beaten, but the run goes on. It also caps fight length for everyone. The final boss is the exception: "Lasts until Dracula is killed" ([Waves](https://dmd.fandom.com/wiki/Waves)).
- **Keeping bosses in the arena** took several patches: "Bosses should not be able to go outside of arenas now"; "Bosses now try to stay inside the arena"; "Fixed Act 2 bosses not teleporting inside black fire arena"; "Fixed ranger not being able to shoot at the top of the boss arenas" ([Patch 0.8.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1798454487617422); [Procedural Horde](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/6931694080290932695); [Patch 0.8.5](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1800357164415339)).
- **Boss health bar in the middle of the screen** ([Monster](https://dmd.fandom.com/wiki/Monster)).
- **Map shrines as boss tools.** A totem that doubles damage, physical shrines whose pillars block fireballs. Players time them for the 7:00 boss ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/3975051362887106827); [Steam, Necromancer](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/4036978698789384741)). Map-object buffs were raised (attack damage 2.4% → 8%, heals 3% → 10%) in 0.6.27 ([Patch 0.6.27](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/6560117310267631145)). This is the DMD counterpart of DRG:S's supply-pod hit: an arena feature that turns the fight.

### 3.3 Telegraphs and patterns

- Telegraphs are mainly ground shapes. The Hands' attacks are "signaled beforehand, for example, in the form of a red line on the ground" ([GamePressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/death-must-die-bosses/z2665a)). The Frog King's slam shows a red circle, but the window is "extremely short" and must be read from the sprite wind-up ([Act 3 feedback](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/596273605315164244/)).
- **I-frame dashing through projectiles** is the intended counter: "you can dodge through the fireballs" ([Steam, Necromancer](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/4036978698789384741)). The Frog King "cannot be dashed through": "why you would rework a ton of skills to require this mechanic, and then you just make them completely negated by the first boss in the new act" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/596273145166509317/)).
- Wind-ups added after complaints: "Wraith Dash now has a windup animation" ([Patch 0.8.4](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1799088287878405)). "Boss projectile attacks no longer hit through divine shield or ignore evade" ([Patch 0.6.55](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/5382426458974679179)).
- Reviews summarised by Vaporlens and elsewhere mention "a lack of sufficient telegraphing of enemy attacks" and "unavoidable attacks, excessive speed" (secondary: search summary; the Vaporlens page I fetched did not contain these lines, see [Vaporlens](https://vaporlens.app/app/2334730/death_must_die.md)).
- **Darkness and lighting** affect readability in Act 3. "Most Enemies now have a light source to stand out in the darkness"; "Decreased Act 3 global darkness so areas with 0 light sources won't be pitch black" ([Patch 0.8.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1798454487617422); [Patch 0.8.5](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1800357164415339)). This bears directly on a dark-fantasy night arena: enemies, and above all the boss and its telegraphs, need their own light.
- **Behaviour trees.** Bosses use composite "sequence" and "weighted random" nodes, condition nodes such as HP below 40%, and action nodes with timeouts ([Act II Dev Log #1](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/5679673637664276438)). Act 1's boss logic was "hard coded and thus hard to extend", which prompted the rewrite (same). This is a Godot-friendly pattern for our bosses: data-defined ability tables plus a small tree.

### 3.4 The gods' blessings and how they bear on bosses

- Blessings come from up to four gods per run. The Moirai are fixed and three are free choices. Gods unlock by cumulative run time: Summer at 0 min, Krom at 5, Mort and the Moirai at 10, Winter at 15, Leigong at 20, Lady Justice at 25, Time at 30, Ninh at 40 ([DMD wiki: God](https://dmd.fandom.com/wiki/God)).
- Boss-relevant blessings: **Temporal Lord** (Time) rewinds 60 s once on death. Players found it underwhelming ("nothing really changes") and hard to notice, and asked for "a glowing aura" or "a dramatic sound cue" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/4137187221847001348/?l=english)). Advice for the 7:00 boss names Lifelink, Lightning Bolt, Time Field and single-target picks such as Mort's Soulstealers ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/3975051362887106827)).
- **Bosses that counter blessings.** The Lady's charm on summons was cut from 20% to 7%. Regular sirens went from 15% to 1% charm chance and from 8 s to 5 s, and summons gained charm immunities. The developer: "the original idea was to have one or two waves in a run that can pose a greater challenge to summon builds, but in practice those waves show up way more often and make summon builds unviable in act 3" ([Act III Hotfix](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1797820624532108)). The fix the developer favours later: "maybe an enemy channel-controls your summon and you can kill it to free it" ([Act 3 feedback](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/596273605315164244/)).
- The **Act 4 god rework** removes slots, adds cross-god synergies and lets players "choose your god after combat", with "bad RNG protection" ([Act 4 News](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1840944183787477)). This bears on our great blessings at 0:00 and 15:00. DMD found that fishing for a specific god "predetermines the run before it is even started" ([Roadmap News](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1816849002015682)).

### 3.5 Difficulty: Darkness and modifiers

- **Darkness** is a numbered difficulty, 0–30, raised in steps of 10 (once 5), for better loot. **Darkness 100** arrived with Act II "for those players that truly want to test how far their builds can go", while "Darkness 30 will still give max item drops". It lets Darkness 30 players pick their own modifiers. The AI refactor allows making enemies "faster and smarter, instead of just adding more hp and damage" ([Dev Log #5](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/5756237364320207330); [Patch 0.7.13](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/5762994032406868290)).
- Concrete boss-side modifier numbers, by patch:
  - Boss Life per modifier level: 20% → 40% ([0.6.27](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/6560117310267631145)), then 40% → 35% (max 120% → 105%) ([0.6.48](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/5382426458970207889)).
  - "No Heal replaced with +2 Elites & Bosses Projectile Count" ([0.6.27](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/6560117310267631145)), later replaced by "Less Pick Up drops" ([0.8.4](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1799088287878405)).
  - Selectable Boss Cooldowns −300% → −200% ([0.8.4](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1799088287878405)).
  - Darkness 100 monster life scaling "From 170% > 1360% to 170% > 340% over the course of the 20 minutes"; damage "From 50% > 133% to 25% > 66%", to "support more builds & playstyles" ([0.8.4](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1799088287878405)).
  - Boss resistances: "Vampire Lord % Resistance Increased (90% > 95%)"; "Other Bosses % Resistance Increased (80% > 90%)" ([0.6.27](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/6560117310267631145)). Reading the source line literally, this is probably status or crowd-control resistance; "Boss stun status resistance 65% > 50%" appears in [0.6.45](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/6560117310279978839). The exact stat: not verified.
  - "Enrage no longer triggered by negative hp regen" ([0.7.25](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/5759616966679765502)). So an enrage exists and is triggered by HP-based conditions. Trigger thresholds: not found.
- **Overtuning post-mortem.** Act III elite-minion subtypes were "added very close to the release… the act became very overtuned. We also don't track any metrics on what gear most players have". The fix was −30% enemy health, −20% enemy damage and +100% item drops ([Act III Hotfix](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1797820624532108)). Act 2 drew the same complaint: players with only Act 1 gear could not pass "the 13-minute boss checkpoint" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/4357871582654485912/)). A boss tuned against assumed gear fails silently for players whose gear is worse.
- **Pact of Greed** after beating Act 2: replay Act 1 at Act 2 difficulty for Tier 2 rewards ([Procedural Horde Patch](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/6931694080290932695)). This reuses old bosses as harder content cheaply.
- Endless or infinite mode is still only planned for Act 4 ("the much requested infinite mode") ([Roadmap News](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/1816849002015682)).

### 3.6 Rewards and victory

Bosses drop items plus **Cosmic Essence**, raised in October 2024 from 4 to 8 per boss in Act 1 and from 8 to 12 in Act 2 ([Procedural Horde Patch](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/6931694080290932695)). Essence is spent in the Constellarium talent tree, "up to 36 essence for each hero" ([Triple-i Announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/5762993398269898423)). Boss drop-rate bonuses from difficulty were cut 12% ([0.6.45](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2334730/view/6560117310279978839)). Victory VFX, camera and sound: not found.

### 3.7 Player opinion (DMD)

- **The Lady (Act 3 final), loved:** "the epitome of a great boss"; "so fun because you can dance with her"; offers "different rotations" at range and in melee ([Act 3 feedback](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/596273605315164244/)).
- **Frog King, hated by some:** short slam window, high aggression, and not dash-through-able, which hurts specific builds ([Act 3 feedback](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/596273605315164244/); [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/596273145166509317/)).
- **Scarecrow:** the developer (Ren) thought it the hardest. Players note "constant ad spam" but "much more forgiving" telegraphs than the Frog King (Act 3 feedback).
- **Dracula:** "undoubtedly the most difficult opponent" of Act 1 ([GamePressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/death-must-die-bosses/z2665a)).
- **The Necromancer:** "a low-difficulty boss meant for newer players"; learnable by dodging fireballs and using pillar cover ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/4036978698789384741)).
- **Boss frequency:** "How in the hell are you supposed to deal with the extreme rate of bosses?" was answered with the 7/14/20 cadence and the timed box. The consensus blamed gear, not spawn rate ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2334730/discussions/0/4036978698783342049/)).
