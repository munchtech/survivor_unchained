# E2 — Boss fights in Path of Exile 1, Path of Exile 2 and Last Epoch

Strand: isometric ARPG bosses. Key question throughout: what makes a boss mechanic work from a top-down camera, amid hundreds of the player's own effects, for builds ranging from weak to absurd?

Source notes. The current community PoE1 wiki (poewiki.net) and PoE2 wiki (poe2wiki.net) blocked automated access (Cloudflare / Anubis), so PoE1 monster statistics come from the older Fandom wiki through its MediaWiki API. That wiki stopped being the main community wiki around 2021, so its numbers may be from older patches. Mechanics and phase thresholds were cross-checked against Maxroll guides, which are kept up to date. Reddit could not be reached (rate-limited). Player opinion therefore comes from the official PoE forum, Steam discussions and the Last Epoch forum. "(secondary)" marks a claim seen only in a search-result summary.

## 1. Summary of lessons for Survivor Unchained

1. **Name and voice the killing blow.** PoE's most famous one-shots have a fixed voice line just before them: Sirus shouts "DIE!" before his Die Beam ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Sirus,_Awakener_of_Worlds)); the Maven's memory game starts with "SCURRY, SCURRY!" and her tri-beam with "STAND STILL!" ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-maven-boss-guide)); the Searing Exarch calls "Disintegration!" and "Annihilation!" ([search summary of Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-searing-exarch-boss-guide), secondary). In a screen full of effects, sound is the one channel the player's build cannot drown out. Survivor Unchained's 30:00 boss should have a short spoken or musical sting tied to each lethal attack.
2. **Use one colour for "you cannot tank this".** PoE2 marks attacks that cannot be blocked or evaded with a red flash on the boss and an audio cue during the wind-up ([poe2wiki summary](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Blockable), secondary). GGG still uses the convention in recent patches ("Cyclone is now unblockable (red flash)", [0.5.2 notes via patched.gg](https://patched.gg/games/path-of-exile-2/052-patch-notes)). The colour should be reserved for that meaning and never reused for decoration.
3. **Make safety a shape on the floor, not a stat check.** The isometric fights that read best show the answer as a shape: Zana's bubble against bullet hell ([Fandom Shaper](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/The_Shaper)), the inner ring of the Elder's Ring of Death ([Fandom Uber Elder](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Uber_Elder)), Sirus's maze exits marked by flares, the Arbiter of Ash's Flame Seed circle that is safe *inside* ([poe-vault](https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/the-arbiter-of-ash-boss-guide)), and the Maven's three arena zones. Showing "where to stand" reads better than showing "where not to stand" once the floor is busy.
4. **Lingering ground hazards turn a fight into a timer, and players hate them when they cannot be seen.** Shaper vortices last 25 s and phase 3 "potentially makes most of the arena unsafe" ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/The_Shaper)). Uber Maven's void zones persist "for the entire encounter" ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-maven-boss-guide)). Last Epoch's Aberroth is criticised for "countless DoT Puddles and no-go zones" ([LE forum](https://forum.lastepoch.com/t/aberroth-melee-nightmare/74332)). A lingering hazard can work as a soft enrage, but only if it stays readable under the horde and the player's own effects. A survivors game should keep at most one kind of persistent hazard and fade the player's effects over it.
5. **Use health gates, not raw HP, to stop absurd builds skipping the fight.** PoE uses invulnerable intermissions at fixed thresholds (Sirus at 75/50/25%, Uber Elder swapping invulnerability every 25%). Last Epoch 1.1 replaced an adaptive damage-reduction system with "ward breakpoints": at each health breakpoint the boss gains ward that decays over time ([Maxroll 1.1 notes](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/news/1-1-patch-notes)). PoE2 added emergence damage reduction that fades over time and fades faster once the boss moves ([poe-vault](https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/news/path-of-exile-2-introduces-anti-burst-boss-mechanics)). Each of these guarantees the player sees some of the fight however strong the build is, while a strong build still finishes much sooner.
6. **Uber versions are a cheap, popular way to stretch the endgame.** PoE's uber pinnacles add a flat "Monsters take 70% less damage" plus a few twists, such as extra projectiles, tracking beams and persistent zones ([Maxroll Sirus](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/sirus-awakener-of-worlds-boss-guide), [Maxroll Maven](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-maven-boss-guide)). Last Epoch's Uber Aberroth adds Harbinger adds and a heal to full at phase 5 ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/monolith/aberroth-boss-guide)). This suits the endless post-30:00 mode in Survivor Unchained: a re-summoned or "uber" night boss with a damage-taken multiplier and one or two added mechanics.
7. **Bosses that use the horde work well in this genre.** Xesht's fight starts with a run through a breach horde where "killing monsters adds to a timer", and the boss chance is lost if the timer hits zero ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/bosses/xesht)). The Shaper and Uber Elder spawn add waves at thresholds that refill flasks ([Maxroll Uber Elder](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-uber-elder-boss-guide)). Atziri's adds walk to her and heal her ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Atziri,_Queen_of_the_Vaal)). Making the horde a resource or a threat, not filler, ties the boss to the survivors loop.
8. **Players accept deaths they can learn from and hate deaths they cannot read.** Jonathan Rogers: "if you expect to die in a boss fight the first time as you'll learn it, then that's actually okay" ([Maxroll interview](https://maxroll.gg/poe/news/pax-west-path-of-exile-2-interview-with-jonathan-rogers)). The loudest PoE2 complaints are about deaths whose cause could not be seen: builds "make it literally impossible to see" mechanics ([PoE forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3906258)), and there is "no audio clue" for some lethal effects ([PoE forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3643563)). Losing the attempt and its loot on one death (PoE2 Citadels, the Arbiter before 0.1.1) caused the most anger. GGG later gave the Arbiter respawn attempts ([0.1.1 notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3695606)).
9. **A boss that is too easily read is forgettable.** Players called the Eater of Worlds "the 'easiest endgame boss in the game'" because of "slow, well telegraphed moves" that leave "way too much of a dps window" ([PoE forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3235325)). Telegraphs need to be clear but short. Difficulty should come from overlapping clear patterns, as in Arbiter phase 2 and Uber Elder, not from hidden ones.

### 2.2 Atziri, Queen of the Vaal (2014) — the original "boss as a puzzle box"

- **Normal phase:** built-in damage reflect (2% of physical and elemental damage taken) and curse reflection, so a careless high-damage build hurts itself ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Atziri,_Queen_of_the_Vaal)). Storm Call hits for 7,319–21,958 lightning, with a base duration of 3 s before it lands (2.5 s and 10,813–32,438 when empowered) (same).
- **Add phase:** she shields herself and summons minions that "walk towards her and heal her when they reach her". The minions do not attack. The wiki calls it "by far the safest phase and a good time for recovering flask charges" (same). Here the horde is the threat to the boss's health bar, not to the player, which is a clean pattern to borrow for a survivors boss.
- **Split phase:** at 75%, 50% and 25% she splits into four copies. One must be killed to make her vulnerable again. One copy holds a mirror and "reflects 100% of physical and elemental damage taken"; attacking it with direct damage "is consequently fatal". If her minions heal her back above a threshold, the split does not repeat (same). This is a build-check expressed as a target choice: the player must recognise the right copy in the crowd.
- **Rhythm:** her Flameblast "is always cast on top of the player (with the same cast time and delay), it is essential for players to get used to the rhythm" (same). Fixed cast times teach players a beat, and the beat carries over between attempts.
- **Reward:** Normal Atziri always drops one of a fixed set of uniques. Uber Atziri always drops one of nine Atziri's Splendour variants (same).

### 2.3 The Shaper — phases, corridor runs and a protective NPC

- **Attacks** ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/The_Shaper)):
  - **Shaper Beam:** aimed at the player and "once fired, the beam does NOT rotate". It lasts 3 s and deals 5,384 fire + 4,405 cold + 6,057 lightning per second; it "can kill most builds in 1-2 seconds". The rule (aim, lock, fire) is fair from a top-down view because the line is visible and fixed.
  - **Teleport slam:** "The Shaper disappears into the void, and teleports to the player and slams", dealing 300% damage. The counter is "constant movement". The telegraph is the boss *vanishing*, which still reads in a crowd because the largest object on screen disappears.
  - **Vortex balls:** slow seeking orbs. On contact they create a vortex that deals heavy cold damage over time and lasts 25 s. Players "must attract the balls to the edges of the arena". This is player-placed clutter: the player decides where the arena shrinks.
  - **Monster portal:** the Shaper becomes immune and spawns monsters that attack Zana. If she dies she cannot cast her bubble for a while.
  - **Bullet hell:** the Shaper vanishes, fills the arena with purple projectiles (5,639–8,458 cold each), and "the only way to escape it is to stay within Zana's protective red bubble". A red dome is the most readable possible safe zone.
- **Phases:** damage him to 50%, after which he heals to 60% and must be brought to 50% again. He then banishes Zana and the player to a corridor of floating islands, with the Uncreated at the end. He "heals to 60–100% health depending on how long you took to return". Phase 2 ends at 25% (corridor with the Unshaped). Phase 3 heals him to "50–100%" and adds a clone that copies his attacks (same). The corridor is a pacing break: the player fights the horde, catches their breath, and is punished for slowness by the heal.
- **Escalation by phase:** vortex frequency rises each phase. In phase 3 it "potentially makes most of the arena unsafe and leaves very little room for maneuvering" (same). This is a soft enrage made of accumulated hazards.
- **Reward:** always drops a Shaper unique plus an Uber Elder fragment (same).
- **Why it is remembered:** the voice acting (Daryl Habraken), Zana's pleading, and the "To me exile, quickly!" call that announces the bubble (same). The call is another sound-first telegraph.

### 2.4 Uber Elder — two kits at once, with invulnerability swaps as the gate

- **Structure:** "One of the bosses will be invulnerable, alternating between the two for every 25% health lost… When both bosses are at 25% health, both bosses will be vulnerable." Volatile Anomalies and Madness Propagators "continuously spawn during the fight, alternating every 20 seconds" ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Uber_Elder)). Maxroll describes seven phases: boss phases at 100–75, 75–50, 50–25 and 25–0, separated by Elder intermissions (4 Null Portals) and a Shaper intermission (a monster portal). Intermission adds "restore flask charges when defeated" ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-uber-elder-boss-guide)). The invulnerable boss is "marked by a distinct silver glow" (same). That is the readability rule for a two-boss fight: one global colour says "don't shoot this one".
- **The only one-shot:** the Elder's Expanding Nova / Ring of Death. A ring grows, then explodes for 5,420–6,625 physical outside it; "players must stay within the inner ring", and the wiki notes "it is the only one-shot mechanic" ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Uber_Elder)). The uber version "casts 3 instances of his Ring of Death ability that trigger in quick succession" ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-uber-elder-boss-guide)).
- **Self-inflicted clutter:** players "must be very careful to place the Shaper's Vortex portals around the edges of the arena failing which the entire arena will [be] littered" ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Uber_Elder)). Vortices persist for the whole fight ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-uber-elder-boss-guide)).
- **Split arena:** I could not verify the "split arena" in the brief from any source I could reach. The Fandom page and Maxroll describe one shared arena with alternating invulnerability. Treat the split arena as unconfirmed.
- **Reward:** can drop the Watcher's Eye with three mods, "potentially hundreds of Exalted Orbs" ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Uber_Elder)).

### 2.5 Sirus, Awakener of Worlds — the Die Beam, storms and the meteor maze

- **Thresholds:** "4 Phases. At 75%, 50% and 25% Health, Sirus goes into an intermission Phase." In each intermission he "teleports to the center of the arena circle and spawns a Deatomization Storm" that chases the player, and the player triggers the next phase "by moving beneath Sirus". In phase 4 he "restores his Health back to 75%" ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/sirus-awakener-of-worlds-boss-guide)). The Fandom version history notes he heals to 75% (from 50%) at Awakening level 8 ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Sirus,_Awakener_of_Worlds)).
- **Arena as enrage:** storms "deal massive damage… Around Sirus is a large animated ring which indicates a safety zone". A storm spawns at each threshold "or if the player is outside of the safety zone for too long" (same). Meteor Strike and the geysers fire "many more" projectiles if the player is outside the safety zone (same). The arena shrinks in steps, so a faster kill means a bigger arena. A survivors boss could shrink the arena the same way in response to time, not damage.
- **Die Beam:** "Sirus fires 3 beams that deal a huge amount of damage which can one-shot most builds. The only way to avoid this is to listen for the dialogue and move away from the current position… Before casting this, Sirus says 'DIE!'" Each beam carries one element (corrupted blood, ignite, freeze or shock) (same). In phase 4 he "fires 4 beams that track your position" ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/sirus-awakener-of-worlds-boss-guide)). The fix is simple and teachable: hear the word, then move. The Multi Clone variant fires *mid-sentence*: "Feel the thrill of <fires> THE VOID!" ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Sirus,_Awakener_of_Worlds)). The voice line works as a countdown.
- **Meteor Maze:** Sirus "summons all players… to the centre of the maze. The player must escape from each layer before Sirus collapses the maze." Exits are "placed at a random location in the circle indicated by animated flares". Failing costs 16,344–24,517 (two rings) or 21,793–32,689 (three or more rings) (same). Rings grow by phase: 1, 2, 3 ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/sirus-awakener-of-worlds-boss-guide)). This top-down navigation test does not depend on DPS, which is why it equalises builds.
- **Corridor Blast:** Sirus builds walls into a corridor and fires slow projectiles down it. He "can be interrupted by moving towards and through him" ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Sirus,_Awakener_of_Worlds)). Rewarding the player for running *toward* the threat is a nice inversion.
- **Difficulty dial:** Awakening level gives "A0 Sirus" to "A9 Sirus"; players can unsocket watchstones for an easier fight "albeit with lower-tier drops" (same). Difficulty is a dial the player sets in exchange for reward.
- **Uber Sirus:** "Monsters take 70% less damage"; "The Die Beam ability tracks the player in all phases with full power"; the final phase uses a six-beam rotating pattern ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/sirus-awakener-of-worlds-boss-guide)).
- **Shattering arena:** a collapsing or shattering floor in the final phase is not described in either source I could read. Not found.

### 2.6 The Maven — the memory game and the Crucible

- **The Crucible (invitations):** after the Maven witnesses three map-boss kills she invites the player to fight those bosses *simultaneously*, then four, five, six and finally ten. Area levels are 73/75/78/80/83 ([Fandom](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/The_Maven%27s_Crucible)). Spawns are staggered: "Endgame invitations spawn 2 monsters at once, then the other 2 after 20 seconds or if any are killed early" (same). Strong builds pull the next wave in sooner, while weak builds are not overwhelmed. That staggering transfers directly to a survivors boss-rush. While witnessing a boss, the Maven can buff it (clone, extra mitigation, speed, a heal) or debuff the player with orbs ([Fandom Maven](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/The_Maven)).
- **GGG on the Crucible:** Chris Wilson: "3, 4, 5, 6… And the 10 one can be farmed. That's the one that is a item that you can craft mods onto". When base scaling plateaus, "mods start to kick in on the invitation items". GGG worked on "making it so the bosses can work anywhere so that you could just drop a Kitava on The Coast" ([Echoes Q&A transcript](https://pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3008244)). Bosses built to work in any arena is a requirement if Survivor Unchained wants to drop day-side story bosses into night arenas.
- **Maven fight:** seven phases alternating Maven (each 100→0) and the Brain / Nucleus (100→66, 66→33, 33→0) ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-maven-boss-guide)).
- **Memory game:** "SCURRY, SCURRY!" The arena splits into three zones (left, top, right). She lights a pattern of 3 zones (phase 3) or 6 (phases 5 and 7). The player must "repeat her pattern by walking into the zones in the order they were lit up" while an expanding blast channels. "Every wrong step… heavily slows you down and increases the speed of the blast's expansion" (same). Search summaries add that zones flash green when correct and red when wrong, and that the blast is 3–6 converted hits ([vhpg](https://www.vhpg.com/?p=51036), secondary). Three large zones are readable from the isometric camera at any effect density. The game punishes errors with a slow and a faster timer instead of instant death.
- **Escape hatch, and a design smell:** Maxroll advises: "If you are unable to do the Memory Game, simply logout and re-join the encounter. You have a 60 second buff that makes you invulnerable" (same). When an exploit is the standard advice, the mechanic has failed for part of the audience.
- **Uber Maven:** +4 projectiles, "Void Zones from Volatile Orbs persist for the entire encounter", 70% less damage taken, and she "begins the fight fully empowered" (same).

### 2.7 The Searing Exarch and the Eater of Worlds (3.17, 2022)

- **Exarch:** above 75% a basic kit (flame walls, mortars, Searing Runes). Below 75% the "Lantern Explosion – 'Incineration!'" is added. Below 50% come the rolling meteor walls ("The fires of a thousand suns!"), during which the boss is invulnerable and "the Maven assists by shooting at meteors… creating random gaps". "The lower the bosses' health, the more Meteor Walls spawn." Minions, totems and Frost Wall can body-block meteors. Runes apply Cauterized Flesh (−5% maximum fire resistance and 50% reduced travel-skill cooldown recovery for 10 s), and standing on a rune "prevents… recovery" ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-searing-exarch-boss-guide)). Uber: 70% less damage taken, 2 additional projectiles, a Molten Minotaur at each meteor phase, "16 lines of Meteors" (same). Rune debuffs that cut mobility are an example of punishing the player's escape tools instead of their health.
- **Eater of Worlds:** above ~90%, only tentacle slams (3 tentacles at the player's position). Below 90% the beams and slams are added. Below 75% "he regularly switches to" the doom phase: "If you fail to activate the Spheres within ~20 seconds, the Eater of Worlds yells 'All will be devoured!' and releases a massive, lethal explosion." Horror Orbs drown the player: "After 3 seconds of drowning, your Character dies" ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-eater-of-worlds-boss-guide)). The ~20 s sphere task is a localised enrage: a small objective with a voiced fail state.
- **Player reception:** one forum poster ranked "Black Star > Searing Exarch > Infinite Hunger > Eater of Worlds". They called the Exarch "Really good boss design, but a fairly simple fight" and the Eater "the 'easiest endgame boss in the game'" because of "slow, well telegraphed moves" ([PoE forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3235325)). When the uber versions arrived, another player wrote "if you don't have mirror+ tier build which can instakill… you have no chance". They called the Exarch "screen wide instadeath" and complained that Uber Shaper has "NO DEFENCE BUBBLE" ([PoE forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3267851)). Normal is too legible and uber is too lethal, and the gap between the two versions is the complaint.

### 2.8 GGG design statements (PoE1 and cross-game)

- Uber pinnacles share one lever: an area modifier "Monsters take 70% less damage" ([Maxroll Sirus](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/sirus-awakener-of-worlds-boss-guide), [Maxroll Maven](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-maven-boss-guide), [Maxroll Exarch](https://maxroll.gg/poe/bosses/the-searing-exarch-boss-guide)) plus mechanic tweaks.
- Lead designer Rory Rackham discussed uber boss design on Shacknews E6 2022, but the article page holds no transcript ([Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/130937/shacknews-e6-2022-path-of-exile-uber-boss-deep-dive-exilecon)). Quotes not found.
- Jonathan Rogers on why top builds make boss mechanics moot: "At the top end of players, you get this absolute ridiculousness with 600 projectiles a second… You just can't see f***ing anything, so you don't have any ability to respond to boss mechanics." He still wants players to feel "like you still broke it" ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/path-of-exile-2/combat)). This is the central tension for Survivor Unchained too.

## 3. Path of Exile 2 (early access from December 2024)

### 3.1 Philosophy: Souls-influenced, with the dodge roll as the base verb

- **Dodge roll:** "no cooldown and no restrictions"; combat "will be a little bit slower"; stun and freeze build-up is "a little bit like Poise from games like Elden Ring, though the meters tend to be a lot smaller" (Jonathan Rogers, [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/path-of-exile-2/combat)).
- **Deaths as teaching** (Rogers, [Maxroll PAX West interview](https://maxroll.gg/poe/news/pax-west-path-of-exile-2-interview-with-jonathan-rogers)):
  - "I think it's important that players die early to get them used to the idea that dying is fine."
  - "The number of times you should die to a boss is like two times before you're able to fight it, and that's for every boss in the game."
  - "All the boss mechanics are all avoidable. They're all telegraphed, you can dodge, roll out of the way if you want to."
  - "Even if you have bad stats, you can still get through the boss fight with just like learning the pattern."
- Rogers on build variance: "A boss that felt easy with one class might be brutal with another" (Dropped Frames podcast as summarised by [poe-vault](https://poe-vault.com/poe2/news/poe2-dev-talks-bosses-builds-and-what-comes-next)).

### 3.2 Telegraph conventions

- **Red flash = unblockable / unevadable:** "some abilities that are used by bosses will be preceded by an animation in the form of a red flash and an audio cue" ([poe2wiki search summary](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Blockable), secondary). The 0.5.2 notes confirm the convention: Siora's "Cyclone is now unblockable (red flash) but damage is reduced across all skills and wind-ups are longer" ([patched.gg 0.5.2](https://patched.gg/games/path-of-exile-2/052-patch-notes)). A Steam player summed up the deal: "There are some 1 shot mechanics, but they usually have a long windup with the boss glowing red" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2694490/discussions/0/594008890765594377/)).
- **Friction:** "Some monsters have blockable hits where they punch you, but unblockable slams where they punch the ground under you." The same poster argues this makes "glass cannon… by far the most effective way to play" ([PoE forum](https://pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3759881)). If the defensive stats do not apply to the attacks that kill, players stop building defence and race to kill instead.

### 3.3 Act bosses

| Boss | Thresholds | Notable telegraphs and mechanics |
|---|---|---|
| Count Geonor (Act 1) | Wolf form at ~50%; harder at 25% | "Requiem on High": ice shards "marked by pillars of bluish light"; mist "shrinks the arena while spawning werewolves"; at 25% faster, red-coloured bolts that cause bleeding. Called "a skill check at the end of Act 1" ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/path-of-exile-2/count-geonor-boss)) |
| Jamanra, the Abomination (Act 2) | Abilities unlock at 90/85/80%; phase 2 at 65%; then 60/40/20/15% | Each threshold adds one tool: rotating beacon rings, a sandstorm where you hide behind a shield, lightning walls (max 2), portals at 20% and 15%. Two attacks are labelled "Requires Dodge" ([poe-vault](https://poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/act-2-boss-guide)). Life lowered by about 17% in 0.5.2 ([patched.gg](https://patched.gg/games/path-of-exile-2/052-patch-notes)) |
| Doryani (Act 3) | Phase 1 to 45% (adds at 90%); mech phase with adds at 90/70/60/40% | At 60% the pilot detaches and orbits the arena edge; a "Lightning Cage" around the mech is safe *inside*; at 40% attacks turn into red "Vaal" variants ([poe-vault](https://poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/act-3-boss-guide)) |

What works: fine-grained thresholds (every 5–10%) that add one mechanic at a time teach the kit in layers, and the fight becomes a list the player masters. Jamanra's "hide behind the shield" sandstorm is a cover mechanic that reads instantly from above. Geonor's mist literally shrinks the arena while adding werewolves, a combined arena-and-horde escalation that suits a survivors boss. Player sentiment on act bosses is mixed. Some players report "I needed help on a ton of the bosses through the campaign. The difficulty of the levels vs the bosses isn't even close" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2694490/discussions/0/598521781543791291/)); others say "it's not hard it's just right" (same thread).

### 3.4 Endgame pinnacles

- **Arbiter of Ash:** 5.04 million life (uber 22.6 million) ([poe-vault](https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/guides/the-arbiter-of-ash-boss-guide)). Phase 1 runs to 55%; phase 2 starts when he "picks up his weapon from the middle of the arena". Flame Seed "explodes, dealing damage everywhere but in the circle". The "Hallways" pattern is arena-wide flame blasts with gaps, and safe lanes have a "slightly bluish tint". Phase 2 orbs, if touched, "cover all the ground in the arena with either burning ground (black orb) or chilling ground (white orb)" (same). Phase 2 overlaps attacks, forcing the player to handle "Donut Nukes" while dodging slams (search summary of the same guide, secondary).
  - **0.1.1 patch (January 2025):** "To address the problem of only having one attempt at the Arbiter of Ash Boss fight, you will now have up to six respawn attempts." Only the key-holder can trigger respawns, and "higher difficulties of the Boss will lower the number of respawn attempts". The boss "is now invulnerable for longer during its emergence and now takes significantly reduced damage while emerging". Flame Seed can no longer be interrupted, and the gale can no longer be blocked. GGG added: "We'll be looking to add respawn attempts to other Pinnacle content over time" ([official 0.1.1 notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3695606)). Search summaries say the boss also "now phas[es] at a lower life threshold, and during the transition… heals back to full" (secondary).
- **Xesht, We That Are One (Breach):** the fight starts with a horde run. "Killing monsters adds to a timer, and if the timer ever reaches zero you lose the chance to fight Xesht." Life is 6 million at difficulty 0 and 32 million at difficulty 4. Phase 2 starts at about half life with a portal transition. Telegraphs: Hand Smash has a "pink-ish purple telegraph" and is the "hardest hitting attack"; Triple Blast is signalled by the "shoulders glow purple"; Finger Gun is announced by a sound. Players beat him four times at rising difficulty to unlock the Breach passives ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/bosses/xesht)). This is the closest existing analogue to a survivors 30:00 boss: the horde is the qualification, and the kill rate buys time.
- **Anti-burst (2026, ahead of "Vaal Legacy"):** when a boss emerges or becomes aggressive it gains "temporary damage reduction" that "starts at full strength", "gradually tapers down over time" and "falls off faster as the boss moves from its starting position". Stationary bosses get shorter windows. The aim is to "reduce the feeling of 'unfair' deaths and improve moment-to-moment decision-making". The same update improved phase-change sequences for the Act 2 and Act 4 final bosses ([poe-vault](https://www.poe-vault.com/poe2/news/path-of-exile-2-introduces-anti-burst-boss-mechanics)). The patch number was not given.

### 3.5 Player complaints (PoE2)

- **One attempt, everything lost:** "So far i have attempted 5 Citadel maps and have died in all of them… This one attempt crap is ridiculous and you lose everything" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2694490/discussions/0/598517805390812290/)). A reply sums up the community's coping strategy: "Endgame bosses are not meant to be fought in a traditional way. You're supposed to get OP and trade 1-shot mechanics and hope they die before you get 1-shot" (same).
- **Visual clarity:** "POE2 has these cool boss fights with lots of mechanics that you need to avoid, but there are builds that make it literally impossible to see them… This heavily incentivizes builds that one shot bosses." Players ask for "a visual effect opacity slider" ([PoE forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3906258)).
- **Missing cues for hardcore:** Balbala's poison cloud has "no audio clue"; Zalmarath's dagger is near-impossible on a first attempt ([PoE forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3643563)).
- **Defence that doesn't apply:** the slam-unblockable thread above.

The pattern: players accept dying to a telegraphed attack. They object when they cannot see the attack or when one death costs a lot.

## 4. Last Epoch

### 4.1 Lagon (Chapter 7 and the "Ending the Storm" timeline, level 75)

- **Phase 1:** Lagon towers over a crescent ledge, and "Lagon's main body does not take damage". The player damages two large tentacles at the sides. Telegraphs: a pincer slam "comes after a lengthy wind-up"; the eye cone "can be identified from Lagon closing his eye"; a "telegraph[ed] moon blast" is easy to avoid but heavy; before the lightning beam sweep he "slowly moves his head towards you while also focusing with his one eye" ([Fandom LE](https://lastepoch.fandom.com/wiki/Lagon)).
- **Progress meter in the world:** "When the moon tile in front of Lagon is fully charged… Lagon teleports you to the lower part of the arena." Phase 2 fills a second moon by killing tentacles while adds (Adrift Meruna, Flayer Sharks) and tidal waves arrive. Phase 3 is a fight to the death. In the Monolith version, phase 2's tidal waves continue into phase 3. On defeat he submits: "Your skills entertain me, and for that, you shall live!" (same).
- **Lessons:** a giant boss that cannot be hit directly, with a moon meter painted on the floor, works well from an isometric camera. The health bar becomes part of the arena, and the boss's scale is shown by making only his limbs targetable. Players single out Lagon's beam as a lethal threat for melee ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/899770/discussions/0/597397069919979061/)).

### 4.2 The Shade of Orobyss and corruption scaling

- **Spawn and kit:** he appears as an "Echo of a World" node on any timeline without needing stability. His defeat "brings corruption on the timeline, while resetting it" ([Fandom LE Monolith](https://lastepoch.fandom.com/wiki/Monolith_of_Fate)). Each fight he picks "Both skills from one of five Damage Types (Fire, Lightning, Cold, Physical, Poison)", "Three Void Skills" and "One Movement Skill" from a 30-skill pool ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/monolith/shade-of-orobyss-boss-guide)). A randomised boss kit keeps a repeated encounter fresh and could suit an endless-mode boss.
- **Corruption as the difficulty dial:** corruption "adds a permanent monster health and damage increase to all echoes, while increasing the amount of stability echoes can bring, monster level, item rarity and experience gained" ([Fandom LE](https://lastepoch.fandom.com/wiki/Monolith_of_Fate)). Corruption gain depends on the Shade's distance from the timeline centre, timeline-boss kills since the last Shade, and corruption in other timelines ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/monolith/shade-of-orobyss-boss-guide)). Gaze of Orobyss (gained per timeline-boss kill in empowered timelines) increases the next Shade's corruption, "all Gaze is lost if you die to the Shade" (same). Patch 1.1: "Effective Corruption for calculating experience bonus increases at 25% speed after 300 Corruption" and "at 5% speed after 1000 Corruption" ([Maxroll 1.1 notes](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/news/1-1-patch-notes)). **I could not find the exact health and damage increase per point of corruption.** The official support article blocked access.
- **Lesson:** the player sets difficulty by choosing to fight a gatekeeper boss, and the multiplier is open-ended. Survivor Unchained's post-30:00 endless mode could add a stack per extra boss kill in the same way.

### 4.3 Aberroth and Uber Aberroth (pinnacle, 1.1 "Harbingers of Ruin")

- **Access:** each of the 10 timelines has a Harbinger that spawns after the timeline boss and "absorb[s] abilities from the Timeline Boss that spawns them". Harbinger Eyes are the key ([Maxroll 1.1 notes](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/news/1-1-patch-notes)). A boss that inherits the previous boss's moves gives a stage-two fight for free.
- **Phases:** 100–77, 77–63, 63–49, 49–35 and 35–0%. On each transition "it gains 90% less damage taken and stun immunity for 4.5 seconds". Phase 2 adds meteors and roots. Phase 3 creates "four quadrants, each with detrimental ground effects". Phase 4 adds a cursing scythe. In phase 5 "arena is much smaller" ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/monolith/aberroth-boss-guide)).
- **Stacking debuffs:** Frailty ("5% less damage per stack", "10% less health leech, ward retention, and health regen per stack") and Curse ("Reduces All Resistances by 10%", "Cannot be cleansed. Unlimited stacks") (same). This is an attrition enrage: the longer the fight, the weaker the player.
- **Uber Aberroth:** Maxroll lists 500+ corruption, entry via the "Threshold of Eternity" Woven Echo (a guaranteed drop from regular Aberroth), Harbingers spawning at phases 2 and 4, and the boss "heals to full health at the start of Phase 5". Rewards: Immortal Vise (80%) and Shattered Worlds (20%). Normal Aberroth drops Nihilis 14.29%, Seed of Ekkidrasil 38.1% and Null Portent 47.62% (same). A forum user believed the Uber fight scales with corruption; another clarified that "Both Aberroth and Uber Aberroth are static fights" (secondary, [LE forum](https://forum.lastepoch.com/t/does-aberroth-get-harder-if-i-try-him-at-corruption-1000-vs-300-non-uber/77058)).
- **Player opinion:**
  - A melee player's complaint: "Uncleansable ailments, countless DoT Puddles and no-go zones". Rebuttal: "The uncleansable ailment comes from a ability with a clear audio cue and wind up animation"; and "it's supposed to be the pinnacle boss. The hardest content in the game." The original poster later found it "so easy now" once they slowed down and took safe windows ([LE forum](https://forum.lastepoch.com/t/aberroth-melee-nightmare/74332)).
  - A Steam thread titled "Aberath fight is darksouls bullethell and its not good" comes from a self-described older player with slower reactions. Replies stress sustain over raw defence ("You will STILL die if you have no means of restoring that 10 000 HP back to full"), or "a massive damage build, enough to cut short some of the phases" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/899770/discussions/0/4513254014341870025)).
  - Players learn that DPS can skip the content of a phase, though not its gate.

### 4.4 Julra (Temporal Sanctum dungeon)

- **Arena and era shift:** a circular arena "where the four side areas are harmful". The player switches between the Divine and Ruined eras ([Fandom LE](https://lastepoch.fandom.com/wiki/Julra)).
- **Catastrophic Implosion:** "sets a huge clock in motion that, when it reaches 12 o'clock, causes a screen wide Void Damage AoE blast that also applies 4 stacks of Doom. This attack can be avoided by switching Eras" ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/dungeons/temporal-sanctum-dungeon-guide)). A clock face on the floor is an excellent top-down telegraph: it shows both "lethal" and "how long until".
- **Dimensional Tears:** "exist in both eras and persist as long as Julra is undefeated". She always places one on the player when the player switches eras ([Fandom LE](https://lastepoch.fandom.com/wiki/Julra)). Every escape adds permanent clutter, so the player's own escapes shrink the arena (compare the Shaper's vortices).
- **Era-dependent targets:** pillars can be destroyed in the Divine era but are invulnerable in the Ruined era, where they cast telegraphed bolts. A healing fountain must be broken (same; [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/dungeons/temporal-sanctum-dungeon-guide)).
- **Tier scaling:** tier modifiers add health and damage, from "30% Increased Health and damage to enemies" at tier 1 up to "180%" at tier 4. Each tier has its own unique drop: Julra's Stardial, Somnia, Julra's Obsession, and Vessel of Strife at tier 4. The dungeon's reward is crafting Legendaries in the Eternity Cache ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/dungeons/temporal-sanctum-dungeon-guide), [Fandom LE](https://lastepoch.fandom.com/wiki/Julra)).
- **Opinion:** Julra's blast is named alongside Lagon's beam as an attack melee players must respect ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/899770/discussions/0/597397069919979061/)). Search summaries note that "there is no way to tell when or where [Julra's abilities] will strike" ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/mmo/last-epoch-julra-boss-guide-unique-drops-mechanics-best-builds), secondary).

### 4.5 Last Epoch's anti-burst system (1.1)

- **Before 1.1:** bosses had "a variable amount of damage reduction based on how quickly they have been taking damage", which is adaptive damage reduction.
- **From 1.1:** that was replaced by breakpoints. "When their health is reduced to a breakpoint, they gain ward based on their maximum health. Any extra damage beyond the breakpoint passes through to the ward they gain." The ward's "decay… scales with how much time has passed since the boss gained it". Chapter, timeline and dungeon bosses have more breakpoints, while "most other bosses have two". Also: "Bosses have 50% more effective health against stun and freeze" ([Maxroll 1.1 notes](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/news/1-1-patch-notes)).
- **Why it matters:** adaptive damage reduction is invisible and feels like cheating. Breakpoints are legible, since the player sees the health bar stop and a ward appear. They still stop a one-shot through the whole bar, and extra damage beyond the breakpoint is not wasted, so a strong build still feels strong.

## 5. What makes a mechanic work from a top-down camera — synthesis

| Principle | Works (example) | Fails (example) |
|---|---|---|
| Sound-first lethal cue | Sirus "DIE!"; Maven "SCURRY, SCURRY!"; Eater "All will be devoured!" | Balbala's cloud with "no audio clue" |
| Safe zone as a big, uniquely coloured shape | Zana's red bubble; Elder's inner ring; Arbiter's seed circle and blue-tinted lanes; Doryani's lightning cage | Effects hidden under player VFX ("literally impossible to see") |
| Telegraph encodes time as well as place | Julra's clock reaching 12; Sirus's line spoken before he fires | Fast unreadable stabs (Zalmarath) |
| Boss disappearance or invulnerability glow as a cue | Shaper vanishes before the slam; Uber Elder's silver glow | — |
| One-shots rare and named | Uber Elder has exactly one (Ring of Death) | "Screen wide instadeath" ubers; PoE2 slams unaffected by defence |
| Accumulating hazards as soft enrage | Shaper vortices (player-placed); Sirus storms (shrinking arena) | Uber Maven permanent zones; Aberroth "DoT puddles" |
| Health gates against burst | Sirus 75/50/25 intermissions; LE ward breakpoints; PoE2 emergence DR | Pre-1.1 LE invisible adaptive damage reduction |
| Horde has a job | Atziri healers; Xesht kill-timer; Uber Elder adds refill flasks | — |
| Low cost of failure | Arbiter's 6 respawns (0.1.1) | One-attempt Citadels ("you lose everything") |
