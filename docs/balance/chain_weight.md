# The chained swing: a cut is a share of the blow (M3 MR 3, 10 Nov)

Sunder and Oathblade are written as BLOWS (Sunder 28 every 1.6 s, Oathblade 22.81 every 1.05 s) and swung, when held, as a chain of CUTS (`press.chain.period`, 0.70 s). The record keeps the blow; the chain block only says how long a cut is. A reader that knows nothing of chains (the arena, the kindled form, a tool) gets the blow as written.

**The weight.** A cut weighs the share of the blow's wait that its own period takes: Sunder 0.70 / 1.6 = 0.4375, Oathblade 0.70 / 1.05 = 0.6667; a heavy cut on a longer clip weighs more by its period (`SwingChain.PeriodScale`). The weight is live only inside the cut's cast (`WeaponInst.CutW`, as `FireMul` is): cards, echoes, DoT ticks and zones read the written blow.

**Rates hold per second.** Damage, Resolve, Flare fill, lifesteal, knockback travel, boss stagger, heavy counts (own, Hand, rite), stack and chill build, flat procs. Counts are kept in written blows with the part carried (`Sim/Blows.cs`), never reset.

**Events keep the blow.** A heavy's extras land whole (Sunder's line at 28, the heavy's Resolve and shove extra). The opening cut of a count is a whole blow (the first heavy comes no later than about today's: Sunder cut 8 at 4.9 s). An opener crit lands whole on the first cut. A Shock is eaten by one blow's worth of cuts. A death burst and a DoT's size read the blow the cut is a share of. Once-per-firing keys are once per written blow.

**Statuses riding a cut (b3a, `Battle.ApplyStatus`).** Sized on the blow. Hard control (Stun, Fear, Charm) rolls at chance x weight and lands whole (as many a minute; the stagger it adds to a boss is NOT weighted again). Every other kind rolls `Blows.Chance(chance, weight)` (the cuts of one blow's worth compound to the blow's chance; 1 stays 1, so it never gaps) and what a landed one builds (burn, poison, bleed stacks, chill) is the share of a blow it stands for (`statusBuild`, in [weight, 1]); the FIRST application on a foe free of the status is a whole one (a fresh foe burns and chills as from a blow). Measured, 63.9 s chained against unchained: bleed 0.35 -1.7%, burn at chance 1 +0.5%, a 20% stun's count +0.6%, a stun rider's boss stagger -0.1%.

**Watch when a lamp gives a grip skill a stacking status under chance 1 (main, accepted as the blow's quantization, in her favor).** A 35% stacking burn does +23% chained (a 3 s burn on 1.6 s blows gets ONE re-roll before it lapses, the cuts get four), and a chill freezes 9 times against 8 in 63.9 s (after a thaw the next cut comes within 0.7 s, the next blow within 1.6 s). No shipped grip status is in that case today (the lamps' are chance 1). `tests/StatusWeightTests.cs` pins both as measured.

**A hit that lands after its cast carries its cut's weight.** `Projectile.W` is stamped at `Launch` (`w.CutW`) and read at the hit (`HitOpts.W`, before the weapon's live weight): Oathkeeper's crescent. An on-kill echo is a whole blow (`OnKillEcho` sets the weight to 1 round its firing).

**A boss's stagger under the crescents: the 0.9294 was one heavy in the air (s1, 10 Nov; not a rate).** A boss's stagger is the weighted row (`HitEnemy`, 0.004 x knockback x weight: a fifth of it) plus the Hand's heavies (`HandHeavy`, 0.1 of the bar each, whole, one per 6 blows' worth on both records: four fifths). 12 heavies a minute, so one is 7% of the minute. At 63.9 s both records had cast 12; the unchained 12th had landed (63.33 s), the chained 12th (cast 63.62 s) was still flying: (11 x 0.1 + 0.2562) / (12 x 0.1 + 0.2592) = 0.9294. The row alone is 0.9884, as the damage; with what is in the air added 0.9975; held 60 s 0.9978, 126 s 0.9981, 630 s 0.9912 (117 v 118 landed). `tests/LateWeightTests.cs` T2 holds the row to the damage, counts the heavies as cast and bounds the whole at 1%.
What is left is the chain's own pace under the crescents: 706.17 blows' worth against 714 in 630 s (-1.1%, the same on damage, the row and the heavies; inside the accepted Oathblade -1.6%). Its cause is not named yet.

**A scheduled strike carries its cut's weight too** (`StrikeSpec.W`, stamped in `ScheduleStrike`, read by `Explode`: Bonesplitter's second strike; built at e8059e64e, its test is still skipped).

**Procs fired by a cut (`Battle.Fire`, `ProcCtx.W`; main's rule, built deterministic).** The Hit / Crit ctx carries the cut's weight; a Status ctx carries the share its application stands for (`build`, with `Damage = hitDamage x build`); Kill and Freeze are whole. At weight 1 every line and draw is as before. Under 1 a trigger is one of three kinds (`TriggerDef.Weighs()`, cached):
- SIZED (every effect sized on the hit with no flat rider, or an Apply): left alone. It fires per cut on the share, with the status weight live inside (an Apply reads the blow and builds by the weight, so a chance 1 status never gaps).
- EXECUTE (only Execute effects): every cut (a threshold, not a rate).
- WHOLE (everything else: buffs, ember, flat heals, cooldown seconds, zones, mixed): once per blow's worth of would-fires (`TriggerInstance.Part`, the first one whole), seeing the blow (`Damage / w`, statuses inside at weight 1). No new RNG draw: the trigger's own chance is rolled as written, the weight is counted.
- An icd started by a cut is `icd x weight` (the trigger's clock runs at the cut's pace: the cut train under icd x w is the blow train under icd), started at the would-fire.
- `Basis.Weapon` reads the written blow.
Measured (63.9 s, Sunder, chained against unchained, `tests/ProcWeightTests.cs`): a flat proc 50 v 50, with icd 4: 14 v 14, three foes and icd 1: 40 v 40; a 0.5 x hit nova under icd 1 +0.62%; an on-hit burn's stacks +0.46%; a flat rule on a bleed's Status -5.56% as counted (-2.86% with the part still held when the run stops; the rest sits in how many bleeds the cuts land, see the statuses' -1.7%).

**The board and the Flare read the blow** (`BoardOnHit`, `FlareOnHit` run inside the weighted window on `blowDmg`, e8059e64e): the rite's bleed 0.9997 chained against unchained on both skills. Poacher's Mercy's doubling is thinned by the weight, but its once-a-second gate still blocks the cut after each doubling (0.80 at crit 0.5): the gate is to run at `1 s x weight` like an icd (decided, not built).

**Rule for lamp and wick writers (owed, logged by the coordinator).** Code counts a blow's worth, never cuts: "every third blow" is 3 blows' worth of cuts (6.86 Sunder cuts, 4.5 Oathblade cuts), and what it throws is blow-sized. So lamp WORDS must never say "every third swing", "once a swing" or "in one swing": a player counts cuts and the number will not match. Say a time ("every 3 s of cutting throws a crescent"), or name the heavy ("each heavy cut"), which the player can see. A "kills N in one swing" lamp is counted over one blow's worth of cuts.
