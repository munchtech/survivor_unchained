# The chained swing: a cut is a share of the blow (M3 MR 3, 10 Nov)

## 10 Nov, M3 lead 16: the landed rule, the clock, the real-path A/B

**The landed rule, as built** (`godot/logic/Sim`: `WeaponInst.PaidTo`, `Battle.BankOf`, `Blows.Rested`, `CastOnce`). Each grip has a time bank. `PaidTo` is the start of the wait its last landed cut paid for; before anything lands it reads as a full bank, so the battle's first cut is whole. A cut weighs `max(own share, min(1, earned))`, where earned is the time since `PaidTo` over the written wait. The answer is exactly 1. A whiff, a dropped cut, or a crescent or strike that hits nothing spends nothing and counts nothing (no count, no Wick pulses, no follow-ups). A cut that lands on several bodies carries one weight and commits once.

**The invariant (the test).** Over any press pattern the summed landed weight is at most (t + W) / W, where W is the written wait. `RestedOpeningTests` runs 400 patterns per record, 60 s each. Worst margins: Oathblade +0.0000 against the paid-forward bound, Sunder -0.0521; at period ends -0.6667 and -0.4375. The answer never pays more than one blow's weight in its own wait's span (blows per 0.85 s span from an answer: 1.0000 at waits of 0.85 and up).

Sunder and Oathblade are written as BLOWS (Sunder 28 every 1.6 s, Oathblade 22.81 every 1.05 s) and swung, when held, as a chain of CUTS (`press.chain.period`, 0.70 s). The record keeps the blow; the chain block only says how long a cut is. A reader that knows nothing of chains (the arena, the kindled form, a tool) gets the blow as written.

**The weight.** A cut weighs the share of the blow's wait that its own period takes: Sunder 0.70 / 1.6 = 0.4375, Oathblade 0.70 / 1.05 = 0.6667; a heavy cut on a longer clip weighs more by its period (`SwingChain.PeriodScale`). The weight is live only inside the cut's cast (`WeaponInst.CutW`, as `FireMul` is): cards, echoes, DoT ticks and zones read the written blow.

**Rates hold per second.** Damage, Resolve, Flare fill, lifesteal, knockback travel, boss stagger, heavy counts (own, Hand, rite), stack and chill build, flat procs. Counts are kept in written blows with the part carried (`Sim/Blows.cs`), never reset.

**Events keep the blow.** A heavy's extras land whole (Sunder's line at 28, the heavy's Resolve and shove extra). The opening cut of a count is a whole blow (superseded 10 Nov, see top: the first cut of a battle is whole by the bank, a later opening weighs what the wait earned) (the first heavy comes no later than about today's: Sunder cut 8 at 4.9 s). An opener crit lands whole on the first cut. A Shock is eaten by one blow's worth of cuts. A death burst and a DoT's size read the blow the cut is a share of. Once-per-firing keys are once per written blow.

**Statuses riding a cut (b3a, `Battle.ApplyStatus`).** Sized on the blow. Hard control (Stun, Fear, Charm) rolls at chance x weight and lands whole (as many a minute; the stagger it adds to a boss is NOT weighted again). Every other kind rolls `Blows.Chance(chance, weight)` (the cuts of one blow's worth compound to the blow's chance; 1 stays 1, so it never gaps) and what a landed one builds (burn, poison, bleed stacks, chill) is the share of a blow it stands for (`statusBuild`, in [weight, 1]); the FIRST application on a foe free of the status is a whole one (a fresh foe burns and chills as from a blow). **Procs fired by a cut (`Battle.Fire`, `ProcCtx.W`; main's rule, built deterministic).** The Hit / Crit ctx carries the cut's weight; a Status ctx carries the share its application stands for (`build`, with `Damage = hitDamage x build`); Kill and Freeze are whole. At weight 1 every line and draw is as before. Under 1 a trigger is one of three kinds (`TriggerDef.Weighs()`, cached):
- SIZED (every effect sized on the hit with no flat rider, or an Apply): left alone. It fires per cut on the share, with the status weight live inside (an Apply reads the blow and builds by the weight, so a chance 1 status never gaps).
- EXECUTE (only Execute effects): every cut (a threshold, not a rate).
- WHOLE (everything else: buffs, ember, flat heals, cooldown seconds, zones, mixed): once per blow's worth of would-fires (`TriggerInstance.Part`, the first one whole), seeing the blow (`Damage / w`, statuses inside at weight 1). No new RNG draw: the trigger's own chance is rolled as written, the weight is counted.
- An icd started by a cut is `icd x weight` (the trigger's clock runs at the cut's pace: the cut train under icd x w is the blow train under icd), started at the would-fire.
- `Basis.Weapon` reads the written blow.

**Rule for lamp and wick writers (owed, logged by the coordinator).** Code counts a blow's worth, never cuts: "every third blow" is 3 blows' worth of cuts (6.86 Sunder cuts, 4.5 Oathblade cuts), and what it throws is blow-sized. So lamp WORDS must never say "every third swing", "once a swing" or "in one swing": a player counts cuts and the number will not match. Say a time ("every 3 s of cutting throws a crescent"), or name the heavy ("each heavy cut"), which the player can see. A "kills N in one swing" lamp is counted over one blow's worth of cuts.
