# SB00: the plate plan (the lead's production sheet)

The script is `docs/story/storybook/sb00_stay_where_they_reach.md`; this page says how each plate is built: its frame, its layers (z: 1 is the reference plane, larger is farther), its move and its living touches. The look is in "The hand" below once locked. Times are SB00's.

## The hand (to lock on the style frames)
- Painted and inked: brush ink contours of varied weight over washes; big simple shapes, deep shadow, detail only where the light falls; paper tooth everywhere; no airbrush, no photographic detail, no 3D rim light.
- Night palette: indigo, umber and slate washes; bare warm paper as the light. Only two strong hues, and they are the story: ember orange-gold (#ff8a3a / #ffd07a: the ground's light, hearths, the dead) and the cage's cold blue (#8ac8ff: only the Low Ford's iron cages and the lamp at the water). Oil flame yellower and smoky. The Legion rust-red and dull bronze; the Watch slate blue-gray (#4a5064); the priests undyed linen.
- Composed for the 2.39:1 window with the move in mind; one focal light per plate; the frame's edges sink into ink.

## The traveler and the Watchman (the same in every plate)
- **The traveler:** anyone (the title screen's rule): a long hooded coat of dark weathered wool, the deep hood up, gloved hands, a small pack; seen from behind or three-quarters behind; no face, no hair, no body shape that reads one sex, no weapon.
- **The Watchman (P4, P5):** an old soldier, gray beard, slate blue-gray cloak and tabard, a round wooden shield, a spear, a small lamp on his belt.
