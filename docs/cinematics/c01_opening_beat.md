# C01, the opening restaged: the empty bedroll

The writer's beat for the next Cinematics lead (Story 13, 29 October 2026). This
page says what the player sees and hears and in what order; Cinematics stages it
and folds it into `c01_drowned_fire.md` when built. Nothing here is staged yet.

## The owner's words (28 October, verbatim)

- "honestly the begining bit isn't even great. maybe an empty bedroll is better
  than our head on the ground, and then our shadow and then some footprints,
  then us getting up and game starting"
- (the same night, on C01) "the opening cinematic when here eyes dart is
  comically not good"

**What the note is for** (function, not the literal order): open on absence and
evidence, not on her face. The player is handed three plain things (a bed nobody
slept in, a shadow, prints) before they are handed her; she is seen whole only
when she rises; her face comes last, briefly, with no eye business; and the game
starts sooner. The owner's order is kept exactly: bedroll, shadow, footprints,
rising, play.

## What goes, what stays

- **Out:** shot 2 (the ECU of the wet strand dripping) and shot 3 (her face on
  its side on the ground, eyes opening at 2.0 s). The eyes are never seen
  opening in the new cut: when her face is first seen, she is already up and
  looking at one thing.
- **Folded:** shots 4 (elbow) and 5 (the hand at the coals) become the rising,
  one shot. Shot 6's look at the bedroll is now the opening image itself; its
  flask is shown as she stands.
- **Moved:** shot 6's letter, run to blue water, and the 1.5 s look at her
  background's item go to **C04 part A**, the dawn at the water: she drinks the
  river from the flask and takes out the letter; the river took both. One still
  beat there instead of three eye movements here. (Cinematics adds it to C04 A
  after A4 or A5; the writer's call, since the plant is paid where it was always
  paid, by Vonnra and in C32. SB00's "For the illustrator" continuity note on
  the letter shifts from C01 to C04.)
- **Reordered:** the prints (old shot 7) now come BEFORE she rises; the breath
  that makes no smoke (old shot 8) comes after, shortened; old 9 to 12 stand.
- **Lines:** no new narrator lines and none cut. The five lines of
  `cin_drowned_fire` keep their order (`bedroll`, `prints`, `lamp`, `call`,
  `frost`) and move to the shots below. No data change; no re-record (every
  narrator take is a placeholder).

## Performance

- **Shot 5.** Nothing on the face; the hair covers it. The hand at the coals does
  not pull back: that is the whole shot.
- **Shot 6.** Gaze: down to the flask (0, 0.8), hold 1.5 s; to the boots (0, 0.9),
  hold 1.5 s; the lift to the road over 0.8 s. `Wander` 0. One blink, slow, during
  the exhale (`mouth_open` 0.2 over 1.2 s, back to 0.05). `brows_sad` 0.2 flat
  throughout: someone trying to remember, who cannot tell that she cannot.
  Nothing else. If the face rig can't hold a look still for 1.5 s without
  micro-saccades, frame shot 6 from behind her shoulder instead and keep the
  face for shot 8.
- **Shot 8.** The saccade to R1 is the only fast eye movement in the cinematic:
  `eyes_wide` 0.6, `brows_sad` 0, `brows_angry` 0.2.
- **Short hair variants:** the shadow (shot 3) still reads as a head; the weed
  strand hangs from the collar's silhouette instead.

## Checked against the bedroll line and SB00

- **The bedroll line** (`cin_drowned_fire.bedroll`, the game's first words, a
  mother checking a bed: TREATMENT §3.4 plant 1) is now said over the bed
  itself, with frost on the folded blanket. The plant is stronger, not weaker:
  the player sees the unslept bed before they see her, and the narrator's first
  sentence is the first thing on screen.
- **SB00's hand-in.** The storybook's last camp (P9: "a bedroll unrolled with its
  blanket still folded on it, a pack", the fire burning high at dusk) is the same
  bedroll, drawn; P10 is the drowning; SB00 ends in black on silence. C01 shot 1
  keeps the handover's sound exactly (1.0 s silence, the drop, the owl), and shot
  2 opens on the one object the player saw in the drawn camp, hours later, by
  embers, frosted. The drawn fire becomes the real embers; the folded blanket
  carries across. The flask on its cord (SB00 P9/P10, C01 shot 6) still carries;
  the letter's continuity moves to C04 A (above).
- **The prologue's silence rule holds:** nobody speaks at the water's edge (SB00
  P10); C01's first words come only at the bedroll.
- **Her prints end at her,** as before: the shadow and the prints both point at a
  body the player has not been shown, which is the point of the order.
