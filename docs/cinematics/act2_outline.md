# Act 2 cinematics: outline

Act 2, *The North Road*, answers **what you are**. These are outlines, not
shooting scripts: each gives its trigger, what it does (who wants, hides and
changes; what it pays and plants), its beats and key images, its variants, and
draft key lines. Write each up to the Act 1 standard (`c01` to `c13`) before it
is built. The beats are `STORY_BIBLE.md` section 7; the facts are Act 1's
(`WRITING_PASS.md` section 9) unless marked.

## C20 · The Ground Opens (Act 2 opens)

- **Trigger.** The first dawn after the fortune (`chapter.done`), or the first
  night after it if the breakthrough is wide.
- **Does.** The Waystation feels what Ashford felt. Tam was right. Pays C06 (the
  children), C09 (the farm window in the fortune's last reading), `tam.tock`.
- **Beats.** The knocking under the Penhale farm's floor, at night, louder; the
  dog that would not go in the barn; the barn's floor sagging; the ground opening
  like a mouth; the farm going down (or not); things coming up out of it: pale,
  segmented, the size of dogs, moving like something remembered; the town's bell;
  people running to the edge with lamps. Then dawn on the hole.
- **Variants.**
  - *Where:* under the farm, or (if `dig.pump` was `blown`) at the old sinkhole in
    the Verge, the farm spared: then the cinematic is the sinkhole widening, seen
    from the farm's yard with the Penhales standing in it, safe.
  - *How wide* (bible 7.1: what fed the Dig, 0 to 2): a slow sinking with the
    family carrying their furniture out; the barn going in a night; the whole farm
    going at once.
  - *Who is home:* Tam's Pa in the house when it goes (if he never came in): the
    survivor's run to the door is the cinematic's spine (playable to its end, then
    cut on whether she reached him). If she heard the knocking (`tam.tock`) she can
    have brought them in the evening before: then the shot is the family on the road
    to town with a cart, Tam on top of it, watching the farm go behind them.
  - *Who holds the edge:* Maeca and the Pack (`cured` or `allied`) on the lip,
    wolves and a barefoot woman against the pale things; or the Watch alone; or
    Holloway's powder (`be.crates` = `watch`): he collapses the hole, and the farm
    with it, and the lamplings under it.
- **Key lines.** Tam, on the cart or at the edge: "It knocked. I said it knocked."
  Tam's Pa (Penhale, Somerset, swearing): "My father ploughed that. And his.
  ...Go on, then, take it." Wenna, handing out masks: "Bitterroot in the beak,
  child. Breathe through it or die." Holloway, at the powder: "Light it."

## C21 · Chapter Four (Keegan)

- **Trigger.** The survivor at the north gate after C20, the gate's question
  open.
- **Does.** Keegan has read chapter four of her handbook so many times the
  binding has gone. She has watched for the signs. She asks the survivor straight.
  Pays: the breath motif, `keegan.saw_risen`, `keegan.ready` ("You would not like
  it if I saw them"), `keegan.ch4`. Moves: Keegan and the survivor, to companion,
  enemy, or nothing.
- **Beats.** Keegan at the gate at dusk with the handbook open, reading aloud,
  formally, the way she recites. She looks up. The two of them in the cold as the
  light goes: her breath smokes; as the dark comes and the ember wakes, the
  survivor's stops smoking. Keegan watches it stop. She asks.
- **Chapter four** (Keegan reads it; this is the handbook's own text, two
  hundred years older than she is, and it sounds it): "Of the Unchained, and
  Their Return to the Dark. Their breath showeth not by night. They return. The
  dead make way for them; the hound will not. They lose their mothers' names
  before their faces. They cannot abide running water. The knight that hath seen
  these signs shall return the Unchained to the dark, with mercy, and shall not be
  persuaded."
- **The wrong sign.** The last sign is false, and Keegan knows it: the one she
  is reading it to came up out of a river. She reads it anyway, because it is in
  the book, and her voice goes flat on it. That is what she means by "badly
  written". It also tells the player the handbook is a book, written by people
  who guessed.
- **Variants.** Her respect and trust for the survivor, whether the survivor lies
  to her now ("Did you die on the Low Ford road?"), and whether Keegan has seen her
  come back from death:
  - *She steps aside.* She closes the handbook, puts it inside her breastplate, and
    unbars the gate: "I have read chapter four thirty times. I have decided it is
    badly written." She comes north.
  - *She stands.* A duel at the gate at night: she fights with the handbook's
    discipline and no anger. Won: kill her (she says "With mercy," as the handbook
    does) or spare her (she sits in the road with her sword across her knees, and
    says nothing, and is still there at dawn).
  - *She reports.* She lets the survivor pass and sits down to write to the
    chapterhouse, which already knows.
- **Key lines.** "I have been watching your breath. At night. For three weeks. I
  have kept a table." / (the slip) "I don't— I do not want to be right." / To a lie:
  "Your breath does not show, and you are lying to me. One of those is not your
  fault." (Her breath is the only one in the shot: the survivor's exhale is
  audible, as in C01, and leaves nothing in the cold.)

## C22 · The Letter (Holloway)

- **Trigger.** Night, Holloway's table, once the north gate is open (or before, if
  he decides to hand the survivor over).
- **Does.** Sallow's letter: deliver "the one from the ford" and the Watch's
  arrears are paid in full. Eleven men, a year unpaid. Pays `holloway.letter_seen`,
  `holloway.post_told`, `holloway.lied_to`.
- **Variants** (bible 7.3): *he burns it*: the survivor finds him at dawn with the
  ash and the cup, and he says what the letter said and what he did with it, and
  nothing else; *he hands her over*: the ambush at the north gate, his men ashamed,
  Holloway counting them out of habit as they come; *he tells Keegan*: and does
  nothing, which the survivor learns from Keegan.
- **Key lines.** Burned: "It offered to pay my men. All of it. ...I've written that
  down as well." Ambush: "Eleven. I counted. I'm sorry I'm good at it." (Holloway's
  one "sorry" in the game; he says it to his own counting.)

## C23 · Barefoot (the boots)

- **Trigger.** Whoever holds Pell's sister's letter puts it in Maeca's hand (bible
  7.4): the survivor, Holloway, Redcowl, Pell.
- **Does.** Maeca learns who signed for the boots. Pays the whole Ashford thread
  (`maeca.barefoot`, `maeca.signed`, `holloway.ashford`, `pell.t_pell`).
- **Beats.** Maeca reading the letter barefoot in the frost outside the Watch
  house. She goes in. What happens inside depends on her and on the survivor.
- **Variants.** *She kills him* at his own table (the survivor arrives to his
  count of boots still open in front of him). *She leaves*: she walks out of the
  east gate with the Pack, and does not look back, and the town's dogs howl.
  *She makes him hold the gate beside her*: at the war (C31) they stand together,
  not speaking.
- **Key lines.** Maeca (three words or fewer about Ashford, as ever): "You counted
  them." / Holloway: "Every pair." / Maeca: "Then count us."

## C24 · What He Carried (Harlan)

- **Trigger.** Act 2's first morning if Jory knows (`jory.knows_be`), or when
  Pell's receipts come out.
- **Does.** The grieving uncle armed the Dig. Pays C08 (Harlan's hand on Jory's
  shoulder), `harlan.be_dig`, `be.crates` = `harlan`.
- **Variants.** Jory confronts him in the shop in front of customers (if
  `jory.told_knew`, he quotes the survivor). The survivor's choice: Holloway; let
  him make it right (Harlan opens his strongbox on the counter and pays the
  evacuation and the Kerchiefs as soldiers: "It's all ember money. All of it.
  Spend it on the hole."); let the town find out (the shutters, the stone through
  the window).
- **Key line.** Jory: "Salt, you said. Every rut in Thornhollow."

## C25 · The Numbers (Pell)

- **Trigger.** The survivor finds Pell, wherever `pell.fate` and `caravan.pell`
  put him: Redcowl's cage, the south road, the cells, his desk.
- **Does.** The villain of the caravan is the one man with the numbers. Pays C06
  (the cages, now with Pell in one), `pell.books`.
- **Beats.** Pell, very polite because very frightened, doing sums in the dirt with
  a stick (in the cage) or on a cell wall with a nail: ember by the cartload into
  the hill, the depth, the night the chain gives. He gives the survivor a date.
- **Key line.** "I'm not a good man. I'm a careful one. ...Carefully, then: you
  have nine days."

## C26 · The Bandits Are the Army

- **Trigger.** After C20, at the Waystation's east gate.
- **Variants.**
  - *Redcowl comes* (alive, not at war with her): forty-one mouths at the gate, a
    dozen blades, the children, the old red standard carried in front; with
    `be.crates` = `redcowl`, six crates on a cart. Holloway at the gate with eleven
    men. The survivor stands between them. Redcowl tells the levy's story if she
    said "Ashford" to him (`redcowl.ashford_said`). Rav's tip-off comes out:
    Redcowl, laughing, names "the little bird with the sail-needle".
  - *Rav comes* (Redcowl died in Act 1): Rav walks down into the Roost in his
    doctor's coat and comes out of it in the red hat, and leads them to the gate.
    His regard for the survivor decides whether they fight beside her.
- **Key lines.** Redcowl to Holloway: "You turned the wagons back once,
  Captain. Turn us." (Holloway's count belongs to C22 and C23; Redcowl's grievance
  is the relief that never came up the road.) Rav, in the hat: "Don't look at me like that, pal. Somebody had to wear
  it, and he's not using it."

## C27 · The Hooded Buyer (Brannoc)

- **Trigger.** Night, the smithy, the buyer come for the last two irons.
- **Variants.** *Told the truth:* Brannoc has asked the survivor to sit up with
  him in the forge's dark. The buyer comes: hooded, square coin, Toll Tower violet
  at the cuff. Brannoc puts the two irons on the anvil and breaks them with three
  strokes, and says "Tell her no." The buyer goes. *Lied to:* he sells them; the
  survivor sees it from across the road; later (C28) a grey mare comes up the
  south road alone, and Brannoc reads its saddle-bag, and understands, and looks for
  the survivor.
- **Key line** (truth): "Tell her no." (He says "her". He knows.)

## C28 · The Kiln Ford (the second Unchained)

- **Trigger.** Only if the irons were sold (C27, lied to): the night Vonnra wakes
  the next crossing south.
- **Does.** Vonnra hedges her bet; the survivor watches someone she knows cross at
  night on toll work and drown, and rise. Mirrors C01 exactly (the fire, the prints).
- **Variants.** *Who:* Sella (if she lied to Vonnra for the survivor and ran), Jory
  (if he walked out on Harlan by the south road), or a stranger. The survivor can
  be there too late, or in time to stop the crossing and not the drowning.
- **Key image.** A second set of wet prints in the frost, coming up from a river.

## C29 · Silverstair

- **Trigger.** The north road's end: the Vigil's chapterhouse.
- **Does.** The order that guarded the valley is the threat. Sallow; the silver
  cages; Edric Marrow; the ledger. Pays `wayfinder.notes` ("So has he"),
  `wayfinder.name`, `keegan.vigil`.
- **Beats.** Silver cages in a cold hall, the risen in them sitting very upright
  (the cold hall's breath effects do the rest; no shot is built on it); Sallow at
  a desk writing in silver ink; the ledger open; the
  survivor's entry in Ysolde's hand; Edric in a cage drawing the corners of a
  barrow on the floor with a fingernail.
- **Variants.** The name in the ledger (hers, "Nobody", "Lark"); Keegan with her
  (she breaks with the Vigil here) or not; free the cages, leave them, bargain.
- **Key lines.** Sallow: "You've come back more often than anyone in the book.
  I'm afraid that puts you on the credit side." / Edric, to the survivor: "Ask her if she's
  got the corners right yet."

## C30 · What You Are (the turn)

- **Trigger.** After Silverstair, the survivor goes to Chid (or he comes to her).
- **Does.** The truth, said gently by the gentlest person who knows: she drowned at
  the Low Ford and got up. Every Act 1 seed lands here: the prints, the breath, the
  Warden's "Lie down." (the Order's word for the dead, said to her), "You smell like
  downstairs", the far bank, the mother's face. Moves: Chid and the survivor; he does not yet say what he is.
- **Beats.** The shrine at night, one candle. Chid tells it as a story about
  someone else, then stops pretending. The survivor's hands. A long silence. Then
  Chid asks her mother's name again, as he did in Act 1 (`chid.names`), gently, and
  waits, and this time the camera waits with him on her face for as long as she
  takes. (If he never asked in Act 1, he asks for the first time, and says so.) No
  breath beat here: C21 had it, and C52 will.
- **Choices.** "Did it hurt?" / "Who did this?" / "Am I still me?" (each a short
  answer; none a lecture).
- **Key lines.** Chid: "You were cold when they brought you in. Properly cold.
  And then you weren't." / *(Only if she heard him say it in Act 1, the once-only
  choice "You look like you've seen this before.")* "I said I'd forgotten how it
  looks. I hadn't. You don't." / "Am I still me?" "The ones in the ditch don't
  ask."

## C31 · The War at the Gate (Act 2's climax)

- **Trigger.** Sallow's people come south for the Unchained.
- **Does.** Everything Act 2 decided stands at the north gate at night: who holds
  it, who fights beside whom, who is missing. A night arena with the town behind
  it; its cinematic is the line forming and the line after.
- **Variants.** The roll of who is there (Holloway, Keegan, Maeca and the Pack,
  Redcowl's people or Rav's, Brannoc with a hammer, Harlan's money in the
  Kerchiefs' new boots, the freed from Silverstair).
- **Key image.** Everyone's breath smoking in the torchlight on the wall, and
  hers, and Chid's, and the freed Unchained's, not.

## C32 · Come Down With Me (Act 2 closes)

- **Trigger.** The dawn after the war.
- **Does.** Vonnra opens her cellar: six sigil-keys in a cloth, the seventh (the
  bones' wedge) in the survivor's hand. She asks. Plants Act 3; pays C09's ledger.
- **Variants.** Accused in Act 1 (`vonnra.accused`): she says the survivor's name
  and asks plainly; not accused: "traveller", and she arranges.
- **Key line.** "Come down with me. It is not far. It is only deep."
