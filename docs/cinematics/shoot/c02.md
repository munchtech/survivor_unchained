# C02 · None Cross After Dark: shooting script

The written script is `docs/cinematics/c02_none_cross.md`, and the timeline the game plays is `godot/data/cinematics/c02.json`. This page says how it is filmed, and it is written from that timeline as it now plays (previs pass 1, 4 October 2026, surveyed on the ford). It runs 48.5 s with today's takes.

## Coverage, line and rhythm

- **The set.** The Low Ford, as it is:
  - the river runs east to west at z ≈ -44;
  - its bed is a flat -1.35 from bank to bank, under water at -1.05, so it is shin-deep everywhere;
  - the stepping stones cross north to south at x ≈ 0 to 3;
  - the near post P0 is at (0, -33.5), and the far posts P1 and P2 are at (±9.7, -49.6).

  She stops at the water's edge beside P0 (`her`, 2.5, -31, facing north). The Warden lies in the river at `bed`: feet at (2.6, -44.5), head east. He ends on `warden_end` (2.9, -32.9), an arm's length north of her and facing her. The drowned stand in three rows north-east of the stones (x 7 to 13, z -40.6 to -46.4), sunk into the bed to the waist and facing north.
- **The line of action** runs north to south through her and him.
  - Every camera on the two of them stays west of it, the post's side. That covers 4, 6's end, 7, 8 and 11.
  - Shot 1 is behind her, on the line.
  - Shot 9 sits on the line beside her, at her eyeline, and is read as her look up at him.
  - The drowned have their own axis: they face north until shot 10 turns them to her. Shots 2 and 10 are both taken from her side of the water.
- **Coverage.** The scene is built from her looking:
  - two close shots on her face (4 and 8);
  - his three entrances: the lamp (1, 3), the rise (5) and the wade (6);
  - the two of them together (7, 11);
  - the drowned twice (2, 10).
- **Rhythm.** It holds still and slow for the song: 4.0, 4.0, 3.5, then 8.2 s on her face. It quickens as he comes: 5.0, 4.0, 4.0. It stops dead for "Lie down." (3.8 s of an ECU in silence), then cuts short and hard: 3.0, 2.5, 4.5, 2.0.
- **Sound bridge.** W1 is sung under the water from the CU on her face, so the song is heard before its singer is seen. The rise comes on the cut out of her face.

## Changes from the written script, and why

- **She walks into shot 1.** The trigger catches her mid-stride 17 m from the ford, so she finishes the walk to the water's edge in the opening shot rather than being found standing.
- **The pool.** The ford has no deeper pool below the stones: its bed is a flat -1.35, shin-deep. The drowned are sunk into the bed so the water stands at their waists. They are placed north-east of the stones, where the river is widest in shot 2's frame. A deeper pool is still wanted from the zone.
- **The Warden in the river** lies with his head east, as written. He is posed on a stand-in (the kit's open-handed `Spell_Simple_Idle`, tilted onto his back), so the fist is open, until `lie_arm_up` lands. His own lamp hangs from the fist on its bail; he lies 0.55 m higher than at first, so it hangs clear of the water.
- **Shot 2** looks north-east across their backs from the near bank, not east from the west bank, because they are north-east of her.
- **Shot 3** is 85 mm, not 135 mm. The stand-in fist and the kit's lamp don't hold a frame that tight.
- **Shot 5** is from the south-west, so the far post stands behind him and his height reads against it.
- **Shot 7** is a 2S from her back-left, not a profile. A profile from the west puts the near post between the camera and them, and this angle shows his height against hers better than a profile would.
- **Shot 8's lamp** is his own, brought to her face by `bend_lift`. A glow's light stays at its flame, so the blue lights that side of her face in the ECU.
- **Shot 9** sits on the line, at her eyeline. From her other side her shield fills the frame.
- **Shot 10** is 65 mm from beside her, past his legs: from behind him the drowned were too small to read their turn.
- **Shot 11's flames and ice** are frost novas on the three posts until the leaning flames and the ice skins exist.
- **Length:** 48.5 s, not 44.0, because W1's take is 7.5 s and shot 4 holds it.
