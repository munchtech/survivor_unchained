The written script is `docs/cinematics/c04_first_light.md` (part B), and the timeline the game plays is `godot/data/cinematics/c04b.json`. This page says how it is filmed, and it is written from that timeline as it now plays (previs pass 1, 4 October 2026, surveyed in the Waystation at dawn; B1, B4, B4a and B4b reframed on 4 October). ## Coverage, line and rhythm

- **The set.**
  - The south gate is at (0, 38). The road runs north past the stalls to the square's south edge at `square` (0, 7) and the well at (0, 0).
  - The inn's door is at (-11.3, 11), on the square's west side, and Rook's day spot is beside it.
  - The toll tower is at (33, -8), east of the square. Its upper window is on its west face, and its sill is 7.0 m up. The lamp stands on the sill at the mark `lamp` (30.95, -7.0), with its flame 7.12 m up.
  - From the square the window shows over the roofs. From the gate it shows only from about 12 m up; lower, the near roofs hide it.
  - The Watch stands at the south gate, at (-4.6, 34.6) and (4.6, 34.6).
- **When it plays:** on the first arrival (`waystation.visited` unset), under the travel fade. Its chapter card is the place's title, so the zone's own title is not shown.
- **Rook** is the cinematic's own (`npc` cast, at `rook2` (-10.3, 12.2), turned toward the square). The zone's Rook is hidden until the hand-back (`rook_cine`), so she can be placed a step off the wall where a camera can see her. The town's folk are the zone's own.
- **The line of action** runs up the road with her, south to north. From B4 it runs between Rook and her, west to east. B4 stands behind Rook's right shoulder (Rook frame left, the survivor right and beyond her), and B4b is its reverse from the same side, so Rook looks off to the right.
- **Coverage.** The town and its card (B1). The Watch's remark behind her back (B2). Her walk into the faces (B3). Then Rook's three shots: her look (B4), the tower (B4a) and her look back (B4b). The square closes it (B5).
- **Rhythm.** A long crane in (7.0). The guard's line (3.9). The walk (5.0). Then Rook's exchange (4.0, 2.6, 2.0): the lamp holds long enough for its smoke to climb. Then the rise into play (3.0).

## Changes from the written script, and why

- **Rook is the cinematic's own,** one step off the inn's wall. Her day spot is against the wall, and every camera that frames her and the square from there stands inside the inn.
- **B4 has Rook at frame left,** behind her right shoulder, not in the foreground right. Only the side away from the wall has a clear line to the square.
- **B4b is Rook's single,** the reverse of B4, not B4 again. From behind her the look back and the arms are lost, and the lamp's going out wants to land on her face.
- **The lamp is the keeper's lamp-iron,** the Warden's own pattern, standing on the sill; its flame is an ordinary warm one. The eye can tie Vonnra's lamp to the Warden's before anyone says so, and C09 puts the same lamp on her table.
- **B1 starts at 12 m, not 5 m.** From 5 m the near roofs hide the tower, so the lamp couldn't be planted.
- **B4a is 2.6 s, not 1.5 s,** at 280 mm, not 200 mm. The smoke needs time to climb, and the lamp needs size to be found at a cut.
- **B4a** is taken from the square near the survivor, not from Rook's eyeline: from the inn's door the roofs hide the tower's window. The cut still reads as Rook's look, because it comes on her turn.
- **B2** frames the guard's post rather than his shoulder. The zone's Watch stands where it stands, and the cinematic doesn't move it yet.
- **The walk** is split into three moves (to 26, to 21.5 and to the square), so she is walking in every shot she is in.
- **Length:** 27.5 s against the written 26.0: GD1's take is 0.4 s longer than its window, and B4a is 1.1 s longer.
