The written script is `docs/cinematics/c04_first_light.md` (part A), and the timeline the game plays is `godot/data/cinematics/c04a.json`. This page says how it is filmed, and it is written from that timeline as it now plays (previs pass 1, 4 October 2026, surveyed on the north bank at dawn). It runs 42.0 s with today's takes.

## Coverage, line and rhythm

- **The set.**
  - The ford's north edge is at `bank` (1, -49), in the water.
  - The road climbs north between the pines past `road` (0.5, -58) to `top` (0.2, -66), and on to the gate at (0, -112).
  - The trees are pines, not birches.
  - The sun comes from the east (+x), but there is no sun disc yet: the light is the night-to-dawn blend.
- **When it plays.**
  - It starts when she reaches the north bank after C03.
  - If she stays on the south bank, it starts 25 s after C03 hands back, with a variant first shot (A1s). The zone sets the fact `prologue.dawn_south`.
- **The line of action** runs along the road, south to north, with her walking it. The cameras on her stand to the road's east (A3) or on it (A4 to A8). A2 looks east into the trees from the road's west side: it is a shot of the place, and she is not in it.
- **Coverage.** One wide of the light coming (A2). Then a slow move inward on her: MS, MLS, CU, MCU, CU (A3 to A7). Then the long walk away (A8). The face shots carry the scene. The ember's draining (A4) is the only effect.
- **Rhythm.** The first quiet of the game. Long, even holds (4.0 to 5.5 s). A7 holds 3 s of silence after the line, and is 8.5 s in all. Nothing is cut short.

## Changes from the written script, and why

- **The trigger** is the north bank (z < -50), or 25 s after C03, as written. The zone waits for it, and holds the dawn where C03 left it.
- **A1** is taken beside her path rather than on it, so she walks past the camera, not through it.
- **A2** looks into pines, because the zone's trees are pines. The iron by the verge isn't in frame: the road's irons are south of here.
- **The sun** is the dawn blend on the cinematic's clock. There is no sun disc, god-rays or frost steaming yet.
- **A4's ember** is a fire nova at her feet until the draining effect exists.
- **The face shots** (A5 to A7) are aimed on her eyes, not on a height above the road, so they hold for any survivor's height.
- **A8** ends on `top`, where the walk ends, so she has control where the picture left her.
- **Length:** 42.0 s, not 40.0, because N6's take is 5.1 s and A7 holds it with its silence.
