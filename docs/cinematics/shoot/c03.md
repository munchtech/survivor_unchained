# C03 · The Heart Goes Down: shooting script

The written script is `docs/cinematics/c03_heart_goes_down.md`, and the timeline the game plays is `godot/data/cinematics/c03.json`. This page says how it is filmed, and it is written from that timeline as it now plays (previs pass 1, 4 October 2026). It runs 51.8 s with today's takes.

## Coverage, line and rhythm

- **The set is wherever he falls.**
  - The zone passes the cinematic two marks: `w`, where the Warden died (facing south), and `her`, 4 m south of him (facing north).
  - Every camera and place is an offset from those two, so the scene holds wherever the fight ends.
  - She is put on `her` on the cut after the blow; shot 1 is on his head, so the move is never seen.
  - The default marks, (4, -42) and (4, -38), are in the ford, and the previs is shot there.
- **The line of action** runs north to south through the two of them.
  - Shots 1 and 2, the end of the fight, are taken from its west side.
  - The line is crossed on purpose on his fall to his knees: from shot 3 on, every camera is on its east side. The fight's side gives way to the mercy's side.
  - The cut that crosses it is from a wide (2) to a low CU (3), on the biggest movement in the scene.
- **Coverage.** Three sections, each built on one eyeline:
  - the man in the giant: 1, 2, 3, 3b, 4 and 5, his look to the lamp and hers to him;
  - the heart: 6, 7 and 8, her look following it;
  - the robbery: 9, 10 and 11, Grimtunnel between her and the heart.
  - The ELS (12) closes it on the whole valley.
- **Rhythm.** Short and hard for the blow (2.0). Then slow and even for the mercy (4.0, 4.0, 2.5, 2.5, 4.0). The heart comes on a gentle push (4.0, 3.5, 2.0). The burst shortens nothing but fills the frame. Grimtunnel's lines set the length of 9 to 11 (4.3, 7.0, 5.1). The groan sits in a 5 s still wide.

## Changes from the written script, and why

- **Marks from the fall.** Everything is placed from where he dies, and she is always put 4 m south, facing him. Then every camera is an offset that holds wherever the fight ends. The written script's "if she is further than 8 m" becomes "always", which costs nothing behind shot 1's close framing.
- **The line is crossed after shot 2,** on the fall to his knees (see the coverage section). The next pass in the engine should try shots 1 and 2 from the east instead, and keep whichever reads better.
- **Shot 1** is 50 mm, not 85 mm. The giant's head and shoulders need the wider lens at a usable distance. The blow is slowed on his clip, not on the world, which is held.
- **Shot 3** is 50 mm, for the same reason.
- **Shot 4** is a 35 mm insert of his arm and the lamp, not 65 mm on the fist: the stand-in clip holds the hand open.
- **Shot 5:** he folds forward and lies face down at her feet (`m_fold_forward`), toward her. The heart rises from his chest, 2 m from her, so its drift to her hand in shot 7 is short and close.
- **Shot 8** is 85 mm and the heart stops a hand's breadth from her hand. On the stand-in her hand is on her sword's hilt.
- **Shots 9 and 11** are framed from her right and wide, because Grimtunnel is as tall as her. His burrowing and surfacing are the zone's own, so a skip does them too.
- **Shot 12** is 30 m east, not 60 m. At 60 m the camera sits in the trees of the north bank, and from 30 m the river leads the eye to her. The irons are lit in shot 1, because the zone keeps them dark at night.
- **The dawn** is begun in this cinematic (to a fifth) and held there by the road until C04 A brings the sun up. The two cinematics then share one sunrise.
- **Length:** 51.8 s, not 45.0, because the three Grimtunnel takes run long. Shot 10 holds G2 to 7.0 s, and that is the place to trim when his motion lands.
