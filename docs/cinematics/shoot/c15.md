# C15 · The Stream Runs Clear (the Verge at dawn): shooting script

New for S1 beat 9 (`docs/story/PLAN.md` section 4; `docs/handoff/story.md` "Line items", P9). The timeline is `godot/data/cinematics/c15.json`. About 20 s. No her, no faces: the place carries it. Realistic in-engine (the cinematic styles), the look bible is law (`docs/design/LOOK_BIBLE.md`: dawn is pale gold sun against lilac shade, value first, a lit pool in every frame).

## When it plays

- On her first time in the Verge by day (Dawn or Day) after the fact `hollow.healed` exists ("chain": the stream cleared with the scar; "scar": the stream ran clear already, the scar only), or that dawn if she is already in the Verge. Once: the fact `c15.seen`. Skippable after 1.5 s.
- The Verge plays its `stream.clear` state (the clean-water look, play and film alike): no blight paint or glow along the stream.
- The film's light is dawn whatever the hour she walks in (an `atmosphere` cue per shot; the zone's own light comes back at the hand-back over the play-grade ease).

## The words

Only Wenna's `cb_clean` line, over the last shot, as a voice from off (she is not in the Verge): the variant the dialogue picks (`stream.clear` cured "It's cleaner than I made it. I don't know why."; `hollow.healed` "scar" "It's cleaner than it was. I don't know why."; else "It's clean. I don't know why it's clean."). No narrator, no title card. The overnight report already said what happened; the film shows it.

## Hand-back

To play wherever she stands (her entry point), through the one cut rule (`godot/src/Game/GameSeam.cs`), measured by `--cutlog` + `tools/experience/cut_check.py`. The film never shows her, so the hand-back is a bars-out cut to the play camera with the zone's light restored over the ease (C02's play-grade ease, under 8% per frame).

## Staging rules

- No blight green, no red ember anywhere in frame (the scar is closed: if a frame finds an ember-scar light or the Dig's slurry glow, it is the state that's wrong, not the frame).
- The wolves are tier 2 here (story space, good on a budget): judge the staging, the read of "drinking, calm, daylight", not their fur at 85 mm. Keep them at MLS or wider.
- Value first: each frame has one lit pool (the water, the sun on the far bank).
