# C15 · The Stream Runs Clear (the Verge at dawn): shooting script

New for S1 beat 9 (`docs/story/PLAN.md` section 4; `docs/handoff/story.md` "Line items", P9). The timeline is `godot/data/cinematics/c15.json`. About 7.2 s (one shot since round 5). No her, no faces: the place carries it. Realistic in-engine (the cinematic styles), the look bible is law (`docs/design/LOOK_BIBLE.md`: dawn is pale gold sun against lilac shade, value first, a lit pool in every frame).

## When it plays

- On her first time in the Verge by day (Dawn or Day) after the fact `hollow.healed` exists ("chain": the stream cleared with the scar; "scar": the stream ran clear already, the scar only), or that dawn if she is already in the Verge. Once: the fact `c15.seen`. Skippable after 1.5 s.
- The Verge plays its `stream.clear` state (the clean-water look, play and film alike): no blight paint or glow along the stream.
- The film's light is dawn whatever the hour she walks in (an `atmosphere` cue per shot; the zone's own light comes back at the hand-back over the play-grade ease).

## The words

Only Wenna's `cb_clean` line, over the last shot (the stream running clear), as a voice from off (she is not in the Verge): the variant the dialogue picks (`stream.clear` cured "It's cleaner than I made it. I don't know why."; `hollow.healed` "scar" "It's cleaner than it was. I don't know why."; else "It's clean. I don't know why it's clean."). No narrator, no title card. The overnight report already said what happened; the film shows it.

## Shots (targets; the takes decide the last shot's length)

One shot (shot 2 cut in round 4, shot 1 in round 5), following the water downhill from the cause (round 3, 5 Nov: the water close-up cut; the bed can't show without set work and it would not reach the player). Every shot is dawn: the sun low from the east, a warm pale-gold key (#f3d9a0), cool lilac shade, a brighter band of sky at the horizon, the water the lit pool (the sky and the sun in it as soft bands).

## Knowns (judge r3, PASS WITH KNOWN)

- The near-right rock's slab shadow against the low back sun: recheck after the look lead's rock rebuild; hide that one rock for the film only if it persists.
- Little near mist (a second, nearer mist volume would add it).
- The Dig small and grey in the haze.
- The sky band is a thin sliver.
- Surfaces are the look lead's.

## Hand-back

To play wherever she stands (her entry point), through the one cut rule (`godot/src/Game/GameSeam.cs`), measured by `--cutlog` + `tools/experience/cut_check.py`. The film never shows her and ends about 123 m from her, so the hand-back is the DIP (GameSeam, 5 Nov): bars out as the picture dips to black over 0.4 s, the play camera placed at black, the zone's play light and grade set while black, the picture rises over 0.5 s, the HUD after the rise begins. Taken on its own (`CineHandover.Dips`: last camera over 25 m from her, or her body outside its last frame); `cut_check.py` prints `dip` for it.

## Staging rules

- No blight green, no red ember anywhere in frame (the scar is closed: if a frame finds an ember-scar light or the Dig's slurry glow, it is the state that's wrong, not the frame).
- The wolves are tier 2 here (story space, good on a budget): judge the staging, the read of "drinking, calm, daylight", not their fur at 85 mm. Keep them at MLS or wider.
- Value first: each frame has one lit pool (the water, the sun on the far bank).
- No camp or tent in frame (the blind's camp sits behind the reach's cameras).
