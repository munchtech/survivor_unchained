Supersedes the end of `docs/cinematics/c13_behind_the_door.md` (that page's "hand at the gate" and `redi` are retired). Story: `docs/story/PLAN.md` section 4, beats 7-8; `docs/handoff/story.md` "Line items" (P9). Three timelines on the built pipeline, played by the story night `vault_opened` (`godot/logic/Play/Zones/StoryNight.cs`): `c13_door.json` (at Corvane's kill, P6's fight), `c13_arrival.json` (the boss's arrival), `c13_end.json` (the boss's fall, into the reckoning). Realistic in-engine; cutscene face motion minimal (walk-ups, turns and the body carry the beats); the look bible is law (night: moon indigo against the sources; here the sources are violet: his lamp, the sigil, the heart, and the dead's eye-lights).

**Placeholders (judge the staging, not their surfaces):** Corvane (the `chid` NPC model) and the Barrow Lord (the `boss_dead` model) until P3 remakes them. Stage around them: a kinder camera, low light on them, her and the dead carry the frame. Never an MCU or closer on either.

## The set (Vault.cs coordinates, north is -z; `VaultOpened.Ground`, `Maps/Arenas/Vault.cs`)

- **The court** (z 22.5..40.5, x -11..11): the forecourt in front of the door, open to the moon, low rubble and wall stubs only at |x| > 9 and the south edge. Corvane's mark `corvane` (0, 25), facing south; her arrival `court_in` (0, 37).
- **The door** in the court's north wall at z 22: black stone smooth as glass, the sigil of seven notches on its face at chest-to-head height, the cut letters HIC LEGIO SEPTIMA SEPELIVIT QUOD URERE NON POTUIT over it. It sinks into the ground to open.
- **The hall** (z 22..-20): roofless, open to the moon, its wall tops standing; the fallen beams and sarcophagi (`VaultOpened.Cover`).
- **The stair head** (z -20..-38, the landing 24 m wide) and the stair's mouth in the north wall of the landing at about z -38: steps cut in black stone going down north into the dark. Ranks of the dead along the walls.
