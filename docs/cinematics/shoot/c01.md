# C01 · The Drowned Fire: shooting script

The written script is `docs/cinematics/c01_drowned_fire.md`, and the timeline the game plays is `godot/data/cinematics/c01.json`. This page says how it is filmed, and it is written from that timeline as it now plays (previs pass 5, 5 October 2026: the camp made for its close-ups). It runs about 69.5 s with the narrator's placeholders.

## Coverage, line and rhythm

- **The set.** Lowford's camp (`src/World/Camp.cs`, `Campfire.cs`): the fire at (-10.5, 90.5), burnt down to a bed of coals and four sticks in a ring of eleven field stones, sooted on their inner faces; no tripod or pot. Two sawn pine logs, barked, their ends greyed and checked: the seat north to south at x ≈ -8.5, z 89.2 to 91.4, and a lower one north of the fire. The bedroll is west of the fire. She lies curled on her right side at the fire ring's east edge, her face to the embers (`lie`, -9.65, 90.90, turned so the clip's front faces the fire). She wakes, comes up onto her elbow and kneels back on her heels where she lay; she never sits on the log. The road runs north to the ford (z -44) and the gate (z -112).
- **The line of action** is her look at the fire, east to west. Her face is only to be had from the fire's side, so the cameras that hold it look back at her across the coals: shot 3 from the north-west while she lies; then 5, 6 and 6a from the west and south, the side the scene keeps to until shot 12's crane swings round behind her on purpose as she turns to fight. The cut from 3 to 5 crosses through the coals themselves, on her first movement. She is lying or kneeling and looking down, so no eyeline is crossed.
- **Coverage.** The scene is built from her: three close-ups (3, 8, 8b) and one MS (6) carry her face. The top shot (2) and the hand at the coals (6a) carry the body's wrongness without a face. Two wides (9, 11) carry the place and the dead. One extreme long shot (8a) carries the road, the ford and the lamp, the far world calling.
- **Rhythm.** The cut stays slow while she wakes: long holds of 3 to 8 s through shots 2 to 8b. Nothing moves but the camera's sink and push, and her slow waking: lying, the elbow, the kneel and the reach, each in its own shot. Once the frost breaks it quickens: 5.0, 2.5, 3.5, 3.5. Silence and the drone carry the change; the narrator never hurries.
- **Sound bridge.** The lamp line runs on over the cut from 8a into 8b, so the voice "comes down to you" across the cut, from the far lamp to her face.

## Shots

The cut as it now plays (29 Oct 2026, final cut per the max judge r3), about 12 to 15 s: black with the bedroll line, the road and the lamp, her from behind, then play. Times are as the timeline lays them out with today's takes. Each shot that fits a line holds to the end of the take, plus its tail. (The coverage notes above and the shot notes in `c01_drowned_fire.md` describe the earlier, longer cut.)

## Changes from the written script, and why

- **29 Oct, Cinematics 11 r2 (unjudged):** shot 12's camera moved to her left rear at shoulder height, 35 mm, (-12.9, 1.45, 93.6) on (-11.8, 0.85, 86.0): her head and braid against the camp ahead, the fire on her far side as a rim; the `key`, `bounce` and `eyes` rigs dropped (they lit her back from this side) and the fire's light 0.85 to 0.6. Her mark and the `play` hand-over are unchanged.
- **29 Oct, Cinematics 10: the empty-bedroll restage per `c01_opening_beat.md`** ran three max-judge rounds. The shadow and prints shots failed far (the shadow read as plain darkness, the prints as stains) and were cut in round 1; the rise, the breath and the turn were cut in round 3 (her body surface, the braid, props). The camp set was rebuilt (a wool bedroll, ring stones on their own light layer, charred log ends, a pack and saddlebags for the crate and chest) and passed at the play camera. What ships is black with the bedroll line, the road and the lamp, and her held from behind as the frost breaks, then a hard cut to play.
- **Shot 2** is a top shot, not an ECU of a hair on a stone. The stone ECU sat inside the ring's geometry, and the top shot tells more in the same time.
- **She lies on her side and kneels where she lay** (animation's `lie_side_wake`, `sit_back_heels` and `reach_coals`), not on the log. Every face shot aims at her bones, so it follows the body it is given.
- **The hand at the coals (written shot 5) comes after the kneel,** as shot 6a: the reach is made kneeling, and it now says more: awake, she holds her hand there on purpose. The top shot already shows the hand lying in the ash.
- **Shot 6** is shot from her left, across the embers, to keep the line.
- **The camp is made for its close-ups.** The web game's camp was blocked out in primitives (octagonal logs, a tripod of sticks over a black ball, a ring of low-poly stones); a tripod leg and a stone lay across her in shots 5 and 6. The logs are now sawn pine, the ring made stones, the fire a bed of coals, and the tripod is gone: one traveller's fire.
- **Her looks are head turns** (the `head` cue), not turns of the body: kneeling, she looks over her shoulder at the trail (7) and whips round to R1 (10).
- **Shot 7** is 7.5 s, fitted to N2, to hold the trail.
- **Shots 8a and 8b** (the story lead's call up the road, ae194ff) sit between 8 and 9. The lamp line runs over the cut.
- **R1** rises at (-17, 90), due west past the fire, where shot 9 sees it and shot 10's turn finds it.
