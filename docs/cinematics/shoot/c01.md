# C01 · The Drowned Fire: shooting script

The written script is `docs/cinematics/c01_drowned_fire.md`, and the timeline the game plays is `godot/data/cinematics/c01.json`. This page says how it is filmed, and it is written from that timeline as it now plays (previs pass 3, 4 October 2026). It runs about 68 s with the narrator's placeholders.

## Coverage, line and rhythm

- **The set.** Lowford's camp: the fire at (-10.5, 90.5); the log north to south at x ≈ -8.4, z 89.2 to 92.6; the bedroll south-west of the fire. She wakes prone, head north, between the fire and the log (`lie`, -9.1, 91.05). The road runs north to the ford (z -44) and the gate (z -112).
- **The line of action** runs north to south through her and the fire. Every camera stays on its south side until shot 12, whose crane crosses it on purpose as she turns to fight. Shot 5 shoots through the embers from the west, and it is the one shot on the fire's far side. It is a look *at* her from where the cold is not, and she is still lying down, so no eyeline is crossed.
- **Coverage.** The scene is built from her: three close-ups (3, 8, 8b) and one MCU (6) carry her face. Two inserts (2, 4) carry the body's wrongness without a face. Two wides (9, 11) carry the place and the dead. One extreme long shot (8a) carries the road, the ford and the lamp, the far world calling.
- **Rhythm.** The cut stays slow while she wakes: long holds of 3 to 8 s through shots 2 to 8b, with nothing moving but the camera's sink and push. Once the frost breaks it quickens: 5.0, 2.5, 3.5, 3.5. Silence and the drone carry the change; the narrator never hurries.
- **Sound bridge.** The lamp line runs on over the cut from 8a into 8b, so the voice "comes down to you" across the cut, from the far lamp to her face.

## Shots

Times are as the timeline lays them out with today's takes. Each shot that fits a line holds to the end of the take, plus its tail.

### 0 · HOLD · 0.5 s
Creation's last frame, held: her by the high fire, dry. Under it the scene is set:
- her hands are emptied, and her weapon (and shield) are propped on the log;
- she lies prone on `lie`, on frame 0 of `her/get_up`, lids shut.

### 1 · BLACK · 3.5 s
- **Picture:** black. The world is changed behind it:
  - the fire drops to embers (flames 0.12; light 0 at 0.34, `#ff7430`, slow flicker);
  - frost is laid round the camp, cleared within 3.4 m of the fire, and knocked off the grass along her trail;
  - her wet prints run from the verge (-6, 72) to where she lay;
  - the toll tower's lamp is lit, far up the road (glow at (3, 44, -260), on a dark tower).
- **Sound:** `water_close` (the drowning, muffled, bubbles past the ear), then two drips on stone and an owl, once, far right. No music (`silence`).

### 2 · TOP · 3.5 s (28 mm)
- **Camera:** straight down from 4.2 m over her and the fire ring, sinking slowly to 3.7 m (`slow` ease).
- **Picture:** the embers, the tripod's legs, and her face down beside them; her left hand lies in the ash at the coals' edge. The frost silvers the corners. This replaces the written ECU of a hair on a stone: that camera was inside the ring's stones, and this frame says more. She lies where no sleeper would, at a fire no one could bear.
- **Sound:** two drips.

### 3 · CU · 4.0 s (85 mm)
- **Camera:** on her face, from her left at ground level, pushing in 0.1 m over the shot. Focus on the eyes, f/2.
- **Performance:** at 2.0 s her lids open over 0.15 s (`eyes_wide` 0.25, easing to 0.05). No gasp, no start. Blinks are held until 2.2 s.
- **Picture:** half ember, half moon.

### 4 · INSERT · 3.0 s (28 mm)

- **Sound:** a little cloth and water.

### 5 · MS · 3.5 s (35 mm)

### 6 · MCU · 7.0 s (50 mm)

### 7 · OTS · 7.5 s, fitting N2 (28 mm)

### 8 · CU · 4.0 s (85 mm)

### 8a · ELS · 4.3 s (40 mm)
- **Camera:** on the road at (3, 1.4, 5), looking north up it, past the trees.
- **Picture:**
  - the ford's three blue lamps low by the water, and the gate's wall beyond;
  - high above all of it, one small gold lamp burning in the dark on the tower, looking south.
  It is never a figure. The gate's own torches (lights 7 and 8) are put out for this shot, so the lamp is the only warm light up the road.
- **Sound:** the lamp line (narrator) begins at 0.6 s: "Far up the road one lamp burns high in the dark, and a voice comes down to you over the frost, close as if she stood at your shoulder." Cut on its comma: the voice lead wrote a breath there.

### 8b · CU · 4.5 s, fitting the lamp line and the call (85 mm)
- **Camera:** as 8, a touch tighter.
- **Performance:**

- **Sound:** the call, 0.5 s after the narration ends, "Come up, traveller. ...No charge, this once." It is Vonnra, unnamed: far, thinned by the cold, and unhurried.
- **Subtitle:** labelled "A voice up the road", not in italics, and never named, even after the reveal. The narrator never speaks a person's words, and the label is how a careful player later matches the voice to Vonnra (the story lead; `far_voice` is not among the `narrators`).

### 12 · MLS to play · 3.5 s (35 mm)

## Changes from the written script, and why

- **Shot 2** is a top shot, not an ECU of a hair on a stone. The stone ECU sat inside the ring's geometry, and the top shot tells more in the same time.
- **Shot 4** (the hand) is shot from above. The low insert was inside her arm. Until `reach_coals` exists, the hand lies at the coals rather than reaching for them.
- **Shots 6 to 9:** she sits on the log, not on the ground, until `lie_side_wake` and `sit_back_heels` exist. Every face shot was reframed to that eyeline.
- **Shot 6** is shot from her left (the fire's side) to keep the line.
- **Shot 7** is 7.5 s, fitted to N2, to hold the trail.
- **Shots 8a and 8b** (the story lead's call up the road, ae194ff) sit between 8 and 9. The lamp line runs over the cut.
- **R1** rises at (-17, 90), due west past the fire, where shot 9 sees it and shot 10's turn finds it.
