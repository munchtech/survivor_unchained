# C06 · Forty-One Mouths

**The Roost, walked into in peace; Redcowl.** Priority 2 · Act 1 · about 38 s,
then his conversation · skippable

## What it does

She walks down into the ravine expecting a bandit camp and finds a village
that has lost its village: red cloth everywhere, cookfires, washing, an old man
mending, children with a wooden sword. In the corner, three men in cages; a
woman is passing bowls of stew in through the bars, and when her own child
holds up a bowl she says "Them first." Under an awning made of an old red
standard, a big laughing man is sitting on six crates as if they were a bench,
drumming his heels on the one marked in chalk "B.E.". He looks up.

- **Redcowl wants** to know what she is (a threat, a fool, or an opportunity)
  before she knows anything about him. **He hides** Ashford: the standard over
  his head is the town's, and he will never say its name. **He reveals**, by
  what his people do, that he feeds his prisoners before his own.
- **She wants** the Coyle wagons, or the teamsters, or just to get out alive.
  **She learns** that the people she came to rob back are hungry.
- **Plants:** the six crates as a bench (`redcowl.crates`: "My lads were using
  them for seats"; the B.E. choice; Act 2's powder); the red standard with
  Ashford's arms (Act 2: the levy's story, `redcowl.ashford_said`); the forty-one
  (`redcowl.who`); the cages fed first, shown here, so that his own account of
  the cages can end on "...Ask them if they're hungry." (`redcowl.cages`) instead of
  telling what the scene has shown; the children (Act 2: what Redcowl is fighting
  for when he fights the Dig).
- **Pays:** the wreck's red fletching; Rav's "Wear red, walk slow, keep your
  hands empty".

## Place, time, light

- **Redcowl's Roost**, the ravine south-east in the Verge: entrance from the
  north-west down a cut path at about (44, 66), the camp's floor round (52, 78).
  Cages at (62, 84); the cargo under canvas at (44, 86); Redcowl's mark (54, 90).
  For this scene the six crates are moved beside his mark, at (54.5, 91), stacked
  two high and three long, one end chalked "B.E.", and he sits on them.
- **Time:** day. Best at midday or after: the ravine's floor in sun, its walls
  in shadow.
- **Light:** sun down into the cut; smoke from three cookfires catching it in
  shafts; red cloth everywhere glowing where the light comes through it. Warmer
  and more crowded than anywhere in the Verge.
- **Mood:** the opposite of what she was told. Then a held breath: crossbows.

## Into the conversation

The cinematic hands into Redcowl's conversation without dropping the bars or
the framing. Through the conversation the camera cuts among three set-ups, by
who is speaking:
- **On Redcowl:** MS, 50, from her side, slightly low, the standard behind him
  (his face is never closer than a medium shot until he has a face rig).
- **On her:** MCU, 85, over his shoulder, the camp's life behind her.
- **On a turn:** when a choice changes the room (the ledger shown, the Watch
  bluff, B.E. told, "Ashford" said), a 2S wide at 35 from the cages' side, so the
  camp is in it and reacts: the sentries' crossbows come up, the cookfire talk
  stops, the woman at the cages turns.

Special beats inside the conversation:
- **"Ashford's levy. That's who you are."** (`redcowl.ashford`): on his line, the
  standard over his head, in an insert at 85: the white tower, the crossed picks.
  The old man stops sewing.
- **"Those six crates..."** (`redcowl.crates` to `crates_keep`): on "The hill.",
  he puts his hand flat on the crate he is sitting on and does not take it away
  for the rest of the scene. On "Then nobody's having them" he stands up off them.
- **"The Watch is on its way."** (`redcowl.trick`): the whistle from the ridge; the
  2S wide: the whole camp moving at once, the washing torn down, the children
  scooped up, the pot taken off the fire with the stew still in it.
- **The ledger** (`redcowl.pell`): he reads standing; the camp goes quiet round
  him; on "After!" he sets the tin cup down on the crate very carefully, as if it were full. (His brother's tell: Rav does the same with the news in C11.)

## Performance

**Redcowl.** A big man playing a bigger one. He laughs before everything
threatening and goes flat after it: the laugh is the bait, the flatness is the
hook. He never stands up unless something matters (the ledger, the crates,
"Ashford"). His eyes go to his people whenever she looks away from him: the
children, the cages, the fires. He drums his heels on the blasting ember
because he does not know what it is.

**The woman at the cages (W1).** Thin, red scarf over her hair, forty. She
feeds the prisoners because she has been told to and because she would anyway;
she says K1 to her child without looking at him, quietly, as she has said it
every day for a week.

**The child (C1).** Holds up the bowl; is told; lowers it; waits, looking at the
survivor instead. No tears; it is routine.

**The survivor.**
- *Shots 1 and 2.* Hands open, away from her weapon (Rav's advice). Gaze moving
  over the camp (`Wander` 0.25), `brows_up` 0.2.
- *Shot 4.* Gaze on the woman and the child; `brows_sad` 0.3 on "Them first."
- *Shot 8.* Gaze on his eyes, steady; `brows_angry` 0.1, nothing more. She does
  not laugh back.
