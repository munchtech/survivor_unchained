# What makes ARPGs fun and satisfying

R1 research, 10 November 2026. This doc tests the owner's guess (mob density, sounds, visuals, corpse explosions, loot) against postmortems, GDC talks, designer interviews, live-ops reversals and a few studies. Quotes are short (15 words or fewer, one per source). Community threads appear only where many of them show the same pattern. Where a source is a low-quality tutorial site, I say so, and those numbers are starting points, not facts.

**The short answer.** The owner's five guesses are all real, but they are outputs. Underneath them sit three things the guess leaves out: **(a) the kill-reward rhythm** (every kill is a lever pull, and something is always almost finished), **(b) build expression** (the player authors their own power and can feel it grow), and **(c) readable cause and effect** (the player can see what killed what, and why). Density, sound, visuals, gibs and loot drops are how (a)-(c) reach the senses. Each one hurts when it is pushed past the point where it serves them. The industry's biggest corrections of the last decade were all about "too much": D3's Loot 2.0, D4's Loot Reborn, PoE2's move away from screen-wiping, and PoE's ongoing fight with on-death clutter.

## 1. The top 10 drivers, ranked

Ranking rule: how often the driver is the stated reason a game worked or failed, weighted by how directly it touches the minute-to-minute loop.

### 1. The kill-reward loop as a slot machine (variable reward on every kill)
- **Evidence.** David Brevik describes Diablo's loot as a slot machine where each kill pulls the lever ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/why-video-game-loot-is-so-addictive-according-to-the-c-1846695147); [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/74666/diablos-loot-lottery-rng-is-like-slot-machine-says-david-brevik/index.html)). He also said unidentified items gave "two slot machine pulls," the drop and the reveal ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/why-video-game-loot-is-so-addictive-according-to-the-c-1846695147)). The Diablo II postmortem names the core: "Players continually kill monsters and get rewarded with treasure and experience" ([D2 postmortem](https://www.cs.hmc.edu/~markk/SWE_copies/postmortem__diablo_ii.html)). Vampire Survivors' creator came from mobile gambling games ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/vampire-survivors-saved-its-creator-from-working-on-mobile-gambling-games)), and its chest opening is built on the same reward circuitry ([The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/vampire-survivors-how-developers-used-gambling-psychology-to-create-a-bafta-winning-game-203613)).
- **Technique.** Three outcomes per pull: nothing, "your quarter back" (a small, frequent reward such as gold, a potion or a common item), and a rare jackpot. **Split the reward into two beats** (the drop, then the reveal) so one roll pays twice. Every kill must be *able* to pay, even if it almost never does.

### 2. Build expression: the player authors their own power
- **Evidence.** The D2 postmortem credits a skill system where one class plays very differently, for example a Necromancer who "will summon hordes of skeletons" ([D2 postmortem](https://www.cs.hmc.edu/~markk/SWE_copies/postmortem__diablo_ii.html)). Quantic Foundry's player-motivation model puts Diablo under **Power**: players who like to start weak and climb through levels and gear ([Quantic Foundry model reference](https://quanticfoundry.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Gamer-Motivation-Model-Reference-Updated.pdf)). Vampire Survivors commentary keeps returning to the thrill of finding a new combination that works ([Secret Sauce](https://jboger.substack.com/p/the-secret-sauce-of-vampire-survivors)). GGG kept build complexity in PoE2 even while reworking combat ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/path-of-exile-2-director-is-on-a-quest-to-make-the-perfect-action-rpg-and-isnt-afraid-to-borrow-from-path-of-exile-1-to-pull-it-off)).
- **Technique.** Players need choices that change *how the game plays*, not just its numbers. Each skill modifier should change shape, count, behavior or trigger. A "+8% damage" modifier is not build expression.

### 3. A felt power curve, which scaling must not erase
- **Evidence.** At D4's launch, players said level scaling made leveling unsatisfying because fights never got easier ([Charlie Intel](https://charlieintel.com/diablo/diablo-4-players-slam-painful-progression-level-scaling-256197)). Critics called the early game the slowest part ([Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/games/diablo-4s-biggest-lingering-problems-have-ruined-its-promising-launch/)). Quantic Foundry describes Power players as wanting a visible climb from weak ([model reference](https://quanticfoundry.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Gamer-Motivation-Model-Reference-Updated.pdf)). Vampire Survivors is described as a "reverse bullet hell" where you become the bullet hell ([si.com](https://videogames.si.com/features/vampire-survivors-satisfying-good)).
- **Technique.** Let the player **outgrow content**. Revisiting an early zone should feel like mowing grass. Put difficulty in *new* content and opt-in modifiers, never in invisible scaling that cancels the player's gear. Mark the jumps the player can feel: a new skill shape, a key item, or a breakpoint where a pack dies in one cast instead of two.

### 4. Combat feel: impact the player can feel at the moment of contact
- **Evidence.** Sakurai on hitstop: on a hit, "both parties momentarily freeze, emphasizing the power of impact." In Smash, freeze length scales with damage, has a hard cap, and the victim shakes during the freeze ([Sakurai column, Source Gaming](https://sourcegaming.info/2015/11/11/thoughts-on-hitstop-sakurais-famitsu-column-vol-490-1/)). He also notes it is *less practical in crowded free-for-alls* (same source), which describes an ARPG horde exactly. Monster Hunter Wilds was criticized for losing hitstop ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/capcom-if-youre-listening-monster-hunter-wilds-needs-its-hitstop-back)). In Nijman's Vlambeer talk, 30 small tweaks turn a dull shooter into a good one, including screen shake and a 33% chance that enemies explode on death ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/vlambeer-co-founder-shares-advice-on-building-better-action-games)). Lost Ark's combat is praised mainly as "superb at a visual and tactile level" ([PC Gamer review](https://www.pcgamer.com/lost-ark-review/)), and its team layers swing-wind sounds with metallic impact sounds to convey weapon weight ([Guardianknight dev post](https://www.playlostark.com/en-us/news/articles/guardianknight-class-behind-the-scenes)).
- **Numbers (tutorial-grade sources, so use as starting points).** Hitstop of 2-4 frames on light hits, 5-8 on medium, and anything over ~20 frames reads as a bug ([gamejuice](https://gamejuice.pages.dev/articles/hit-stop-frame-trick); [uhiyama-lab](https://uhiyama-lab.com/en/notes/ue/game-feel-hit-feedback/)). A Godot implementation sets time_scale to 0.05 for ~0.08 s ([dev.to](https://dev.to/saltmire/godot-4-screen-shake-and-hit-stop-in-one-script-11eh)). Sources agree on freezing **only the attacker and the target, not UI or audio** ([bugnet](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-fix-hitstop-or-freeze-frame-feeling-off)).
- **Technique for a horde game.** Hitstop goes on *the player's heavy hits, crits, finishers and elites*, never on every trash hit. It scales with the hit and is capped. Add victim shake during the freeze, a flash on contact, a hurt reaction and knockback. Screen shake is directional and decays quickly. Use juice on *what the player caused* ([Kao 2024 summary](https://www.universityxp.com/research/2026/7/19/how-does-juicy-game-feedback-motivate-testing-curiosity-competence-and-effectance)).

### 5. Mob density with rhythm (packs, elites, lulls)
- **Evidence.** D4 patch 1.1.1 raised density in Nightmare Dungeons and Helltides by about 50% ([Prima](https://primagames.com/news/diablo-4-mob-density-increases-detailed); [Maxroll recap](https://maxroll.gg/d4/news/diablo-4-patch-1-1-1-campfire-chat-recap)). PC Gamer called it possibly the patch's most important change ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/blizzard-just-dumped-buckets-of-demons-on-diablo-4-to-make-the-endgame-grind-way-less-miserable)). The developers also warned they could overdo it, both in combat difficulty and in performance ([diablo4.life](https://diablo4.life/news/notes-from-campfire-chat-2)). D2 swapped fixed bosses for "a system for randomly generating thousands" of unique monsters ([D2 postmortem](https://www.cs.hmc.edu/~markk/SWE_copies/postmortem__diablo_ii.html)). These are elite packs with random affixes. Valve's Left 4 Dead director runs Build Up, Sustain Peak and Relax, with default values of a 15 s minimum build-up and a 5-10 s sustained peak ([Valve DirectorOptions](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/DirectorOptions)).
- **Technique.** Density means *kills per minute plus anticipation*, not a carpet of monsters. Use packs with gaps between them. A run reads as pack, pack, elite pack (a named elite with affixes and better odds), lull, then a jackpot or event. The lull is part of the design: it is where loot gets picked up and the player looks ahead.

### 6. Loot legibility: tiers you can read, sounds and beams for the rare drop
- **Evidence.** D3 kept the series' "fwip-fwip-fwip" item-drop sound ([Gameranx interview with Joseph Lawrence](https://gameranx.com/updates/id/7975/article/hear-no-evil-blizzard-sound-design-supervisor-talks-about-diablo-iii-s-gruesome-noises/)), and reviewers said the loot-drop whistle took them straight back to the original game ([Killscreen](https://www.killscreen.com/sounds-violence)). In the first Diablo, rings were meant to drop with a special sound ([mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/fathers-of-diablo-explain-how-they-invented-loot-in-video-games-that-makes-us-addicted,670631)). D4 gave Uber Uniques a unique drop sound and a purple beam after they sat unnoticed in inventories for weeks ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/d4/news/uber-uniques-are-getting-a-facelift-in-diablo-4)). In a fan poll of D4's best sounds, the mythic drop sound won, even though many voters had never heard it ([mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/players-want-to-know-which-sounds-in-diablo-4-cause-the-most-goosebumps-the-famous-butcher-is-only-in-second-place,1519376)). PoE's filters exist because drops overwhelm, and they mark high-value items with styling and sounds ([poewiki: item filter](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Filter); [mein-mmo on NeverSink](https://mein-mmo.de/en/the-most-important-tool-for-path-of-exile-2-receives-a-major-update-for-its-10th-anniversary-from-the-german-developer,1250413)).
- **Technique.** One escalating ladder carries color, label, sound and beam together. The top tiers get a **sound you can recognize with your eyes closed** and a beam visible off-screen or from across the screen. Keep it rare enough that it stays an event.

### 7. Nested goals (short, mid and long loops that overlap)
- **Evidence.** The D2 postmortem: "There's always a quest that is almost finished" ([D2 postmortem](https://www.cs.hmc.edu/~markk/SWE_copies/postmortem__diablo_ii.html)). Max Schaefer on Torchlight: players should always feel they need "one more room" or one more dungeon ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/interview-runic-games-schaefer-goes-in-depth-on-i-torchlight-i-)). D3's Greater Rifts set a 15-minute timer and leaderboards, and faster clears earn higher keys ([Blizzard 2.1 notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/14569138/patch-2-1-0-ptr-patch-notes-updated-august-13-2014); [Diablo wiki](https://di.diablowiki.net/GR)). D4's Infernal Hordes run in waves, with a choice of three boon/bane offers after each wave ([Blizzard](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24119591)). Reported waves last 90 s ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/98959/diablo-4-is-adding-rogue-like-horde-mode-in-season-5/index.html); unconfirmed by Blizzard). The mode became permanent ([Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/diablo/diablo-4-infernal-hordes-activity-permanent-feature-2868206)).
- **Technique.** At any moment the player should hold three open counters: **seconds** (this pack, this wave), **minutes** (this zone, this rift, the next skill level), and **hours** (this item, this endgame tier). Offset their completions so one closing never leaves the player with nothing open.

### 8. Audio as half the feedback
- **Evidence.** D3's sound supervisor said that if a sound takes too long to play or doesn't sound powerful, it isn't satisfying ([Gameranx](https://gameranx.com/updates/id/7975/article/hear-no-evil-blizzard-sound-design-supervisor-talks-about-diablo-iii-s-gruesome-noises/)). D2 polished even the small sounds: "Moving around inventory items produces pleasing sounds" ([D2 postmortem](https://www.cs.hmc.edu/~markk/SWE_copies/postmortem__diablo_ii.html)). Studies of juicy audio and haptics find that redundant sensory feedback improves player experience ([Smets & van der Spek](https://dl.ifip.org/hal-04144377v1); [Waterloo haptics](https://uwaterloo.ca/haptic-experience-lab/references/juicy-haptic-design-vibrotactile-embellishments-can-improve)). Nothing I found gives a measured "% of fun". The share is shown by what players remember: a drop sound wins the poll (driver 6), and the old drop whistle triggers nostalgia.
- **Technique.** Sound has **attack first**: no slow fade-ins on hits. Impact layers combine transient, body and material. Per-tier loot sounds. Each skill gets an audio identity distinct enough to read blind. Loud crowds need a mixer priority so the player's hit and the rare drop always cut through.

### 9. Readability over spectacle (the player can always see the threat)
- **Evidence.** PoE2's director says a build "can't be emitting 500 projectiles a second" covering the screen, and boss attacks are telegraphed ([Maxroll PAX interview](https://maxroll.gg/poe/news/pax-west-path-of-exile-2-interview-with-jonathan-rogers)). On-death and ground effects are a top player complaint across PoE threads ([forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3638737); [0.3 feedback](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3853332)). A common proposal is drawing enemy effects above your own, above allies' ([forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3626389)). Kao's 3,018-player action-RPG study found that **no juice and extreme juice both lowered** player experience ([as summarized in arXiv 2512.13695](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.13695)). His 2024 study warns juice hurts when it hides the link between action and feedback ([summary](https://www.universityxp.com/research/2026/7/19/how-does-juicy-game-feedback-motivate-testing-curiosity-competence-and-effectance)).
- **Technique.** Lay the screen out in layers: **enemy telegraphs > player character > player effects > ally effects > corpses and gibs**. Fade your own effects in dense fights. Give enemy telegraphs one reserved color family that no player skill uses.

### 10. Movement that feels good on its own
- **Evidence.** D4 gives every class an evade (short dash in any direction, ~5 s cooldown) ([Prima](https://primagames.com/tips/how-to-dodge-and-evade-in-diablo-4); [fextralife](https://diablo4.wiki.fextralife.com/Evade)). PoE2 has no movement-only skills: most skills carry a mobility component, and many move you forward when you target far ([poe2wiki](https://poe2wiki.net/wiki/Movement)). In PoE1, leap and dash skills that make you walk to minimum range before casting are a long-running complaint ([forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3532522)). So is Last Epoch's Fury Leap ([forum](https://forum.lastepoch.com/t/why-fury-leap-feels-so-bad/39171?page=2)).
- **Technique.** Mobility skills fire **instantly, toward the cursor, at any range** (clamped, never walk-then-cast). They have a landing payoff (damage, knockback, sound) so they double as attacks, plus enough charges or a short cooldown that the player uses them between packs as well as for dodging.

## 2. The owner's five guesses

| Guess | Verdict | What the evidence says |
|---|---|---|
| **Mob density** | **Confirmed, refined** | D4's ~50% density increase was the patch's standout fix ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/blizzard-just-dumped-buckets-of-demons-on-diablo-4-to-make-the-endgame-grind-way-less-miserable)). But density only pays with **rhythm** (packs, elites, lulls; [Valve director](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/DirectorOptions)), **performance headroom** (D4's own caveat; [diablo4.life](https://diablo4.life/news/notes-from-campfire-chat-2)), and **a reason to care about the next pack** (loot odds, elites). The measure is kills per minute and time between packs, not monsters on screen. |
| **Sounds** | **Confirmed, upgraded** | Probably under-ranked by the owner. The single best-loved D4 sound is a loot sound ([mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/players-want-to-know-which-sounds-in-diablo-4-cause-the-most-goosebumps-the-famous-butcher-is-only-in-second-place,1519376)). Blizzard has kept the same drop sound family for 25 years ([Gameranx](https://gameranx.com/updates/id/7975/article/hear-no-evil-blizzard-sound-design-supervisor-talks-about-diablo-iii-s-gruesome-noises/)). D2 polished even inventory sounds. Hit sounds carry weapon weight ([Lost Ark](https://www.playlostark.com/en-us/news/articles/guardianknight-class-behind-the-scenes)). |
| **Visuals** | **Confirmed, with a ceiling** | Visual and tactile polish is the first thing Lost Ark reviews praise ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/lost-ark-review/)). But spectacle past readability lowers fun: Kao found extreme juice did worse than medium/high ([arXiv summary](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.13695)), and PoE's clutter complaints are constant. Visuals serve impact and threat reading; they are not a goal of their own. |
| **Corpse explosions / gibs** | **Refined (downgraded as a standalone)** | D3 built critical-hit deaths where monsters burst apart, and found it became "a little boring" and excessive ([Diablo wiki: Gore](https://di.diablowiki.net/Gore)). Vlambeer's version is a **33% chance**, not every kill ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/vlambeer-co-founder-shares-advice-on-building-better-action-games)). Ragdolls that read as weight are praised in Grim Dawn reviews ([niche gamer](https://nichegamer.com/reviews/grim-dawn-review)); floaty ones drew alpha complaints ([Crate forum](https://forums.crateentertainment.com/t/combat-video-1-4-realz-this-time/27287?page=5)). On-death effects are a top PoE clutter complaint ([forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3638737)). **The rule:** spectacular deaths are a *reward for big hits* (crit, overkill, finisher) and a *build mechanic* (corpses as ammo: D2's Necromancer). They are not default on every death, and must not cover threats. |
| **Loot** | **Confirmed, the core, but refined to "rare and legible"** | Loot is the genre's engine (Brevik's slot machine). But both Blizzard's and GGG's big corrections cut quantity for quality: D3 Loot 2.0 aimed for "fewer, better" items ([Diablo wiki](https://diablo.fandom.com/wiki/Loot_2.0); [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/diablo-3-reaper-of-souls-loot-system-designed-to-cut-the-legs-out-from-the-auction-house)). D4 S4 promised fewer, better-quality drops ([mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/blizzard-finally-shows-how-the-most-important-aspect-of-diablo-4-is-supposed-to-get-better-loot,1105282/)). D4 S8 cut leveling legendaries because they became the only rarity players cared about ([Icy Veins](https://icy-veins.com/d4/news/blizzards-nerfing-gearing-while-leveling-in-season-8/)). PoE2 put rarity over quantity and moved good drops to bosses ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/news/pax-west-path-of-exile-2-interview-with-jonathan-rogers)). |

**What the guess misses**, in order of weight:
1. **Build expression** (driver 2).
2. **A felt power curve** that scaling must not erase (driver 3).
3. **Nested goals** (driver 7).
4. **Readability**: the brake on every one of the owner's five (driver 9).
5. **Movement feel** (driver 10).
6. **Hit feel**: impact frames and hitstop on contact, which is narrower than "visuals" (driver 4).

## 3. Anti-patterns (what kills the fun)

1. **Loot inflation.** When everything is legendary, nothing is. Inventories fill with trash ([Wowhead on D4 itemization](https://www.wowhead.com/diablo-4/cn/news/the-problems-with-itemization-in-diablo-4-336951)), and the top tier becomes the only one that matters ([Icy Veins S8](https://icy-veins.com/d4/news/blizzards-nerfing-gearing-while-leveling-in-season-8/)). GGG admits more ground loot raises long-term clutter ([FinalBoss on 3.28](https://finalboss.io/path-of-exile-3-28-will-dump-more)).
2. **A filter that removes the hunt.** Last Epoch's team limited filter power because a perfect filter turns "the loot hunt" into a loot *wait* ([devtracker](https://devtrackers.gg/last-epoch/p/fb8ba0e7-idol-loot-filter-options)). The opposite failure is PoE, where a filter becomes the only practical way to play ([mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/the-most-important-tool-for-path-of-exile-2-receives-a-major-update-for-its-10th-anniversary-from-the-german-developer,1250413)).
3. **Buying or skipping the find.** D3's auction houses "really hurt the game" ([Engadget, Jay Wilson GDC 2013](https://www.engadget.com/2013/03/28/diablo-3-director-jay-wilson-auction-houses-really-hurt-game/)). Cheap bought gear broke the find-and-upgrade rhythm ([GameBusiness.jp](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2013/03/31/7764.html)).
4. **Invisible level scaling** that cancels gear, so leveling never feels like getting stronger ([Charlie Intel](https://charlieintel.com/diablo/diablo-4-players-slam-painful-progression-level-scaling-256197)).
5. **Slow early game.** D4's slowest stretch was the opening hours ([Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/games/diablo-4s-biggest-lingering-problems-have-ruined-its-promising-launch/)), and those hours decide whether players stay.
6. **Visual clutter**: on-death effects, ground effects, fog, bloom ([PoE forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3638737); [GGG on bloom](https://pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2907745)).
7. **Binary health and one-shots.** Wyatt Cheng called D3's full-to-empty health swings unhealthy because they remove room to play well ([GameWatcher](https://www.gamewatcher.com/news/2013-26-09-blizzard-argues-for-slower-combat-in-diablo-iii-current-system-not-healthy)).
8. **Screen-wipe monoculture.** A single button clears everything and nothing needs a response; PoE2 is built against it ([Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe/news/pax-west-path-of-exile-2-interview-with-jonathan-rogers)).
9. **Low density with no lulls designed in**: empty walking, the pre-1.1.1 D4 complaint ([Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/diablo-4-patch-111-preview-more-monsters-more-magic-more-legendaries-oh-my)).
10. **Same spectacle every kill.** D3's gory crit deaths wore thin ([Diablo wiki](https://di.diablowiki.net/Gore)).
11. **Sluggish movement skills** that walk to range first or have a dead wind-up ([PoE forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3532522)).
12. **Global freezes.** Hitstop that freezes UI and audio, or fires on every hit in a crowd ([bugnet](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-fix-hitstop-or-freeze-frame-feeling-off); Sakurai on crowds, [Source Gaming](https://sourcegaming.info/2015/11/11/thoughts-on-hitstop-sakurais-famitsu-column-vol-490-1/)).

## 4. What this means for us (prioritized)

Context used: loot tiers with filters, skills with wicks and levels, story zones, the Low Ford tutorial, the arena mode (the survivors night), and the M3 combat lane with hit-shake. Names come from the ARPG.md section headers only. I read nothing else in the repo, so "already done" items may exist that I cannot see.

**P0: the minute-to-minute loop (do before any more content)**

1. **Scope M3 hit-shake to the player's heavy hits and make it local.** Freeze attacker and target only (not UI or audio), scaled by hit weight, with a hard cap, plus victim shake and a flash. Starting values for our tuning: none on trash hits, ~40-60 ms on heavy hits and crits, ~80-100 ms on elite kills and finishers, never above ~150 ms. Screen shake is directional and fast-decaying, and is reserved for the player's big hits and explosions. Judge it at the game camera with 30+ enemies on screen, which is where Sakurai's crowd warning applies.
2. **A drop-sound and beam ladder, locked for all time.** One recognizable sound per top tier, plus a beam for the top two tiers. It must be identifiable with eyes closed, and the top sound must stay rare enough to be an event (D4's mythic-drop result). This is the cheapest high-yield item on the list. Route it through the sound lane with the same bar as visuals.
3. **Measure density as a rhythm, then tune it.** Instrument kills per minute, seconds between packs and elite packs per zone on Low Ford and the first story zone. Targets are a pick for us to playtest: a pack within ~5-8 s of leaving the last one, an elite pack every ~60-90 s, a designed lull after each elite. Keep about 2x performance headroom over the target count, because D4 raised density ~50% after launch and we should be able to as well.
4. **Rule: what's on screen is ranked.** Enemy telegraphs get a reserved color family. Player effects fade above N enemies. Corpses and gibs never cover a telegraph. This belongs in the UI and VFX bible as a standing rule, not a polish pass.

**P1: the reward structure**

5. **Spectacular deaths as rewards, not defaults.** Gib, launch and explosion deaths only on crits, overkill or finishers, at a capped rate (Vlambeer's 33% is a good upper bound per eligible kill). Ragdolls must read heavy, not floaty. Corpse explosion belongs as a **build mechanic** (a wick or skill that spends corpses), which gives the owner's guess a real system home.
6. **Two-beat loot.** For the top tier, split the drop and the reveal: a sealed or "unidentified" moment, then the roll (Brevik's two pulls). Keep it for rares and up so it never turns into a chore.
7. **Ship loot filter defaults, not filter power.** Good per-tier defaults (hide junk after Act 1, always show and sound the top tiers). Keep the filter simple enough that the *find* stays a decision, as Last Epoch argued. Watch cadence so that filters stay optional, not survival gear as in PoE.
8. **Quality over quantity in the cadence call (ARPG.md 7.1).** Fewer, more relevant drops, weighted toward bosses and elites (PoE2, D3 Loot 2.0). Let the top tier exist from Act 1 but keep it rare. The mid tier must keep mattering, which is D4 S8's lesson.

**P2: power and builds**

9. **Wicks must change behavior, not numbers.** Audit the wick list: each should change a skill's shape, count, trigger or element. Replace any "+X%" wick with a behavioral one. This is our build expression and the biggest gap in the owner's guess.
10. **Let the player outgrow content.** No invisible scaling that cancels gear in story zones. When the player returns to Low Ford at a higher level, it should be a mowing moment. Put challenge in new zones and opt-in difficulty (ARPG.md 7.3).
11. **Front-load the power curve.** In Low Ford and Act 1, give the first skill-shape change (a wick or a level-up visual) within the first ~10 minutes, and the second key power jump inside the first hour. D4's slow opening is the warning.

**P3: loops and movement**

12. **The arena mode borrows D4's Infernal Hordes structure.** Short waves (~60-90 s), each a build-up, a peak and a short relax, then a three-choice boon/bane offer between waves. That matches our night cards and wicks (try before you buy). End with a payout chest that uses the full drop ladder. Use a timer or leaderboard per daily challenge (Greater Rift style) for the long loop.
13. **Keep three counters open at all times.** The HUD and journal always show one seconds-scale goal (pack or wave), one minutes-scale goal (zone, skill level), and one hours-scale goal (item chase, endgame tier). Stagger their completions.
14. **Make the dash an instant attack.** Instant, toward the cursor, clamped to max range (never walk-then-cast), with a landing payoff and charges, so it gets used between packs. This fits the leap and cyclone the owner wants, and ARPG.md 3.4's note that a dash leveled only by pressing it is weird. Its level-ups should change its shape (a landing shock, an extra charge, a trail).
15. **Mix audio so the important sounds win.** The player's hit, the rare drop and enemy telegraph cues always cut through crowd noise. Hit sounds have attack first, no slow fade-in. Test with 50+ enemies dying at once.

**How to check us against this.** At the game camera, with a dense pack: (a) can a viewer say what killed what? (b) can they hear a top-tier drop with eyes closed? (c) does the third pack feel different from the first? (d) after 10 minutes, has the player's skill visibly changed shape? Four yeses is the bar.
