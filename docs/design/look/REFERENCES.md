# The references, read technically (L1 step A1, 4 Nov; art director, look-l1)

Taste, never a copy. Generation prompts never name a game, studio or living artist: they describe the qualities below. The owner's words: "a clever, proper art style that we can just build on"; "art is a big deal, not a patch"; for her, "casual badassery and sexyness".

| Reference | What it does, technically | We take | We refuse |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Marvel Rivals** (the owner's pick of kind) | Painted base color in blocks with light painted in (top-lit gradients, painted cavities). A toon ramp over physically based light: a wide lit band, a crisp terminator with a narrow soft edge, and a lifted, colored shadow side with a per-material shadow hue (skin shades warm). Shaped highlights (hair bands, metal streaks). A strong fresnel rim. An outline on characters, weighted by distance, with inner lines painted into the maps. Vibrant color blocks; heroic, semi-stylized anatomy with sex appeal. | The ramp, per-material shadow hue, shaped spec, rim, distance-weighted line, light painted into the maps, appeal in the forms and the camera | The comic-hero palette (primaries everywhere), clean sci-fi surfaces, its characters |
| **Hi-Fi Rush** | Full cel: flat two-tone shading with halftone and hatch screens in shadow, heavy inverted-hull outlines, flat saturated color, comic FX as graphic shapes. | The confidence of the shadow shapes; FX as hard graphic shapes; line weight as the read of a character | Halftone as the default (it reads comic-cute, and we are 18+ dark fantasy); flat anime faces; the pop palette |
| **Overwatch** | Stylized physically based shading, no lines. | **Our storybook plates** (frame 02) | Carved ink line, ember gold against indigo, three value masses. | The palette and values stay the law (bible rules 1-4); the line's character in 3D | - |

**The synthesis, Survivor Unchained's own:** *a dark-fantasy world drawn in ink and painted in blocks, lit by ember against moon.*
- **Light:** two bands and a warm half-tone at the terminator, hard shadow shapes, a colored shade, a shaped highlight, a stepped rim (cold moon and warm ember).
- **Line:** an ink line on everything that acts (her, foes, folk, props, buildings), weighted by distance, never on the ground's small detail.
- **Surfaces:** color blocks with the brush inside them.
- **Forms:** bevels and chunky shapes that catch the band.
- **The line in FX:** spells as hard graphic shapes.
- **Appeal:** her forms and the camera carry her sex appeal and swagger, which no shader adds.

**For a top-down action camera** (ours, unlike the references' third-person shooter), the line and the band carry the read when figures are 40-60 px tall: the silhouette line separates her and the pack from the ground, the band gives every form a lit side at a glance, and the ground must stay quiet (no line, soft bands, big value masses) so the figures read first.
