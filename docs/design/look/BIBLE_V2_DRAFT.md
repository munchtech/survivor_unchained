# The look bible v2: painted cel (DRAFT, L1 step A4; art director, look-l1, 4 Nov)

Rewritten around the style frames and boards once A2/A3 exist (`survivorsunchained_inputs\look_l1\a2\`). Rules 1-4 and the storybook (old 10) stand with their numbers; the rest becomes painted cel. These numbers are the starting point for B1 (the shading model), B5 (the line) and B2 (the material library). The frames and the lookdev stage tune them.

**The look in one line:** a dark-fantasy world drawn in ink and painted in blocks, lit by ember against moon.

| # | Rule | Numbers (start) |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | Value first; shadows keep color; two lights, one warm; vibrance, not saturation | as the bible today (palette.py) |
| 5 | **The band.** Two bands, lit and shade; the terminator hard with a narrow soft edge; a warm half-tone just past it on skin, cooler and narrower elsewhere; a little painted gradient inside the lit band so forms stay round. The key's cast shadows are hard shapes. The ground's band is soft, so terrain never stripes. | terminator at n·l 0, half-width 0.06 (about 7 degrees full); half-tone to n·l 0.2 (skin level (0.85, 0.45, 0.38), others 0.55); form 0.3; shadow step ±0.08 of the PCF; ground half-width 0.15; the band sees 35% of the normal map |
| 6 | **The highlight.** One shaped highlight per form, hard-edged, in the key's color. Matte things (cloth, stone, wood, ground) have none: their light is the band and the rim. | a disc of 6-12 degrees around N·H at 85% key color. Skin 6 degrees at 0.5; leather 9 at 0.6; metal two bands (20 degrees at 0.35 in the sky's color plus 7 at 1.0 in the key's); hair a hardened band per clump |
| 7 | **The rim.** A stepped fresnel band on every figure's lit side: moon-cold at night, pale gold by day, warm near her ember. The crowd's R4 edge is this rim. | width 0.25 (in 1-n·v), strength 0.35 |
| 8 | **The ink line.** On everything that acts: her, foes, folk, props, buildings. Weighted by distance. A deeper tone of the surface, never black. Off on light sources. Never on the ground's small detail. | Class: full (silhouette and 45-degree creases) for props, buildings and gear; silhouette only for her skin and hair, folk, the crowd and foliage; none for ground, grass, scatter, water, sky and FX. 2.5 px at the close-up, 1.5 px at the play camera (per 1080 lines); fades 30-60 m and in fog. Ink = surface × rig ink × 0.25 (night (0.42, 0.45, 0.85), day (0.85, 0.6, 0.5)) |
| 9 | **Color blocks.** Each block (a stone, a plank, a tile, a cloth panel, a hair clump) is one color, varied within its class's range. Light is painted in: a top-lit gradient, a lit bevel stroke on edges, a cool cavity. The brush stays inside each block at one world scale. Normal maps carry big forms only. | brush 6-10 cm (5-8 px at the play camera); no texture detail under about 3 cm; per-block value spread within ±0.06 Oklab L |
| 10 | **Shape language.** Chunky, bevelled, big-medium-small. Props and architecture slightly exaggerated (thicker beams, rounder stones, bigger bevels) so the band has something to catch. | (a judge; the remake list names what can't carry it) |
| 11 | **Her.** Her forms are P1's: semi-stylized, with appeal and a casual swagger carried by pose and camera. Her surfaces: skin with the warm half-tone and small hard highlights, hair clumps as blocks with one hard band, gear by its material class. | her skin hue h 15-90 kept by the grade; rim at most 3; copper hair rich |
| 12 | **The HUD and screens** in the same line and blocks: flat painted bronze, iron and parchment blocks, each with one highlight band and the ink outline. Text in cream (#e8dcc0); one gold line (#d9b56a). Marks are light on the ground. | Style.cs swatches (B6) |
| 13 | **Spells:** one family per school (`Palette.Of`), drawn as hard graphic shapes in two or three stepped bands with a soft outer glow. The only fully saturated pixels at night. | Palette.Of plus B6's band rules |
| 14 | **The storybook** is the same world drawn (unchanged). | as old rule 10 |
