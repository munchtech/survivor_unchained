# Diablo II, III and IV: what the classes lead took

Summary notes for `docs/design/CLASSES.md`. The D4 Codex survey is in `docs/SKILLS_RESEARCH.md`.

## Diablo II (2000; Resurrected 2021)

- **Three trees per class** (Amazon: Bow, Javelin, Passive), 30 skills, one point per level, skills to 20. **Synergies** (1.10): a skill gains from points in named other skills (Fire Bolt +16% per point in Fireball), so a build is a skill and its feeders.
- **Click:** each tree is a fantasy (Sorceress: fire, cold, lightning). **Plan:** points are scarce, synergies make a pure build deep.
- **Failure:** no respec at first (one per difficulty later); dead points in prerequisites; synergies made one skill per tree the answer.
- **Take:** a calling's types named by fantasy, and **"points here feed that"** as a visible rule.

## Diablo III (2012; Reaper of Souls)

- **No points.** Skills unlock by level; each has five **runes** that change it (Multishot rune: Fire at Will). Any combination, swapped freely.
- **Set items define the build** (six-piece sets: "Rend deals 4,000% more"). **Elective mode** lets any skill sit in any slot. **Akarat's Champion**, **Wrath of the Berserker**, **Archon**: the transformation "ultimates" on long cooldowns, and they are what players remember.
- **Click:** the best click-and-have-fun in the genre: no wrong choice, instant swap. **Plan:** shallow in skills; deep only through sets and legendary powers.
- **Failure:** sets with "+4,000%" made the build the set; no character to plan; numbers past meaning.
- **Take:** **free respec and instant experiment**; runes as our skill lamps; **transformation ultimates** (Archon, Berserker) are the most loved class moments: our "Stand" ultimates.

## Diablo IV (2023; Vessel of Hatred 2024; Lord of Hatred, 28 April 2026)

- **At launch:** one tree per class, skills to 5 points, then an enhancement and a choice of two upgrades; clusters gated by points spent; a **key passive** at the end; **Paragon boards** after 50 with glyphs; **Aspects** (Codex of Power) imprinted on gear.
- **Lord of Hatred** (patch of 28 April 2026, coverage from Xbox Wire, Icy Veins, DiabloBytes; details still disputed between sources): key passives removed; each skill has **three branches** (two general, one of build-defining "Bonus Skill Variants" that can convert element or behaviour), skills take up to 12 points, nodes unlock by level instead of points spent; level cap 70.
- **Click:** a clear class identity per tree and a cheap respec. **Plan:** Paragon pathing and glyph radius at the top end.
- **Failure:** **flat nodes** ("+x% damage to vulnerable") with no rule, so the tree was a spreadsheet; **trap choices** (nodes that read well and do nothing for the build); key passives forcing one or two finishers per class, which Blizzard itself named as the reason for the 2026 rework; Paragon so large that the tree stopped mattering.
- **Take:** Blizzard's own fix is our starting point: **skills that branch into variants** that change behaviour, not size; no flat filler; don't let an open-ended paragon bury the tree (our level cap 30 stands).
