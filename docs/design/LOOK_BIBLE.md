# The look bible: painted cel (L1, 4 November; art director, look-l1)

**The look in one line: a dark-fantasy world drawn in ink and painted in blocks, lit by ember against moon.** The owner picked painted cel on 4 November ("a clever, proper art style that we can just build on ... like this style is awesome"), with Marvel Rivals as the reference *kind*, never a copy; "art is a big deal, not a patch"; for her, "casual badassery and sexyness". Forms are semi-stylized: real anatomy and architecture, a little exaggerated and chunky so the light has edges to catch. Light falls in two hard bands, with a warm half-tone and a colored shade. Surfaces are color blocks with the brush inside them. An ink line, a deeper tone of the surface, draws everything that acts, and the ground stays quiet. The storybook is the same world drawn. This page replaces the 3 November bible; `docs/art/look_bible/` page 11's budgets still hold.

**The pictures are the law's other half.** The targets, one per game frame plus the key art and six boards, are listed in `survivorsunchained_inputs\look_l1\a2\targets.json` (sheets: `a2\r2\sheet_targets.jpg`, `sheet_boards.jpg`; the references read in `docs/design/look/REFERENCES.md`). When a target and a rule disagree, the rule wins and the target is redone. **The frame question for every restyle:** would this frame ship in AAA, is it one game with the other twelve, and is it our painted cel (the reference's kind, not its copy)? Shoot with `tools/style/frames.py`, the blocks on clean forms with `tools/style/lookdev.py` (B0), and measure with `palette.py`. A number is a self-check before the judge, never a substitute for one. **Scale:** targets 04-09 and 12 are drawn at up to twice the play camera's size; 10 is at play size. Every block is judged at the play zoom (she 40-60 px tall), where only the silhouette line, the band, the rim and the pool carry the read.

**Derivation (the owner, 4 Nov):** every block is built FROM (a) the game's need, (b) a named reference, or (c) something inherited and named. No block is fitted to today's maps or scenes: the frames are rebuilt to the blocks, never the blocks bent to the frames. Where a source can't carry the style, it is remade, not patched (the owner, 4 Nov evening: "if they need to be redone so be it").
