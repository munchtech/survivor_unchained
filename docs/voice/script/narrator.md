# The narrator: recording script

Voice `narrator`. Generated by `tools/voice/extract.py` from the game's content: do not edit by hand (direction goes in `tools/voice/directions.json`; casting in `tools/voice/voices.json`, rendered to `docs/voice/CASTING.md`).

**Who:** Tells the player what happens and what can be seen, in the present tense and the second person: captions in the world, the silences in conversations, the notice board, the chapter's last page.

**Resting manner:** plain, close, unhurried; pace unhurried. One image per line; lets the full stop do the work. Reads quoted speech without acting it.
