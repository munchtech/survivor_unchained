# 13. The tutorial's launch-ready checklist: the first 20-30 minutes, beat by beat

The owner (18 October): "make the first bit (tutorial) launch ready first as its the part to hook people". This page is the list a beat must clear before it is launch-ready. It points at the boards' DONE lines (pages 1a-1j), page 11 (budgets) and page 12 (inventory), and adds nothing to them. The order and the cinematics' timings come from `docs/cinematics/shoot/animatics/prologue.txt` (the 250-s animatic: about 238 s of cinematics and four 3-s play cards; the rest of the 20-30 minutes is play, whose lengths the combat and experience leads set, so this page gives none). This checklist ends at the town hub; the first map out is the next stretch of the game's look pass.

## Every beat, every frame

A beat is DONE when all of these hold at its cameras (the play and town camera; the close-up at its closest zoom; the Look camera; each cinematic's own shots), at 1:1, day and night where the beat has both:
1. **The whole-frame gate:** a fresh judge, watching as a player, answers yes to "would this frame ship in a AAA game as it stands?" for every frame shown: her anatomy and pose, face and hair, the light, the camera and the staging. A no fails the beat whatever else passes. Calibrated on the owner's three failing C01 frames (`survivorsunchained_inputs\owner_frames_18oct\`). A shot that can't pass is restaged or cut, never closed as known.
2. **The boards' DONE lines** listed for the beat below, each measured or judged as its board says.
3. **Page 11 (budgets):** every 20-s window of the beat in a Release perf run on the owner's 5080, whose log reaches stage=Exit, meets the per-frame targets (GPU at most 6 ms average and 10 ms p99 at 1440p high; at most 4.5 ms average and 7 ms p99 at 1080p medium, an estimate until one run is measured on a real 3060 / 4060-class card; frame p99 at most 16.7 ms; no hitch over 33 ms outside loads; render CPU, game logic and UI draws within their lines), and each new asset meets its row.
4. **Page 12 (inventory):** every row the beat shows is keep, or its rework or build has landed and its old asset is gone from the scene files; every `test` row has been decided.
5. **Tiers:** she is judged at full detail; the world and story NPCs pass if they look good in the shot and run well; fodder is cheap. A tier-2 or tier-3 asset is never failed for not matching her.

**Her, in every beat** (tier 1; owned outside this bible, listed so no beat closes without them): 1a on every piece she wears (worn metal, not plastic: the touch-up lead's metal standard, then the armor-and-weapons pipeline lead's slot parts at page 11's dressed budget); 1b on her skin (the touch-up lead); the touch-up list (the throat at the fire, the hairline, the braid's start, the Reaver cord); the next body build (the fingers, the painted-out anatomy); creation offers only braid, ponytail and pixie; the animation lead's open list (the run's arm swing, the grips, the crossbow hand, a hunter's idle for the Stalker instead of the crouch).

## Beyond the look (each area's own list; a beat isn't launch-ready without them)

These are owned by their areas and checked there, not by the look bible's judge: final voices for every line (ElevenLabs, no placeholders shipped), the music and the sound, the HUD and menus, the fight's feel and difficulty, the first-launch freezes (PERF_AUDIT item 5: ship the VAT bakes and item photos pre-made and warm the pipelines at the title), and saves.

Owners: the tutorial environment lead (beats 1, 3-12, the world; this page's State column), cinematics (beats 3, 5, 7, 9, 11), the story characters lead (the NPCs and fodder in 4-12, and the Ford-Warden, the Grimtunnel and the lamplings until a bosses lead starts), the armor-and-weapons pipeline lead and the touch-up lead (her, in every beat), rendering (the title and the perf runs).
