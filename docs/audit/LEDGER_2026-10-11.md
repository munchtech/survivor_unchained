**Done the same day (the owner said yes to all three):** the 20 branches in 1c are deleted locally and on GitHub, each kept as a tag `archive/<branch>` (the combat run-up numbers went into the combat handoff first). Read-only audit of integration (`claude/vigilant-galileo-l6jqyx` @ 24ac9a06). Nothing was changed, merged or deleted.
