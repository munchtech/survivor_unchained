# Editorial study of the story

A developmental editor's study of *Survivor Unchained*'s story, written
for the author. Nothing here changes the game: these are notes, and the
author decides what to take. Every judgement is backed by a craft
principle from the study, and most by a named example.

## Read in this order

1. **`EDITORIAL_LETTER.md`**: start here. The big picture: what the story is
   (and the controlling idea it already has), what works and why, the
   eight problems ranked by impact, how it holds up against the genre's
   best, how the day and night halves support the theme or don't, and a
   plan in order.
2. **`THE_EMBER_REVEAL.md`**: the large reveal the owner asked for, drafted:
   what everyone believes ember is, and the truth under all of it (ember
   is the dead), with the seeds already in the game, the reveal scene in
   the house voice, and what it does to the endings and the arenas.
3. **`REALNESS_AND_EXTREMES.md`**: the owner's second request. How human the
   cast is (too forgivable: every sin has a noble motive), a third layer
   of motive for each person, and the truly heinous turns available, each
   planted and rationed.
4. **`NOTES_BY_ACT.md`**: the detailed developmental notes. Prologue, Act 1
   thread by thread, Act 2 and Act 3 as planned in the bible: arcs, every
   twist rated (planted, fair, surprising, inevitable), pacing, stakes,
   choices, villains, the timeline and its open questions, and what to
   cut, deepen and set up.
5. **`IDEAS.md`**: thirty-six ideas to make it extraordinary, each marked
   with its cost from a data change to new content, with the top picks
   starred and a "five things first" list.
6. **`LINE_NOTES.md`**: line-level notes on the actual text (prologue,
   zones, every speaker in the dialogue, items, journal, morning reports,
   townsfolk, the board, barks), with alternatives in the established
   voice. Includes the single most important fix (what the lamps do) as
   note 1, and a checklist for the next writer.
7. **`CRAFT_STUDY.md`**: the study the rest is built on, organised by
   problem (voice, character, twists, structure, theme, worldbuilding,
   item lore, run-based story, barks, choice, dialogue, film, editing),
   with sources. Keep it open while writing. The other files cite it by
   section (§).

## If there is time for only one thing

Read `EDITORIAL_LETTER.md` §3 (the problems) and §7 (the plan), then do
the five items at the end of `IDEAS.md`.
