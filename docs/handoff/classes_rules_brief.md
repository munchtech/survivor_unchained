# Wiring the Lamps' phase-1 rules (brief shared by each wiring worker)

The Lamps (docs/design/CLASSES.md §2) light rules a fight reads. Source of truth: each lamp's `text` in
godot/data/content/lamps.json (generated by tools/classes/lamps_build.py + lamps_quarters.py; don't edit them)
and its effect's `args`. List yours with:
  python -c "import json;d=json.load(open('godot/data/content/lamps.json'));[print(l['id'],'|',l['kind'],'|',l['text'],'|',[e for e in l['effects'] if e.get('do')=='rule' and e.get('phase',1)==1]) for l in d['lamps'] if (l.get('calling') or 'crossing')=='<CALLING>' and any(e.get('do')=='rule' and e.get('phase',1)==1 for e in l['effects'])]"

How a fight reads them (godot/logic/Sim/LampLoadout.cs, `Battle.Lamps`, `Battle.Lamp(id)`):
- `Lamp("id")` is the rule lit. `Lamps.Size(id, arg)` is a SIZE (more/inc/add damage, area, duration
  bonus, a share mended): summed over the lamps lighting it and cut to a quarter by night.
- `Lamps.Full(id, arg)` is NOT a size: a price ("weaker", "less", "price", a blue lamp's drawback), a
  threshold ("below", "at", "range", "near", "far", "radius", "count" needed), a duration that defines the
  rule, or a wait. Kept in full by night.
- Counts (extra projectiles, pierce, chains, "add" on a count) are at most +1 by night in total: read them
  with `Lamps.Size` and then `Math.Min(1, ...)` when `Lamps.Night`, rounded down by day.
- Skill lamps and small lamps share ids across skills/tags: `Lamps.HasOn(id, skillId, tags)` and
  `Lamps.SizeOn(id, arg, skillId, tags)` pick the lamps on that skill or any of its tags. Skill ids are the
  weapon/skill ids in godot/logic/Content/Weapons.cs (a WeaponInst's `Def.Id`, tags `Def.Tags`).
- A rule whose skill or status doesn't exist in the game yet: leave it, list it in your report.

Where things live: godot/logic/Sim/Battle.cs (HitEnemy damage multipliers ~line 690-760, KillEnemy, ApplyStatus
statuses and durations, HurtPlayer defense ~1080, PerfectDodge, the block branch, Dash, UpdatePlayer, buffs
AddBuff), godot/logic/Sim/Weapons.cs (Firing: cooldowns, projectiles, pierce, reach per weapon), Arts.cs and
Forms.cs (arts, reflexes), Flare.cs (Flare rules: don't change the fill numbers, a solver tuned them).
Statuses: `StatusKind` (Bleed, Burn, Chill, Frozen, Shock, Poison, Mark, Bound, Stun, Fear...). Champions are
`e.Elite && !e.Boss`. "Still" = `Player.StillT > x`; "moving" = `Player.Moving`.
Each rule is a few lines at the right hook, with a one-line comment naming the lamp. Keep each rule's
string literal (the id in quotes) in the code: a test checks every phase-1 rule id appears in godot/logic/Sim.
