## Key decisions (why)

- **The bar:** a beat is not done until it would make a player stop,
  swallow or laugh out loud. "Well made" is not the bar.
- **Invert the restraint.** Hide the setups and land the payoffs. Today the
  setups confess and the payoffs whisper, which is why nothing twists and
  nothing hits.
- **Every act gets an unmissable devastation**, on the trunk and not a
  branch, in someone the player has loved on screen. Nell can't carry all
  three acts.
- **Maeca kills Holloway:** in front of the player, it is not optional, and
  the player must have loved Holloway first. The six tests are in the
  letter, §3.6.
- **Twist tests:** the false belief; five re-reads; fair but hidden; "it was
  obvious"; the cost; the player's own hands. They are in the letter, §3.7.
