## The owner's words (4 Nov)
On Marvel Rivals: "a clever, proper art style that we can just build on instead of having to make things from nothing all the time. like this style is awesome." The reference's own description: toon/cel shading (distinct shadow shapes, hard-edged color transitions, stylized highlights), comic-book line work, high-contrast lighting, vibrant color blocks, semi-stylized 3D forms with sex appeal. The 18+ tone and the heroine's appeal stand.

## The job: lock the direction as a style the whole game builds on
1. **Amend the bible, one page still:** the painted-masses palette and value rules stand (rules 1-4, 10); rules 5-9 and 11 become painted cel: a two- or three-band shading ramp with hard shadow shapes, a stylized specular, an ink line (depth and normal edges, weight by distance, never on the ground's noise), color blocks on surfaces (texture detail painted out further than today), the HUD and screens in the same line and blocks. Numbers for the ramp (band count, terminator softness in degrees), the line (px at the play camera, 1-2; at the close-up 2-3), and the spec (size, color from the key light).
2. **Build it as shaders and a post pass, not remakes:** a cel ramp in the lit materials (her skin, hair, cloth, leather, metal, the VAT crowd, the world's standard materials: one `gdshaderinc` all of them include), an edge line post pass (depth + normal), the grade kept. Her forms untouched; every asset's maps as they are (the paint presets flatten further in the next restyle rounds).
3. **The thirteen frames** through `tools/style/frames.py shoot` before and after; `palette.py`; `frames.py compare` to an A|B sheet at half size for the owner, plus three 1:1 crops (her at the close-up, the arena night, the Waystation square).
4. **One max judge** on the whole-frame question: "would this ship in AAA, is it one game across the thirteen, and is it the reference's kind of style without being its copy?" Round cap 2. Frame time: ramp + line within 0.6 ms GPU at the play camera.
5. The owner picks on the sheet (relay through the coordinator). On a pick the amended bible is the law for every restyle (R2b, R5-R12) and for P1's bake targets.

## REFRAMED (the owner, 4 Nov, later): "I want art to be...a big deal. not a patch. we are moving in a new direction. cost does not matter - implementation building blocks etc matter."
The shader-and-post-pass is the floor, not the ceiling. L1 is the art direction built as a system: (1) key art, style frames, material and lighting boards made for this game, the bible rewritten around them; (2) reusable blocks: one cel shading model (a shaderinc every lit material includes), a material library as data, a texture authoring pipeline that produces painted-block maps in the style, lighting rigs per time of day, the line/post stack, VFX and UI language as data; (3) the thirteen frames rebuilt through the blocks, with "remake" routed wherever a source can't carry the style; (4) one max judge, the owner's A|B. Budget is not the limit.
