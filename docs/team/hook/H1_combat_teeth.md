## The fixed list (each item ends in "the app can now X" and a tool or data file the next scar reuses)
1. **Danger at tier 1, for a human.** Today the autopilot never fell under 93% life in a 31-minute tier-1 night. Build a "late human" bot in the harness (`godot/balance`): reaction 350-500 ms, dodges ~60% of telegraphs, stands in things. Targets at tier 1: fodder dies to 2-4 auto-attacks at ember 0-10, elites 8-12; the late human ends the night at 30-60% life and falls about 1 night in 4 careless; the deft bot still wins ~90%. Then re-run the acceptance table: the four callings stay within x1.25 (the standing gate); the sim must stay byte-identical where you did not touch it.
2. **Foe health.** A thin bar under a foe once it is below 70% (elites and bosses always), fading when full, drawn in the HUD's style (`godot/src/Ui/Style.cs`, `GameHud.cs` has the boss bar). 320 foes on screen: only damaged foes draw, batched; measure the cost at night/250. Functional and clean; its look goes to H2's bible.
3. **Telegraphs that must be dashed: a kit as data.** `godot/data/content/telegraphs.json` (or next to the arena data): an expanding ring (slows 40% in the band, roots 0.6 s at the center, then the blow), a line charge, a pull, each with a ground tell (decal + timing) and an event (`Ev.Telegraph` with kind, position, phase) so H3's audio lead can give each a sound (coordinate with them by SendMessage; their id comes from main). Past minute 3 every wave shape carries one. The Flare is designed as the save of a named wave shape (the horde closing as a ring: the Flare clears it), so the press is needed, not optional. A tier-1 night with the kit must still meet item 1's targets.
4. **Hover = priority target.** `godot/src/Game/Controls.cs`: the foe under the mouse gets a mark (a ring in the HUD style); her auto-attacks and aimed skills prefer it within range; on a pad, the nearest foe in the right stick's direction. A unit test on the pick.
5. **Bosses distinct.** `godot/logic/Play/Bosses/ArenaBosses.cs` and the VFX presets: each arena boss gets one silhouette read (scale, stance), one color, one signature attack that must be dodged (from the kit in 3), and one tell before it. No new models (creature art is H2's and later): distinguish by scale, tint, light, kit and tell. The Pack-Mother first (the demo's boss).
6. **The arena as a board.** The Deep Shaw: walkable size down to what its crowd fills (an open field reads as "too big"); lanes, two or three chokepoints, a lamp to hold (a reason to stand somewhere), from the arena kit (`docs/team/arena_art.md`, the presets). Write the board as data so the other scars take it. Position must be a decision at minute 5.
7. **Night length.** Tier-1 table nights at the story-night shape: about 12 min, the boss near minute 10 (`STORY_NIGHTS_AND_TIME.md` "The shape"); 30 min from tier 2. `ArenaRun.cs` (`Target()`, the clock, `Arenas.XpFor` scales to 30 today: pay for the fight's own length).
