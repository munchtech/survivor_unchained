## The rule (the coordinator's, from the plan)
**Every 45-60 s of a tier-1 night something must be dodged or she dies, and the crowd comes from more than one side.** A standing build stays possible, earned by gear and Lamps, never as the default. H1's danger was a bot that reacts late; yours is a bot that does nothing.

## The list, in order
1. **The standing bot** in the harness (`godot/balance`, `arena --bot ...`: add `standing`, never moves, never dodges, auto-attacks and drinks; and `kiter`, moves away from the nearest pack at all times, never stands). It is your measure before any change: 32 seeds x 4 callings at tier 1 on the Pack's board. Report its win rate first (we expect it to win most nights today).
2. **Marks that kill** (`data/content/telegraphs.json`, `teeth.json`, `Tells.cs`): a tier-1 ring stood through costs 60-80% of life (not 0.46 + a root); the closing is a wall (inside it after the windup is the fall over 3 s unless she leaves by the gap); the charge knocks down; the pull is followed by a blow. Numbers at tiers 2-4 scale, never softer. Every mark's windup stays readable (H1's 0.7 s tell, the 1.5 s spacing near her); the point is the cost of ignoring it, not surprise.
3. **A surround director** (the pacing/charge director and `boards.json`): spawn lanes from two or three sides, never one; a flank pack every 60-90 s that comes in behind her facing; foes that cut her off (a pack's leader paths to a point ahead of her, not to her); packs that close when she stands (a stand of 3 s inside a pack's reach triggers its lunge). Measure "surrounded": foes within 4 m in three or more of six sectors, for 2 s+, at least twice a night for the late human.
4. **Kiting pays** (a foe kind she must pull: a hitter too slow to catch a mover and lethal to a stander; a pack that stops to feed on a corpse pool for 2 s when she breaks away, so running has a payoff). Tier 1 teaches it once with a callout (`Callout`, the day stretch's pattern).
5. **The lamp is a decision** (N34): a snuffer is explained once in play (one bark on the first snuffer, one callout); the lamp's pay (the Flare fill x1.3, kills x1.1) is a reason to leave and come back, not to sit (a holder who never leaves the pool for 60 s draws the flank pack). Foes stuck on the fold's cover: a nav fix or a leash (a foe stuck 6 s is recalled and re-sent from a lane); test it with H4's `A_pack_left_standing_far_off...` pattern.
6. **Elite bars** (N19): find why no bar showed (the `bars` flag into `FoeMarks.Show` in `GameHud.Foes.cs`, or the Hollow's champions not `Elite` in `Enemies.cs`); fix; one frame at the play camera that shows a champion's bar.
7. **The turn** (N27): measure her facing lag behind the stick, the run blend on a 90 and 180 degree turn, and the camera yaw against Hades and Diablo IV (read: facing snaps within ~100 ms, the body follows over 150-250 ms with a lean, the camera never yaws with the stick in a top-down). Fix what is off; a strip before/after of a figure-eight run, judged with the clip.
8. **Dash charges under her** (N30): a `su-worker` adds charge pips to her bar below her (the bar UI's data, `GameHud.cs` has the counts), in the HUD's bronze language; no new style.
9. **The Ford-Warden's numbers for H8** (N20): a teeth entry for the prologue (he lives until the lamp beat; a human can't bring him down in under 60 s; the crash is the way through). Agree it with H8 (seams-h8) by SendMessage; H8 wires it in `Prologue.cs`; you own the numbers.
10. **Gate:** the standing bot falls in 80%+ of tier-1 nights; the late human still wins about 80% (lowest life median ~0.3); the deft 95%+; the kiter wins more than the standing bot; the four callings within x1.25 (H1's off-calling gate). Then one `su-lead-max` judge (`effort: "max"`) on a tier-1 clip from 0:00 with the human bot at the play camera: the whole-frame question first, then "would a player have to move, and could they tell why?"; round cap 2.
