## Why this is a rebuild, not a retarget
Her clips are sixty-two passes of script-built keys and fixes layered on a bought library (UAL) played raw, on a 100-joint kit skeleton with no twist chains, no spine curve to speak of, no breast or glute bones. The elbow alone took ~3.3M tokens of patches and closed as known. P1's new skeleton (124 bones: five spine joints, two neck joints, scapula and pectoral helpers, forearm and thigh twist chains, breast two-link chains, glutes, correctives at every big joint) can carry weight, counter-rotation and follow-through the old one could not. A global-match retarget keeps every old fault on a better rig. So: the core set is re-authored to the bar on the new rig, through one pipeline; retarget serves only the long tail (rare clips, cinematic one-offs) and anything the judge passes as is.
