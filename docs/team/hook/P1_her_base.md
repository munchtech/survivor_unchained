## The job
1. **The base:** one quad game mesh with deformation loops at every joint (the MakeHuman CC0 base topology, or ours derived from it), one rig (the base-fit rig the clips already target, plus twist bones and corrective shapes at the elbow, shoulder, knee, hip), a slider catalogue as blend targets (ours: height, build, bust with the 0.35 taste and small areolas, hips, heavy = thick-and-curvy, male build for NPCs), LOD tiers (close ~60-100k, play ~25k, far ~8k), one build command (`tools/body/build.py <spec.json>` to a glb) and one check command (bend strips at 90 and 135 degrees measured against reference outlines, silhouette diff, triangle budget, weight sanity).
2. **Her, the first body out of it:** her approved forms (the current sculpt) become targets on the base by wrap; her face wrapped and its maps baked so the Look is unchanged; her maps baked to the new UVs; the painted-cel direction (L1, running in look-l1) is the shading, not your concern beyond clean normals and a bake that reads under it.
3. **The gate (23 Oct rule):** the owner sees the BARE shape first: A (shipped) | B (new) at the Look, the close-up and the play camera, at the owner's angle, half size, through the coordinator. The rig's bend strips go with it. Nothing downstream (P2 gear re-fit, hair attach, animation re-shoot) starts before the owner's pick.
4. After the pick, the next leads: P2 (the 21 set pieces re-fit on the base, tuck maps regenerated by tool), hair cards re-attached, animation re-shot at the game cameras, her painted pass (R10). Write their briefs' facts in your handoff.
