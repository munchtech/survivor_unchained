## The decision (locked; design inside it, never around it)
1. **One character, one build, one combat everywhere.** The night is a shape (an arena or map run: her build at full power from minute 0, the horde, the escalation, 12 minutes, a boss); the day is the world (town, story, gear, the Lamps, the table, the permanent zone with the same combat at story density). Time of day is mood and pacing, never a rule switch. CLASSES 2.10's quarter sizes go.
2. **The cards are the talent tree.** Every skill's Wick (three strands, ~12 lamps, each strand ending in a Turn) is made of real modifiers: an element swap (Ember Volley > Rime: chills then shatters), a shape (split, pierce, linger, hail on a spot), a trigger (on kill, on dash, on a bleed), a cost and pay-off. No lamp is a bare percent. At night each level-up offers three unlit lamps from the skills she carries, lit for the night only and free. Lamps lit for good in town are on from minute 0, in the day's fights too, and take no card slot, so a veteran's draft reaches second Turns and Unions and a newcomer's the basics: one rule that scales.
3. **Try-before-you-buy.** The dawn card remembers what she lit and liked ("You lit Rime three nights running. Light it for good at the lamp-post, 2 points"). Dawn takes only the night's heat (night-lit lamps and ranks); a loss costs the night's loot and time, never her build.
4. **Cut:** the day skill book (tomes, "every third level teaches", attribute needs, the 1/2/3 day slots); the random pool of 26-50 cards; "power from nothing, gone at dawn". **Keep:** the pick every level-up, evolutions as Turns, Unions between carried pairs, the Flare, Lamps and Wicks, the table's tier pick, endless (honing past rank 8 into a second Turn).
