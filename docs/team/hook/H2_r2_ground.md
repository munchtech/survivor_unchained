## The workflow
1. Merge `origin/claude/vigilant-galileo-l6jqyx` into `look-r2` first (the junction rule: `cmd /c rmdir godot\assets` before the merge, remake it to your OWN `public\assets` after, `git update-index --assume-unchanged godot/assets`). Run `--import` once (~2 min; the cache is seeded).
2. Build the preset on the arena ground first (frame 09 is the core loop and the worst): `paint_tex.py` trials on its maps, a single-frame shoot (`frames.py shoot --only 09`), `palette.py`, look once at half size. Then the terrain and the camp. paint_tex runs on the CPU (no GPU turn); Godot shots take `python tools/turn.py take godot "look-r2: <what>"` (four at a time; H4 and R4 share the machine), given back at once.
3. Re-shoot the whole set: `python tools/style/frames.py shoot --out C:\Users\munch\Desktop\survivorsunchained_inputs\look_h2\r2 --root <your worktree>` (about 12 minutes, one godot turn). All thirteen, so the judge sees the whole game.
4. `palette.py ...\look_h2\r2` and `frames.py compare ...\look_h2\r1_2 ...\look_h2\r2 ...\look_h2\r2\ab`: the before/after sheet against the shipped R1.
5. One `su-lead-max` judge (subagent, foreground, `effort: "max"`: the ground is under every play frame; `Item: R2 ground painted`, `Context budget: 150k`). Its brief opens with the whole-frame question for each changed frame: would this frame ship in a AAA game as it stands, and **is this the same game as the storybook plate (frame 02)** and the other eleven? Then rules 5 and 6: one brush, no gravel noise, masses that model the ground under the moon or sun. Give it the ab sheet, the 1:1 halves of 01, 04, 05, 09, 10, 12, the palette.md numbers before and after, the bible, and the finish-line lines (judge at 1:1 game-camera frames only; what shows only enlarged is closed). Round cap 2: a second round only on the judge's must-fixes; then it ships or closes as known with the judge's distance-to-bar in your handoff.
6. Commit as you go (painted maps are art: commit them; keep the raw maps so the preset can be re-run); the full suite green before the MR (`dotnet test godot/tests/Tests.csproj`, the Passed!/Failed! line); push `look-r2`; update your handoff's top section (the numbers before and after, the judge's verdict, the sheet paths, what R7/R8 should pick up); then your handoff top gets the line `MR READY look-r2@<commit>` and you SendMessage the coordinator the same line with the r2 and ab sheet paths, the per-frame numbers, the judge's verdict in two lines, and your cost.

First report within your first few turns: your list (preset, arena ground, terrain, camp, grass, the shoot, the judge) with each item's state and an honest estimate. Scratch in a `look_r2` subfolder of the shared scratchpad; pictures at half size only, and only when a decision needs your eye.
