# Sol concept sheets: her full kit and the Ford-Warden (10 Nov, coordinator #7)

**What this is for.** Concept sheets from ChatGPT (Sol) cost no Meshy credits and are the gate before any 3D work: P2's successor builds the kit from sheets 1-3, P3 remakes the Ford-Warden from sheets 4-6. A judge (and the owner, for her kit) passes a sheet before it feeds a build.

**Rules.** One driver at a time (`survivorsunchained_inputs\chatgpt.lock`). Never OpenAI through Meshy. Save every output at full size to `survivorsunchained_inputs\gpt_sol\<kit|warden>\` and log one provenance line per image (date, model, prompt number, references attached).

**How to run it.** One chat per batch. Attach the references, paste the STYLE block first, then one prompt per message. Ask for 2-3 variations of a sheet only if the first misses.

**References to attach**
- Kit (prompts 1-3): `survivorsunchained_inputs\look_l1\owner_ref_10nov\owner_ref_shield_braid.webp` (the owner's kit) and `survivorsunchained_inputs\look_l1\a2\r2\sheet_boards.jpg` (the picked look boards).
- Ford-Warden (prompts 4-6): `sheet_boards.jpg` only.

## STYLE (first message of each chat)

```text
You are drawing concept sheets for a dark-fantasy action RPG. Hold this style for every image in this chat:

Painted cel. Semi-stylized real anatomy, slightly chunky and bevelled shapes. Every surface is a flat color block with visible brushwork inside it. Light falls in two hard bands, lit and shade, with a narrow soft edge between them; the shade is a colored violet-blue, never gray or black. One hard-edged, shaped highlight per form; metal is painted in broad bands. A weighted ink outline in a deeper tone of each surface, never pure black, and no lines inside skin. Skin is clean: no freckles, pores or blotches. Surface colors are natural and unsaturated; strong color lives only in light sources.

These are production model sheets: plain flat mid-grey background, even front light from the upper left, no scenery, no text, no labels, no logos, no watermark. The design is original: do not copy any existing game's or film's characters. Use the attached boards for the paint style only.
```

## Batch 1: her full kit (gunmetal and gold)

Her, in every kit prompt: an adult woman warrior in her late twenties, athletic and strong with full curves, standing at ease with quiet confidence. Copper-red hair in a braided crown set with small gold rings and one long thick braid down her back.

### 1. Turnaround

```text
A character turnaround of one woman warrior, four full-body views in a row at the same scale with feet on one baseline: front, three-quarter front, side, back. Relaxed A-pose, arms 30-45 degrees from the body, hands open, no weapon and no shield. Wide 3:2 canvas.

Her: an adult woman in her late twenties, athletic and strong with full curves, calm confident face. Copper-red hair in a braided crown set with small gold rings and one long thick braid down her back.

Her armor, taken from the attached reference image and made consistent in all four views: gunmetal steel plate with gold edge trim and fine gold knotwork etching, over dark brown leather. A plunging armored bodice with full plate cups and plate panels down both sides of the ribs. Layered pauldrons on both shoulders. Vambraces on both forearms with leather-wrapped hands. A wide studded leather belt carrying hip plates and an armored brief, thighs bare. Knee-high plated boots with laced leather shins and gold toe caps.

Same face, body, hair and armor in every view. Show how each piece straps on at the side and back.
```

### 2. Pieces, off the body

```text
A parts sheet for the same armor set, each piece drawn alone and off the body, front and back views side by side, all at one scale in a tidy grid:
1. An open-faced helm that covers all of the hair, gunmetal with a gold brow band.
2. The pair of layered pauldrons with their straps.
3. The plunging bodice with its plate cups and side panels, and its back closure.
4. The pair of vambraces with the leather hand wraps.
5. The studded belt with its hip plates and the armored brief.
6. The pair of knee-high plated boots.
Same gunmetal, gold trim, knotwork etching and dark brown leather as the turnaround. Wide 3:2 canvas.
```

### 3. Three tiers by adornment

```text
The same woman and the same armor set shown three times, front view, side by side at one scale, as three quality tiers. The silhouette and the shape of every piece are identical in all three; only material, trim and ornament change.
Left, tier 1: plain dark iron and worn brown leather, no trim, hammer marks, simple rivets.
Middle, tier 2: clean gunmetal with brass edge trim and rivets, tooled leather.
Right, tier 3: polished gunmetal with gold trim, gold knotwork etching on every plate, and a few small ember-orange enamel inlays.
Wide 3:2 canvas.
```

## Batch 2: the Ford-Warden (the first boss, held in close-up)

### 4. Turnaround

```text
A character turnaround of a boss, three full-body views in a row at the same scale with feet on one baseline: front, side, back. Beside the front view, a plain grey silhouette of an ordinary man for scale: the boss stands 2.6 times his height, with a man's proportions, not a monster's.

He is a drowned watchman risen from a river: a hooded giant in the soaked kit of an old town Watch, a long heavy cloak and hood over rusted mail and a leather jerkin, all dark with water and hung with river weed. A long beard heavy with river water and weed spills from the hood. His face is lost in the hood's shadow except for two small points of cold blue light for eyes. In his right hand a long greatsword, point down. In his left fist a square cage-lamp of new black iron holding a cold pale blue flame. He stands like a very tired man made enormous: stooped shoulders, heavy arms.

The hood and cloak are dark, never white or pale. Tall 2:3 canvas if the views fit, otherwise wide.
```

### 5. The close-up under the hood

```text
A chest-up portrait of the same drowned hooded giant at night, seen from slightly below. He lifts his square black-iron cage-lamp near his face, and its cold pale blue flame lights him from below: the wet beard tangled with river weed, the edge of the hood, water running off it. Deep inside the hood only two points of cold blue light for eyes; no other face detail. Behind him, dark river and mist in moon-indigo. This one image may have a simple dark background instead of grey.
```

### 6. His lamp and his sword

```text
A prop sheet, two props, each alone on flat mid-grey at one scale.
1. A square cage-lamp of new black iron with a ring handle on top and a small hinged door: front, side and three-quarter views, once unlit and once holding a cold pale blue flame.
2. A long two-handed greatsword of old town-Watch steel, plain crossguard, leather-wrapped grip, worn and water-stained: full length, both faces.
Wide 3:2 canvas.
```
