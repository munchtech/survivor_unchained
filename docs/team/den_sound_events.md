## What plays when

| moment | event | how |
|---|---|---|
| step into the den | `bed.den` | the zone bed, through the existing zone path (the den is zone id `den`) |
| near a pit | `den.pits` | one loop at the nearest pit's rim, falling off with distance (full at the rim, gone 22 m beyond it, squared), panned toward the pit |
| quiet in the den | `den.pack_far` | the pack at a distance: once about 6 s into a quiet stretch, then at most every 45 s (a little random), panned off-centre; a fight (a hostile within 20 m, a blow in the last 10 s, the Pack-Mother up) fades it out and holds it back |
| den entrance on | `music.den.explore` | recorded loop, 2.5 s rise; the synth score is silenced in the den |
| the Pack-Mother arrives | `music.den.boss` | crossfade from explore (2.5 s in, explore out over 2.5 s); the synth boss mood is silenced |
| she dies | `music.den.falls` | the sting, near-instant in; then quiet (no music) once it ends |
| any foe: notice, bite, lunge, hurt, death | `voice.<wolf|wolfb|boar|yearling>.<moment>` | through the voice limiter (below), positioned at the foe |
| the Pack-Mother's moves | `voice.packmother.<arrival|drive|hamstring|moonhowl|phase|call|death>` | sim `Ev.Sound`, never limited |
| her effort | `her.grunt` | Sunder nearly always (0.9 chance, 0.7 s apart), a heavy swing about every other time (0.55, 1.6 s apart), a light cut seldom (0.06, 3 s apart); a Slash counts as a light cut |
| her leap and landing | `her.leap`, `her.land` | every one |
| her dash | `her.dodge` | 0.6 s apart, 70% of dashes |
| a perfect dodge | `dodge.perfect` | every one |
| a stance swap | `her.stance` | into a stance full, back out at 0.8 gain and 0.88 pitch |
| low health | `heartbeat` | the existing heartbeat pulse, now four recorded single-beat takes (lub, dub, ...) with urgency as gain |
| a big hit | duck | the beds (the recorded score and the ambience) duck to 0.6 for 0.4 s: a foe hit for at least half its health (foes of 60+ HP), an elite or boss kill, her taking a fifth of her health, her landing from a leap; 1.5 s apart |
| a draft or unlock card | duck | the existing draft duck, also applied to the recorded music beds |

## Voice numbers (picked from a 5-wolf fight, `DenSoundTests.PackFight`)

- At most **4 creature voices at once** (`MaxVoices`); a death is always let through (a kill is the thing the player wants to hear).
- Hearing range 30 m.
- Notice gap 0.45 s between any two notices: when a pack of five wakes together, 4 of the 5 notices are held (one bark, not a choir).
- Per kind, per moment cooldowns: notice 1.6 s, bite 0.55 s, lunge 0.9 s, hurt 0.3 s, death 0.18 s.
- Held in the five-wolf fight: wolf hurt about 60%, wolf death about 41%, wolf notice about 73% (in the bot's long runs). Every held voice is a hit the player still sees and hears as the normal impact.
- The unlimited ones: all `voice.packmother.*`.

## Decisions

1. **The arrival howl leads, `stinger.boss` is skipped in the den.** The howl is levelled to the stinger's LUFS, so it is not softer than what it replaces; played at gain 0.8 on the sfx bus it measures about -19.3 against the stinger's about -20.4. Two huge sounds at once would only fight each other and the boss track. Outside the den the stinger is as before.
2. **The pack at a distance is not strictly "before the first fight".** In every bot run the first pack wakes 23-29 m from the mouth and is on her in 4-8 s, so a "before the first blow" window is only a few seconds. The rule counts the quiet stretches instead: it starts 6 s into quiet, repeats at most every 45 s, and a fight silences it. The first listen item says what it should be if the owner wants it strict (a one-line change: `PackFarRule`).
3. **The common wolf's bite borrows the blight wolf's, pitched 0.84-0.92** (no wolf bite was supplied).
4. **Recorded music is read lazily** (a 120 s stereo take is about 63 MB of floats) and starts silent when she steps into the den, so nothing is read at the boss's arrival.
5. **Music beds sit on the music bus** (`Synth.Bed.Bus`), so the draft duck and the big-hit duck apply to them; the pits and the far pack sit on the ambience bus.
6. **No clipping on the master by construction**: the master is a compressor (-16 dB, 4:1, +5 dB makeup) into a limiter with a ceiling of 0.891 (-1 dBFS) that is instant. The offline replica of it, run over each bot run's boss fight with the loudest takes at full strength, no pitch and no lead ducking (the worst case), gives 0 clipped samples; the pre-limiter peaks are +4 to +6.4 dBFS and the worst gain reduction is 7 to 8.6 dB; after the master about -1.9 dBFS (table in `den_sound_run.md`).

## The owner's listen list: one den run, in order

1. **At the mouth, quiet.** The den air under everything. Is it too loud or too thin? About 6 s into standing still, the pack far off (it should read as far, off to one side). Walk toward a pit: the pits loop rises as you near it and pans toward it; walk away: it is gone by about 22 m. The exploration track, from the entrance.
2. **The first wolves.** Notice barks as the pack wakes (a few, never a wall). Their bite, hurt and death. Is the pitched bite (the blight wolf's, borrowed) believable on the common wolf? Is the hurt too busy (it is held about 60%)?
3. **The boar.** Notice, bite, lunge, death. (No hurt was supplied; it hits silently.)
4. **Her, in the fight.** Her grunt: is it too frequent or too rare (the three chances in `DenSound.GruntRule`)? Leap, then land (the land also ducks the music). Dash. A perfect dodge. A stance swap in and out.
5. **Low health.** The heartbeat: single beats, urgency in the gain. Does it read as a heart, not a tick?
6. **A big hit.** The music and ambience dip and come back: not an annoying pump?
7. **A draft or unlock card.** The music ducks under it.
8. **The Pack-Mother arrives.** The howl over the crossfade from the exploration track to the boss track: no pile-up, the stinger absent. Then her drive (the charge), hamstring (the cone), moonhowl (the channel), the phase change, the call. Does the voice sit with the fight, not on top of it?
9. **Her death.** The sting, then quiet: does the quiet after read as a ending, or as a dropout? How long should it stay quiet before the world's own music returns?
10. **Leaving.** Out of the den: the beds fade, the synth score returns.

## Not supplied (so these are silent, borrowed, or still the synth)

- Common wolf **bite**: borrowed from the blight wolf's, pitched down.
- **Boar hurt**.
- The Pack-Mother's **notice, bite, hurt, lunge, shake**: only the seven she has (arrival, drive, hamstring, moonhowl, phase, call, death).
- **Her hurt and death cries**: not supplied (her hurt is still the existing impact).
- The common wolf has no **lunge** in the hollow (the sim gives it a bite only), so `voice.wolf.lunge` is banked but did not fire in any run.
- Voices for the blight wolf (`wolfb`) and the yearling (`yearling`) are banked and tested but neither spawns in the hollow: they sound only in other dungeons.
- Everything else in the den (skills, loot, UI, footsteps) is the synth and the older takes, as before; nothing in the demo list was left unspoken apart from the above.
- `duck.bighit` is a duck, not a take.
