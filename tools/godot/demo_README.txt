SURVIVOR UNCHAINED: THE DEMO
============================

For adults only (18+): violence, gore and mature themes.

What it is
----------
A short slice of the game: wake in Lowford and learn to fight on the road (the
tutorial), reach the Waystation (the town), then walk east into Thornhollow Verge
and go down into the Den under Wolf Hollow. The den is built fresh every time you
enter it, so clear it again and again: its ruler can drop pieces of a new
armor set for the Warden, and it takes a few clears to gather them all.

How to start
------------
1. Unzip the whole folder anywhere (the Desktop is fine). Keep every file together.
2. Double-click SurvivorUnchained.exe.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game is not signed yet.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. The first launch takes longer while your graphics driver prepares the game's
   shaders, and the first minute of play may stutter now and then for the same
   reason. It is smoother after that.
5. At the title: New Journey, make your Warden, and play.

Controls (mouse and keyboard; a controller works too)
--------
Move ........................ W A S D (she faces the mouse)
The four skills ............. Left mouse, Right mouse, 1, 2
Dash ........................ Space (or Shift)
Flare (once it is lit) ...... Q
Draught (heal) .............. R
Interact / talk / enter ..... E
Pack ........................ I or Tab
Lamps (the skill tree) ...... L
Map ......................... M
Pause / menu ................ Esc
Report a bug ................ F8
Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls.

Reporting a bug
---------------
When something goes wrong, press F8 straight away (or Esc > Report a bug).
The game takes a screenshot and keeps the last 30 seconds of play from before
you pressed it. Type a short title, add what happened if you like, and press Send.
The first time, it asks your name once.

Each report is one folder named like  YourName_BR0001_20261119-2130_hollow
inside the "BugReports" folder next to SurvivorUnchained.exe (Esc > Open reports
folder opens it). If that folder cannot be written, the reports go to
%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Survivor Unchained\BugReports instead (the same
menu entry opens the right one).

To send them: right-click the BugReports folder > Send to > Compressed (zipped)
folder, and send that zip to the person who gave you the demo.

The 30-second recording can be turned off in Esc > Settings.

What your PC needs
------------------
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
A graphics card with Vulkan support and up-to-date drivers. The game is made and
tested on an NVIDIA RTX 5080; a recent card with 6 GB of video memory or more
should run it, but lower cards are untested.
About 8 GB of RAM and 6 GB of free disk space.
