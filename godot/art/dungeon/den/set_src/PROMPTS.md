Purpose: game-art modules for the den dungeon, placed in the game as camera-facing painted cards (the play camera never turns: it looks north, 56 degrees down, like Diablo / Path of Exile). The owner allows the ChatGPT imaging route (his account). Provenance per image in `PROVENANCE.txt` beside the images.

- Save the result at FULL SIZE as PNG (blob download, not a screenshot) to `C:\Users\munch\Desktop\survivorsunchained_inputs\gpt_den\<id>.png` (e.g. `B1.png`). If an image clearly misses the brief (wrong view, background not flat magenta, objects touching the edges when they must not, text in it), you may ask ONCE more in the same chat ("Again, but ...": say what to fix), save it as `<id>_2.png`, and note both.
- Provenance: one line per saved image in `PROVENANCE.txt`: date, model shown by ChatGPT, item id, file, references attached, the exact prompt (or "as PROMPTS.md <id>" plus any follow-up text verbatim).

## STYLE (first paragraph of every message)
Use the two attached concept paintings as the style and material reference: the same painted cel look, the same palette, the same brushwork and the same ink outlines (in a deeper tone of each surface, never pure black). Light: warm pale light falling from the upper left; shade is a cool grey-violet, never brown-black; natural unsaturated colours. This is a game art asset for a fixed overhead action-RPG camera that looks down at 56 degrees from the horizontal (like Diablo or Path of Exile): the top of the image is farther away, and we see the tops of things more than their sides. Paint ONLY the asset, isolated on a perfectly flat, solid magenta (#FF00FF) background: no ground plane, no floor, no cast shadow on the background, no text, no labels, no border, no vignette. A grown man would be 1.8 m tall; keep everything at the true scale given.
